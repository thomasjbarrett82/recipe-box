# African Peanut Stew

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Stew, Slow cooker, Vegetarian, Vegan, High fiber, Favorite
- Source: Tom
- Image: /images/african-peanut-stew.jpg
- Serves: 8

## Description

Sweet potatoes and chickpeas slow-cooked in a blended tomato, peanut butter and spice sauce, with kale stirred in at the end.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Slow cook: 6–8 h
Total: 6 h 20 min

## Ingredients

### Sauce

- 1 (795 g / 28 oz) can diced tomatoes
- 240 ml (1 cup) fresh cilantro leaves
- 1 small onion, peeled
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) chunky peanut butter OR non-peanut alternative
- 3 cloves garlic
- 5 ml (1 tsp) cumin
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) kosher salt
- 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) cinnamon
- 5 ml (1 tsp) ginger
- 5 ml (1 tsp) smoked paprika

### Stew

- 6 medium sweet potatoes, peeled and cubed (1.4 kg / 3 lb)
- 1 (425 g / 15 oz) can garbanzo beans, rinsed and drained
- 240 ml (1 cup) chicken or vegetable stock
- 1.9 L (8 cups) kale, chopped

## Method

1. Place sauce ingredients in food processor
2. Blend until just slightly chunky
3. Transfer to slow cooker
4. Stir in potatoes, beans, stock
5. Cook on LOW for 6-8 hours until potatoes are tender
6. Stir in kale 30-45 minutes before done
