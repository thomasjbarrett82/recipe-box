# Almond Bark Crunch

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert, Holiday
- Source: Tara
- Serves: 16

## Description

A no-bake party snack of Golden Grahams, nuts and marshmallows coated in melted white almond bark.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Melt: 5 min
Set: 30 min
Total: 45 min

## Ingredients

- 455 g (1 lb) white almond bark
- 240 ml (1 cup) salted mixed nuts or peanuts
- 240 ml (1 cup) miniature marshmallows
- 960 ml (4 cups) Golden Grahams cereal

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat up a double boiler over stove

### Directions

1. Melt bark in boiler
2. Add remaining ingredients and mix together
3. Drop onto waxed paper or greased cookie sheet and let cool to harden
