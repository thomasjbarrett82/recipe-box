# Almond Joys

- Serves: 48

## Description

Homemade coconut candies, each topped with a whole almond and dipped in chocolate.

## Cook Times

Prep: 45 min
Melt: 10 min
Chill: 1 h
Total: 1 h 55 min

## Ingredients

- 395 g (14 oz) can sweetened condensed milk
- 480 ml (2 cups) powdered sugar
- 960 ml (4 cups) coconut flakes, unsweetened
- 1 bag whole almonds
- 960 ml (4 cups) chocolate
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) shortening

## Method

### Prep

1. Line 13x9 dish with parchment paper

### Directions

1. Mix milk, sugar, & coconut in large bowl
2. Press into dish so it's flat and even
3. Line up almonds and press into top of coconut mixture
4. Transfer to fridge for 2+ hours to firm up
5. Cut into bite size pieces
6. Melt chocolate & shortening in a double boiler
7. Using fork, dip candy into chocolate
8. Set on parchment paper to cool before serving
