# Aloo Green Beans

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Side, Vegetarian, Vegan, High fiber
- Source: Indian Healthy Recipes
- Serves: 4

## Description

Potatoes and green beans stir-fried with garlic, ginger, jalapeño and Indian spices, finished with lemon juice.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Cook: 20 min
Total: 35 min

## Ingredients

- 30 ml (2 tbsp) EVOO
- 455 g (1 lb) potatoes, diced
- 455 g (1 lb) green beans
- 4 garlic cloves, miced
- 1 inch ginger, minced
- 1 jalapeno, minced
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) water
- 5 ml (1 tsp) kosher salt
- 1.25 ml (1/4 tsp) turmeric
- 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) chili powder
- 5 ml (1 tsp) garam masala
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) lemon juice

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat EVOO in large pan over med heat

### Directions

1. Add potatoes and saute for 2-3 mins
   - If green beans are old, add them too
2. Add garlic, ginger, jalapeno and saute for 1 min
3. Add water and cover pan
4. Let cook, stirring occasionally, until fork tender
5. Stir in salt, turmeric, chili powder, and garam masala
   - Add a little water if too dry
6. Stir fry for 2 mins and remove from heat
7. Add lemon juice and any more seasonings to taste
8. Serve with rice or naan along with dal or curry
