# Ambrosia

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert, Holiday
- Source: @bdylanhollis

- Emoji: 🍊
- Serves: 20

## Description

Old-fashioned fruit salad of pineapple, mandarin oranges, cherries, coconut and marshmallows in a whipped cream and sour cream dressing.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Chill: 1 h
Total: 1 h 15 min

## Ingredients

- 320 ml (1 1/3 cups) heavy cream, whipped
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) sour cream
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) coconut, shredded
- 240 ml (1 cup) walnuts, chopped
- 455 g (1 lb) maraschino cherries, drained
- 565 g (20 oz) pineapple, drained
- 680 g (1 1/2 lb) mandarin oranges, drained
- 480 ml (2 cups) mini marshmallows

## Method

1. Mix cream, sour cream, & coconut in large bowl
2. Mix in walnuts, cherries, pineapple
3. Gently fold in oranges
4. Chill in fridge for 1+ hours
5. Fold in marshmallows before serving
