# Apple Cider Cookies

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert
- Source: @bdylanhollis
- Serves: 18

## Description

Soft, spiced cookies made with apple cider and fresh diced apple.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Chill: 15–20 min
Bake: 15–17 min
Total: 55 min

## Ingredients

- 180 ml (3/4 cup) butter, room temperature
- 240 ml (1 cup) brown sugar
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) sugar
- 1 egg
- 480 ml (2 cups) flour
- 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) salt
- 5 ml (1 tsp) baking soda
- 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) nutmeg
- 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) allspice
- 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) ginger
- 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) cardamom
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) cinnamon
- 1 apple, finely chopped
- 60 ml (1/4 cup) + 80 ml (1/3 cup) apple cider
- 240 ml (1 cup) chopped walnuts (optional)
- 45 ml (3 tbsp) butter, melted
- Powdered sugar

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)

### Directions

1. Cream butter and sugars in large bowl
2. Mix in egg
3. In another bowl mix flour, salt, soda, spices
4. Mix in wet + dry with apple, 60 ml (1/4 cup) cider
5. Chill for 15-20 minutes before rolling out onto parchment lined pan
6. Bake for 15-17 mins
7. Mix melted butter and 80 ml (1/3 cup) cider with enough powdered sugar to make frosting
8. Drizzle over cookies and serve
