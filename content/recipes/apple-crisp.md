# Apple Crisp

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert, High fiber
- Serves: 8

## Description

Apples with a splash of bourbon, baked under a crisp oat, pecan and brown sugar topping.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Bake: 45 min
Cool: 15 min
Total: 1 h 20 min

## Ingredients

- 6 large apples, peeled and sliced
- 45 ml (3 tbsp) white sugar
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) cornstarch
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) kosher salt
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) bourbon
- 120 ml (½ cup) rolled oats
- 180 ml (¾ cup) brown sugar
- 120 ml (½ cup) flour
- 240 ml (1 cup) pecans, finely chopped
- 115 g (1 stick) butter
- 5 ml (1 tsp) cinnamon
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) nutmeg
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) kosher salt
- Vanilla ice cream and/or caramel sauce

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 190 °C (375 °F)
2. Grease 8x8 or 11x7 (1.9 L / 2 quarts) casserole dish

### Directions

1. Combine apple slices, sugar, cornstarch, salt, & bourbon in dish
2. Add remaining ingredients in separate bowl
3. Mash with fork or pastry blender until crumb consistency
4. Pour over apple mixture
5. Bake for 45 minutes
6. Remove and let cool for at least 15 minutes
7. Serve with ice cream and/or sauce
