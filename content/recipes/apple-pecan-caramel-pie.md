# Apple Pecan Caramel Pie

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert, High fiber, Holiday
- Serves: 10

## Description

Apple pie layered with toasted pecans and a homemade caramel sauce.

## Cook Times

Prep: 45 min
Bake: 1 h
Cool: 2 h
Total: 3 h 45 min

## Ingredients

- 1 serving pie dough
- 480 ml (2 cups) whole pecans, toasted

### Apple filling

- 1.6 kg (3 ½ lb) baking apples, peeled and sliced
- 60 ml (¼ cup) fresh lemon juice
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) butter, softened
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) flour
- 80 ml (1/3 cup) brown sugar
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) sugar
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) cinnamon
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) ginger
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) kosher salt
- Pinch of allspice and nutmeg

### Caramel

- 240 ml (1 cup) sugar
- 115 g (1 stick) butter
- 160 ml (2/3 cup) heavy cream
- 60 ml (¼ cup) light corn syrup
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) maple syrup
- Optional: 30 ml (2 tbsp) bourbon whiskey
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) molasses
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) kosher salt

## Method

1. Par cook the crust in a 9 inch pie plate
2. Mix butter with flour to a smooth paste
3. Mix with remaining filling ingredients
4. Combine with apples in a large pot
5. Cook over medium heat ~20 minutes until apples are soft
6. Cool and scrape into pie crust
7. Meanwhile, combine all caramel ingredients in a heavy saucepan over medium heat
8. Stop stirring and cook until thermometer reaches 113 °C (236 °F) (~5 minutes)
9. Remove from heat and let cool to at least 88 °C (190 °F)
   - Optional: crush and mix pecans into caramel instead of using for top
10. Spoon the caramel over the apple mixture
11. Arrange the toasted pecans over the top of the pie
12. Let cool completely before serving
