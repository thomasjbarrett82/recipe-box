# Apple Pie

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert, High fiber, Holiday
- Source: Serious Eats (BraveTart)
- Image: /images/apple-pie.jpg
- Serves: 8

## Description

A classic double-crust apple pie. Letting the spiced apples sit for a few hours draws out their juices for a thicker filling.

## Cook Times

Prep: 30 min
Macerate: 3 h
Chill: 30–60 min
Bake: 65–75 min
Total: 6 h 10 min

## Ingredients

### Filling

- 6-7 tart apples, like Granny Smith (6-7 apples)
- 240 ml (1 cup) brown sugar
- 10 ml (2 tsp) cinnamon
- 5 ml (1 tsp) kosher salt
- 1.25 ml (1/4 tsp) ground ginger
- 1.25 ml (1/4 tsp) nutmeg
- 1.25 ml (1/4 tsp) cloves

### To finish

- 60 ml (1/4 cup) cornstarch or tapioca starch
- Pie crust (2 pieces)
- 1 egg
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) cream
- 1.25 ml (1/4 tsp) kosher salt

## Method

### Prep

1. Peel and core the apples, then slice into 1/2 inch wedges
2. Combine filling ingredients in large ziploc bag
3. Toss to combine and let sit at least 3 hours to macerate
4. Flip occasionally to distribute the syrup
5. Can be held up to 8 hours in the fridge

### Directions

1. Add starch to filling, reseal and toss to combine
2. Place bottom pie crust into 8" or 9" pie pan
3. Pour filling into pie pan
4. Drape top pie crust over filling
5. Pinch and trim the edges
6. Place in the fridge for 30-60 minutes until cold and firm
7. Preheat oven to 205 °C (400 °F) before cooking and put rack on low-middle
8. Whisk egg, cream, salt
9. Brush the egg wash over the chilled pie crust
10. Use a sharp knife to cut vents on the top crust
11. Place pie on a lined sheet pan in the oven
12. Bake for 65-75 minutes until 91 °C (195 °F) in the center
13. Remove and cool at least 1 hours before serving
