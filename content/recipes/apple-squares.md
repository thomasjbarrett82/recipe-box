# Apple Squares

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert
- Source: Tara
- Image: /images/apple-squares.jpg
- Serves: 24

## Description

Apple pie filling baked between two layers of flaky pastry in a sheet pan, then cut into squares.

## Cook Times

Prep: 40 min
Bake: 1 h
Total: 1 h 40 min

## Ingredients

### Crust

- 600 ml (2 ½ cups) flour
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) salt
- 240 ml (1 cup) shortening
- 2 egg yolks
- 60 ml (¼ cup) milk

### Filling

- 1.2 L (5 cups) apple, diced
- 240 ml (1 cup) sugar
- 7.5 ml (1 ½ tsp) cinnamon

### Other

- 2 egg whites
- Powdered sugar frosting (optional)

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)

### Directions

1. In medium bowl, combine crust ingredients
2. Divide dough in half
3. Roll out one half over greased cookie sheet
4. Sprinkle corn flakes over dough
5. In another bowl, combine the filling ingredients
6. Spread filling over dough
7. Roll out the other dough half and place over filling
8. Beat the egg white and brush over top of dough
9. Bake for 1 hour
10. Remove and cool before serving
11. (Optional) drizzle frosting over top after cooling
