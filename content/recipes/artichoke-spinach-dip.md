# Artichoke Spinach Dip

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Appetizer, Vegetarian
- Source: Tara
- Image: /images/artichoke-spinach-dip.jpg
- Emoji: 🥬
- Serves: 16

## Description

Warm, creamy dip of spinach, artichoke hearts, cream cheese, Parmesan and pepper jack.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Cook: 25 min
Total: 40 min

## Ingredients

- 285 g (10 oz) box frozen chopped spinach, thawed and drained
- 395 g (14 oz) can artichoke hearts, drained
- 1 medium shallot, minced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 225 g (8 oz) cream cheese, softened
- 240 ml (1 cup) sour cream
- 120 ml (½ cup) Parmesan, grated
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 240 ml (1 cup) Pepper Jack, shredded

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 230 °C (450 °F)

### Directions

1. Mix everything together except pepper jack cheese
2. Spread into a casserole dish or cast-iron pan
3. Sprinkle pepper jack over the top
4. Bake until cheese is melted and starting to brown
5. Cool slightly and serve with chips or crackers
