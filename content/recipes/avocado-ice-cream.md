# Avocado Ice Cream

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert
- Source: @bdylanhollis
- Image: /images/avocado-ice-cream.jpg
- Serves: 10

## Description

Rich, pale green ice cream made with ripe avocados and a cooked custard base.

## Cook Times

Prep: 30 min
Chill: 2 h
Churn: 25 min
Total: 2 h 55 min

## Ingredients

- 120 ml (1/2 cup) sugar
- 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) salt
- 480 ml (2 cups) milk
- 2 eggs
- 240 ml (1 cup) heavy cream
- 2 avocados, mashed
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) lemon juice

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat small pot over med-low heat

### Directions

1. Scald sugar, salt, milk in pot
2. Whisk eggs in a large bowl
3. Slowly mix milk into eggs
4. Stir in cream, avocados, lemon juice
5. Freeze 4+ hours until firm
