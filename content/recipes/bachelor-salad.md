# Bachelor Salad

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Grill, High fiber, Favorite
- Image: /images/bachelor-salad.jpg
- Serves: 4

## Description

A steakhouse dinner salad: grilled steak, roasted potatoes and onions, tomatoes and blue cheese over leaf lettuce with a balsamic dressing.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Cook: 30 min
Total: 50 min

## Ingredients

### Dressing

- 120 ml (½ cup) balsamic vinegar
- 10 ml (2 tsp) Dijon mustard
- 5 ml (1 tsp) minced garlic
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) honey
- 5 ml (1 tsp) thyme
- 240 ml (1 cup) olive oil

### Salad

- 2 large Yukon Gold potatoes, diced
- 240 ml (1 cup) + 15 ml (1 tbsp) + 15 ml (1 tbsp) olive oil
- 2 large yellow onions, chopped
- 4 (170 g / 6 oz) prime quality steaks
- 2 heads leaf lettuce, cleaned and separated
- 240 ml (1 cup) quality tomatoes, diced
- 225 g (8 oz) crumbled blue cheese

## Method

### Prep

1. Mix dressing together and set aside
2. Heat grill to med-high
3. Heat small pan to medium
4. Heat oven to 230 °C (450 °F)

### Directions

1. In bowl, toss potatoes w/ 15 ml (1 tbsp) oil, rosemary, salt & pepper
2. Place on baking sheet and roast for 15-20 minutes until golden brown
3. Heat 15 ml (1 tbsp) oil in pan and caramelize the onions
4. Season steaks with salt & pepper & a little oil
5. Grill steaks for 7 minutes per side, until medium-rare
6. Lest rest for 5 minutes before cubing
7. Toss lettuce, tomatoes, cheese, potatoes, onions, and steak together
8. Coat with dressing and serve
