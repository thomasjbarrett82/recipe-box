# Baked Apples

- Serves: 3

## Description

Whole apples filled with oats, sugar, cinnamon and butter, baked until tender.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Bake: 30 min
Total: 40 min

## Ingredients

- 3 apples, cored with bottom intact
- 240 ml (1 cup) sugar OR brown sugar
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) cinnamon
- 240 ml (1 cup) rolled oats
- 3 pats of butter
- 240 ml (1 cup) water

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)
2. Line 8x8 baking dish with aluminum foil

### Directions

1. Mix sugar, cinnamon, oats in bowl
2. Arrange apples in dish
3. Pour filling into apples
4. Top each apple with butter
5. Add water to dish around apples
6. Bake for 30 mins
7. Let cool before serving with vanilla ice cream
