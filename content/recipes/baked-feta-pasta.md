# Baked Feta Pasta

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Pasta
- Image: /images/baked-feta-pasta.jpg
- Emoji: 🍝
- Serves: 6

## Description

A block of feta baked with cherry tomatoes and garlic, then stirred into a creamy sauce with pasta, chicken and basil.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Bake: 40 min
Total: 55 min

## Ingredients

- 950 ml (2 pints) cherry tomatoes
- 455 g (16 oz) feta cheese
- 60 ml (4 tbsp) EVOO
- 10 ml (2 tsp) garlic salt
- 5 ml (1 tsp) black pepper
- 120 ml (½ cup) heavy cream or half & half
- 120 ml (½ cup) Parmesan cheese
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) garlic
- 60 ml (4 tbsp) fresh basil or 30 ml (2 tbsp) dried basil
- 455 g (16 oz) cooked pasta
- 455 g (1 lb) chicken, cooked and shredded (optional)

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)

### Directions

1. Layer cherry tomatoes in casserole dish
2. Layer cheese on top
3. Drizzle EVOO and sprinkle salt & pepper over everything
4. Bake for 40 minutes
5. Remove and mash everything together
6. Mix in cream, Parmesan, garlic, and basil
7. Fold in pasta (and chicken) until combined
