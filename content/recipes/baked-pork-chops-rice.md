# Baked Pork Chops & Rice

## Recipe Data

- Image: /images/baked-pork-chops-rice.jpg
- Emoji: 🍚
- Serves: 6

## Description

Pork chops baked over rice in a creamy onion and mushroom sauce made with rice milk.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Bake: 40–45 min
Total: 1 h

## Ingredients

- 6 pork chops
- 720 ml (3 cups) cooked rice
- 720 ml (3 cups) rice milk
- 45 ml (3 tbsp) cornstarch
- 1 pkg dry onion & mushroom soup mix

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 205 °C (400 °F)
2. You can steam the rice and prepare the gravy at the same time
3. Sear the pork chops before placing in rice for extra flavor
   - Shorten the baking time to 25 minutes

### Directions

1. Mix rice milk, cornstarch, and soup mix in a small pot
2. Place pot over medium heat
3. Stir mixture frequently until it reaches a gravy consistency
4. Remove from heat and fold into the rice, reserving 60 ml (¼ cup) of gravy
5. Pour rice mixture into a 9”x13” baking dish
6. Arrange chops evenly in dish, pushing down into the rice
7. Pour reserved gravy over the chops
8. Place in the oven and bake for 40-45 minutes, until chops are done
9. Serve with salt & pepper
