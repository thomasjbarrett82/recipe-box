# Banana Bread Waffles

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Breakfast, Quick, Vegetarian
- Serves: 7

## Description

Waffles that taste like banana bread, made with mashed banana, buttermilk and brown sugar.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Cook: 20 min
Total: 30 min

## Ingredients

- 360 ml (1 1/2 cups) flour
- 5 ml (1 tsp) baking powder
- 1.25 ml (1/4 tsp) baking soda
- 1.25 ml (1/4 tsp) salt
- 240 ml (1 cup) mashed banana (about 2)
- 180 ml (3/4 cup) buttermilk
- 60 ml (1/4 cup) brown sugar
- 2 eggs
- 45 ml (3 tbsp) oil or melted butter
- 1.25 ml (1/4 tsp) vanilla

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat waffle iron

### Directions

1. Mix dry ingredients together
2. Mix wet ingredients in another bowl
3. Stir wet into dry until no clumps remain
4. Lightly grease waffle iron before adding batter
5. Drop into waffle iron by 60 ml (¼ cup) scoops
6. Cook until golden brown
7. Serve with any sweet or savory waffle toppings
