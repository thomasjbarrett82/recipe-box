# Banana Bread

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Bread, Favorite
- Image: /images/banana-bread.jpg
- Emoji: 🍌
- Serves: 20

## Description

Moist banana bread made with sour cream, with an optional splash of Bailey's.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Bake: 50–60 min
Total: 1 h 15 min

## Ingredients

- 180 ml (¾ cup) butter, softened
- 360 ml (1 ½ cups) sugar
- 2 eggs
- 5 ml (1 tsp) vanilla OR 15 ml (1 tbsp) Bailey’s
- 2 mashed overripe bananas
- 120 ml (½ cup) sour cream
- 480 ml (2 cups) flour
- 5 ml (1 tsp) baking soda
- 5 ml (1 tsp) kosher salt

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)
2. Lightly grease bottom of 2 bread pans

### Directions

1. In large bowl, cream sugar and butter
2. Mix in eggs, Bailey’s, bananas, and sour cream
3. In a medium bowl, combine the flour, baking soda, and salt
4. Add dry to wet, avoiding overmixing
5. Pour into bread pans
6. Bake for 50-60 minutes, until done
