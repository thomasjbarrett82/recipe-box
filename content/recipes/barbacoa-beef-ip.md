# Barbacoa Beef (IP)

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Spicy, Instant Pot
- Source: Tom
- Image: /images/barbacoa-beef-ip.jpg
- Emoji: 🌮
- Serves: 9

## Description

Chuck roast pressure-cooked with chipotles in adobo, green chiles, lime and spices, then shredded for tacos or bowls.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Cook: 1 h 15 min
Total: 1 h 30 min

## Ingredients

- 60 ml (1/4 cup) beer or stock
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 chipotles in adobo, chopped
- 115 g (4 oz) can green chilis
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) + 30 ml (2 tbsp) lime juice
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) apple cider vinegar
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) cumin powder
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) oregano
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) kosher salt
- 5 ml (1 tsp) black pepper
- 1.25 ml (1/4 tsp) nutmeg
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) EVOO
- 1.4–1.8 kg (3–4 lb) beef chuck roast, trimmed and chunked
- 3 bay leaves

## Method

### Prep

1. Mix everything except EVOO, roast, bay leaves and set aside

### Directions

1. Turn instant pot on saute
2. Add EVOO and sear beef
3. Cancel saute to avoid burning
4. Add sauce and mix with beef to cover it all
5. Add bay leaves on top
6. Cook at high pressure for 60 minutes
7. Let pressure naturally release
8. Discard bay leaves
9. Shred beef before serving
