# BBQ Spaghetti

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Pasta
- Source: The Neelys
- Image: /images/bbq-spaghetti.jpg
- Emoji: 🍝
- Serves: 8

## Description

Spaghetti tossed with ground beef, hot sausage, peppers and onion simmered in BBQ sauce.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Cook: 25 min
Total: 35 min

## Ingredients

- EVOO
- 455 g (1 lb) ground beef, crumbled
- 455 g (1 lb) hot sausage, diced
- 1 green pepper, diced
- 1 onion, diced
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) garlic salt
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) Italian seasoning
- 840–960 ml (3.5–4 cups) BBQ sauce
- 455 g (1 lb) spaghetti noodles

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat large pan over med heat
2. Heat large pot with water over high heat for noodles

### Directions

1. Sauce:
   - Add 15 ml (1 tbsp) EVOO to pan with beef and brown
   - Remove and set aside
   - Add sausage (with EVOO if needed) and brown
   - Remove and set aside
   - Add 15 ml (1 tbsp) EVOO with pepper and onion
   - Saute 2-3 minutes until softened
   - Return meat to pan and add BBQ sauce
   - Drop heat to low-med until pasta is done
2. Pasta:
   - When water starts boiling add the noodles
   - Cook for 7-8 minutes until al dente and drain
3. Once pasta is finished, mix together with sauce and serve
