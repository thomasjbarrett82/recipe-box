# Bean & Spinach Stew

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Stew, High fiber
- Source: @marcus.olaoire on Instagram
- Image: /images/bean-spinach-stew.jpg
- Serves: 6

## Description

Butter beans and vegetables simmered with white wine, cherry tomatoes and fresh spinach.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Cook: 40 min
Total: 55 min

## Ingredients

- EVOO
- 1 onion, diced
- 4 stalks celery, diced
- 2 large carrots, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- Kosher salt & pepper
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) Italian seasoning
- 3 cans butter beans
- 480 ml (2 cups) white wine
- 1 packet gravy mix
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) bouillon
- 455 g (1 lb) cherry tomatoes, halved
- 455 g (1 lb) fresh spinach, washed
- fresh parsley
- lemon juice

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat EVOO in large pan over med heat

### Directions

1. Add mirepoix and cook for 5 minutes until soft
2. Add garlic, salt, pepper, seasoning for 1 minute
3. Add beans, wine, gravy mix, bouillon
4. Bring to low boil
5. Turn to low and cover to simmer for 30 minutes
6. Add tomatoes & spinach, cooked until spinach is wilted
7. Add parsley and lemon juice to taste
