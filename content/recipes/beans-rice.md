# Beans & Rice

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Instant Pot, High fiber, Favorite
- Image: /images/beans-rice.jpg
- Emoji: 🫘
- Serves: 8

## Description

Dried beans and brown rice cooked together in the pressure cooker with tomatoes and cumin.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Cook: 1 h 10 min
Total: 1 h 20 min

## Ingredients

- 455 g (1 lb) dried beans
- 2 + 720 ml (3 cups) broth
- 480 ml (2 cups) brown rice
- 240 ml (1 cup) dried vegetables
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) cumin
- 5 ml (1 tsp) garlic powder
- 5 ml (1 tsp) chili powder
- 2 cans diced tomatoes
- Salt & pepper, hot sauce to taste

## Method

1. Place beans and 480 ml (2 cups) broth in pressure cooker
2. Cook for 30 mins on high and let natural release for 10
3. Add rice, vegetables, spices, and 720 ml (3 cups) broth
4. Cook for another 30 mins and let natural release for 10
5. Stir in tomatoes
6. Season to taste
7. Serve with salsa, cheese, and sour cream
