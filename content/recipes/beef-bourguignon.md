# Beef Bourguignon

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Stew, Dutch oven, Holiday
- Source: Adam Ragusea
- Image: /images/beef-bourguignon.jpg
- Emoji: 🍷
- Serves: 8

## Description

Chuck roast braised for hours in red wine and stock with carrots, celery, whole garlic and pearl onions.

## Cook Times

Prep: 30 min
Cook: 5 h 45 min
Total: 6 h 15 min

## Ingredients

- 1.8 kg (4 lb) chuck roast or short ribs, extra fat removed, chunked
- ~120 ml (1/2 cup) flour
- 30–45 ml (2–3 tbsp) tomato paste
- Red wine
- Chicken stock
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) Worcestershire
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) soy sauce
- 455 g (1 lb) carrots, peeled and chunked
- 2 stalks celery, chunked
- 6 whole cloves garlic, peeled and smashed
- 1 bag frozen pearl onions
- 2 packets unflavored gelatin
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) balsamic vinegar

## Method

### Prep

1. Place Dutch oven in oven with broiler on
2. Season beef with salt & pepper & EVOO

### Directions

1. Add beef to pot evenly to brown for 10 mins
2. Stir in enough flour to coat the meat
3. Return to oven for 5-10 minutes until flour browns
4. Add tomato paste and brown for another 3-4 minutes
5. Turn off broiler and set oven to 135 °C (275 °F)
6. Add enough red wine to go 1/3 way up the beef
7. Add enough stock to go 2/3 way up the beef
8. Add soy and Worcestershire and gently stir everything
9. Cover with lid and cook for 4 hours
10. Gently add in vegetables to avoid breaking up beef
11. Cover and cook for another 90 minutes
12. Bloom gelatin in a little cold water
13. Check sauce for flavor, add more seasoning as appropriate
14. Sprinkle balsamic and gelatin over the entire stew
15. Fold entire stew with large spatula to mix without breaking up beef
16. Turn on broiler again and broil for 10-15 minutes to finish
17. Serve with mashed potatoes or egg noodles
