# Beef & Cabbage Stew

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Stew, Instant Pot, High fiber, Favorite
- Image: /images/beef-cabbage-stew.jpg
- Emoji: 🥬
- Serves: 6

## Description

Beef stew meat, cabbage, potatoes and carrots in a tomato broth, made in the Instant Pot.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Cook: 1 h 30 min
Total: 1 h 50 min

## Ingredients

- 905 g (2 lb) beef stew meat, cubed
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) vegetable oil
- 480 ml (2 cups) chicken broth
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 3 medium carrots, diced
- 1/3 head cabbage, rough chopped
- 3 celery stalks, diced
- 2 large potatoes, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 225 g (8 oz) can tomato sauce
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) kosher salt
- 5 ml (1 tsp) black pepper
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 package onion soup mix
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) Worcestershire sauce

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat pressure cooker to sauté

### Directions

1. Heat oil in pressure cooker and brown beef on all sides
2. Add remaining ingredients
3. Secure the lid and cook at Stew setting (high) for 60 minutes
4. Set to Slow Cook for at least 30 minutes before releasing pressure
