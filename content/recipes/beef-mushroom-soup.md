# Beef & Mushroom Soup

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Soup, Dutch oven, Minnesota
- Image: /images/beef-mushroom-soup.jpg
- Serves: 6

## Description

Beef roast, mushrooms and wild rice braised in beef broth with oregano and fennel.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Cook: 2 h 15 min
Total: 2 h 35 min

## Ingredients

- 15 ml (1 tbsp) EVOO
- 905 g (2 lb) boneless roast
- 5 ml (1 tsp) kosher salt
- 5 ml (1 tsp) black pepper
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) gluten-free flour
- 455 g (16 oz) sliced wild or crimini mushrooms
- ½ yellow onion, diced
- 240 ml (1 cup) carrots, sliced or shredded
- 120 ml (½ cup) wild rice
- 1.4 L (6 cups) beef broth
- 10 ml (2 tsp) chopped oregano
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) fennel seeds

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat EVOO in Dutch oven (or braising pan) over med-high heat

### Directions

1. In shallow dish, mix salt, pepper, and flour
2. Cover roast with flour mixture
3. Add roast to Dutch oven and brown on all sides
4. Remove and set aside
5. Add mushrooms, onion, and carrots
6. Cook for 5-6 minutes
7. Return roast to Dutch oven and add 240 ml (1 cup) of broth
8. Bring to a boil, then turn to low and cover
9. Let braise for 2 hours until fork tender
10. Skim fat with ladle (ice cube inside to help fat congeal)
11. Add remaining ingredients and let simmer for another ½ hour
12. Season with salt & pepper to taste
