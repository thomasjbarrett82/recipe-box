# Beef Stew (IP)

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Stew, Instant Pot
- Image: /images/beef-stew-ip.jpg
- Serves: 8

## Description

Rich beef stew made fast in the Instant Pot, with mushrooms, red wine and stew vegetables.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Cook: 1 h 15 min
Total: 1 h 35 min

## Ingredients

- 720 ml (3 cups) chicken stock
- 2 packets unflavored gelatin
- 45 ml (3 tbsp) tomato paste
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) soy sauce
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) fish sauce
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) Worcestershire sauce
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) oil
- 905 g–1.8 kg (2–4 lb) chuck roast
- Kosher salt and ground black pepper
- 455 g (1 lb) mushrooms, quartered
- 240 ml (1 cup) red wine
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) cornstarch
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 package frozen stew vegetables
- 3-4 cloves garlic, minced
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) thyme
- 455 g (1 lb) potatoes, cubed

## Method

### Prep

1. Combine stock, gelatin, paste, soy, fish, Worcestershire and set aside
2. Season the beef with salt and pepper

### Directions

1. Heat oil in IP and brown the beef on all sides
2. Remove and set aside
3. Add mushrooms to pot and cook for 5 minutes
4. Remove and set aside
5. Add wine to deglaze pot
6. Reduce for 3-5 minutes
7. Cube beef while wine is reducing and toss with cornstarch
8. Add beef, beef drippings, bay leaves, and stock mixture to the pot
9. Cover and cook on high for 40 minutes, then do a rapid release
10. Remove bay leaves and add remaining ingredients
11. Cover and cook on high for 15 minutes, then do a rapid release
12. Season to taste with salt & pepper before serving
