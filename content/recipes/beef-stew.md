# Beef Stew

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Stew, Dutch oven, High fiber
- Image: /images/beef-stew.jpg
- Serves: 8

## Description

Classic beef stew of chuck roast, mushrooms, carrots and celery braised in red wine and stock.

## Cook Times

Prep: 30 min
Cook: 2 h 30 min
Total: 3 h

## Ingredients

### Sauce base

- 960 ml (4 cups) chicken stock
- 115 g (4 oz) tomato paste
- 5 ml (1 tsp) fish sauce
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) Worcestershire
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) soy sauce
- 4 packets unflavored gelatin (30 g / 1 oz total)

### Stew

- 30 ml (2 tbsp) oil
- 1.4 kg (3 lb) chuck roast, cut into 2 steaks
- Kosher salt & black pepper
- 455 g (1 lb) white button mushrooms
- 4-5 carrots, peeled and rough chopped
- 2 ribs celery, diced
- 4-5 cloves garlic, minced
- 240 ml (1 cup) red wine or sherry
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) A/P flour
- 2 bay leaves
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) Italian seasoning
- 455 g (1 lb) Yukon Gold potatoes, peeled and cubed
- 285 g (10 oz) frozen peas

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat Dutch oven on high
2. Heat oven to 165 °C (325 °F)
3. Season beef with salt and pepper

### Directions

1. Mix all sauce base ingredients in blender until homogeneous
2. Heat oil in Dutch oven and add beef
3. Cook until well browned on both sides
4. Remove and let rest on a rimmed sheet
5. Add mushrooms, carrots, celery and cook until lightly browned
6. Add garlic and cook for another minute
7. Add wine and deglaze the pot
8. Cook until reduced by 1/2
9. While reducing, cut beef into 1 inch chunks and toss with flour
10. After reduced, stir in the sauce base
11. Return meat to pot and add seasonings
12. Return pot to a simmer over med heat
13. Transfer to oven with lid partially open
14. Cook for 90 minutes
15. Add potatoes and cook for another 45-60 minutes until beef is tender
16. Remove from oven and stir in peas
17. Season to taste with salt and pepper before serving
