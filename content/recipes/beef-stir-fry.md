# Beef Stir Fry

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main
- Image: /images/beef-stir-fry.jpg
- Emoji: 🥢
- Serves: 4

## Description

Marinated beef and stir-fry vegetables in a garlic, ginger and soy sauce, served over rice.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Cook: 15 min
Total: 35 min

## Ingredients

- 455 g (1 lb) beef, sliced 1/8” thick
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) + 15 ml (1 tbsp) + 15 ml (1 tbsp) vegetable oil
- 960 ml (4 cups) stir-fry blend
- Cooked rice

### Marinade

- 30 ml (2 tbsp) soy sauce
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) minced garlic
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) minced ginger
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) rice vinegar
- 5 ml (1 tsp) crushed red pepper
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) cornstarch

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat large heavy wok or skillet over high heat

### Directions

1. Combine marinade ingredients with beef, set aside
2. Heat 15 ml (1 tbsp) oil in skillet
3. Add vegetables and stir-fry for 5 minutes
4. Remove from skillet and set aside
5. Heat 15 ml (1 tbsp) oil in skillet
6. Add ½ of beef and marinade mixture
7. Stir-fry for 3-5 minutes until beef is cooked
8. Remove from skillet and set aside
9. Heat remaining oil in skillet
10. Add remaining beef and marinade mixture
11. Stir-fry for 3-5 minutes until beef is cooked
12. Return the vegetables and beef to the skillet
13. Remove from heat and stir to combine
14. Server over rice
