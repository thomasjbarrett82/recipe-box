# Beef Stroganoff

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main
- Serves: 6

## Description

Tender strips of beef and mushrooms in a sour cream and Dijon sauce over egg noodles.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Cook: 25 min
Total: 40 min

## Ingredients

- 455 g (1 lb) egg noodles
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) + 15 ml (1 tbsp) EVOO
- 905 g (2 lb) beef tenderloin or sirloin steaks
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) butter
- 1 onion, minced
- 455 g (1 lb) mushrooms, sliced and rinsed
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) cornstarch
- 120 ml (½ cup) sour cream
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) Dijon mustard
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) smoked paprika
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) soy sauce
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) vinegar
- Salt & pepper, to taste

## Method

### Prep

1. Start a large pot of water to boil and cook the noodles
2. Season the steaks with salt & pepper
3. Heat 15 ml (1 tbsp) EVOO in a large heavy skillet over high heat

### Directions

1. Cook steaks in the skillet until well-browned and medium-rare
2. Transfer to a plate and set aside, then slice thin when rested
3. Add the remaining oil to the pan and add the mushrooms
4. Cook for 5 minutes until just starting to brown
5. Add butter and onions
6. Cook for another 5 minutes
7. In a bowl whisk together the remaining ingredients
8. Add the mixture to the pan
9. Bring to a simmer to thicken
10. Return the steaks to the pan and stir together
11. Serve over the noodles
