# Beef Tenderloin

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Holiday
- Image: /images/beef-tenderloin.jpg
- Emoji: 🥩
- Serves: 8

## Description

Dry-brined beef tenderloin, slow-roasted, then seared with butter and thyme. Serve with horseradish cream.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Dry brine: 8–36 h
Roast: 2–3 h
Total: 10 h 30 min

## Ingredients

- 1 beef tenderloin, trimmed
- Kosher salt and black pepper
- 60 ml (4 tbsp) butter
- 4 sprigs thyme
- 1 shallot, sliced
- Chives, minced, for serving
- Horseradish cream, for serving

## Method

### Prep

1. Tie tenderloin with butcher’s twine at 1 inch intervals
2. Season generously with salt and pepper
3. Set on wire rack in foil-lined baking sheet
4. Refrigerate uncovered for 8-36 hours
5. Heat oven to 107 °C (225 °F)

### Directions

1. Place sheet in oven and roast until internal temp is 49 °C (120 °F) (2-3 hours)
2. Remove and let rest for 10 minutes
3. Cut and remove twine
4. While roast is resting, turn on broiler and heat a skillet on high
5. Move the top oven rack 6 inches under the heating element
6. Heat butter in skillet until it turns a light nutty brown
7. Add thyme and shallots and stir until crackling stops
8. Pour mixture over roast and spread with a spoon
9. Remove the shallots from the top
10. Place pan under broiler, turning every 30 seconds
11. Remove after 2-3 minutes once the outside is browned
12. Transfer to cutting board and let rest for 5 minutes
13. Slice into ½ inch slices
14. Sprinkle with chives and salt before serving
