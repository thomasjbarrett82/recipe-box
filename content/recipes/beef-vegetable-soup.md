# Beef Vegetable Soup

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Soup, Instant Pot
- Source: Serious Eats
- Image: /images/beef-vegetable-soup.jpg
- Emoji: 🥕
- Serves: 10

## Description

Hearty soup of chuck roast, vegetables and rice in a tomato and thyme broth, made in the Instant Pot.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Cook: 1 h 30 min
Total: 1 h 50 min

## Ingredients

- 905 g (2 lb) chuck roast
- Kosher salt & black pepper
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) oil
- 4 large carrots, diced
- 2 large onions, diced
- 3 celery ribs, diced
- 1 can diced tomatoes
- 4-5 cloves garlic, minced
- 240 ml (1 cup) white wine
- 1.9 L (2 quarts) chicken stock
- fresh thyme
- 240 ml (1 cup) uncooked rice
- 5 ml (1 tsp) fish sauce
- 5 ml (1 tsp) Worcestershire
- 225 g (8 oz) frozen peas
- 225 g (8 oz) green beans

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat instant pot on saute and add oil

### Directions

1. Season beef with salt and pepper
2. Brown beef in oil and then set aside to rest
3. Add carrots, onions, ribs and cook until softened
4. Add tomatoes, garlic, wine, stock, thyme
5. Chop beef into 1 inch chunks and add to pot, along with juices
6. Cover and cook on high pressure for 60 minutes
7. Release pressure
8. Stir in rice, fish sauce, and Worcestershire
9. Cover and cook on high pressure for 10 minutes
10. Release presure
11. Add peas and beans and cook until warmed through
12. Serve
