# Beer Cheese Soup

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Soup
- Image: /images/beer-cheese-soup.jpg
- Emoji: 🍺
- Serves: 7

## Description

Creamy Cheddar soup with dark beer, garlic and dry mustard.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Cook: 25 min
Total: 35 min

## Ingredients

- 120 ml (½ cup) butter
- 120 ml (½ cup) flour
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 5 ml (1 tsp) onion powder
- 5 ml (1 tsp) Worcestershire
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) salt & pepper
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) dry mustard
- 720 ml (3 cups) chicken broth
- 360 ml (1 ½ cups) heavy cream
- 240 ml (1 cup) dark beer
- 455 g (16 oz) shredded Cheddar

## Method

### Prep

1. Place large, heavy pan over med-high heat

### Directions

1. Melt butter in pan and add flour
2. Whisk and cook until smooth & bubbly
3. Add garlic, onion, Worcestershire, salt, pepper, and mustard
4. Gradually stir in broth, cream, and beer
5. Cook until thickened
6. Slowly add cheese by small handfuls, waiting until melted to add more
7. Do not boil
8. Serve when all cheese is melted into soup
