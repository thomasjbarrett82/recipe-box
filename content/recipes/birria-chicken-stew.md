# Birria Chicken Stew

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Stew, Spicy, Slow cooker, High fiber
- Source: Tom
- Image: /images/birria-chicken-stew.jpg
- Emoji: 🌶️
- Serves: 6

## Description

Chicken thighs slow-cooked with birria seasoning, peppers, corn, black beans and Rotel. Serve with tostadas.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Slow cook: 4–5 h
Total: 4 h 10 min

## Ingredients

- 905 g (2 lb) chicken thighs
- birria seasoning for 905 g (2 lb) meat
- 1 bag frozen peppers & onion
- 1 bag frozen corn
- 2 cans seasoned black beans
- 2 cans Rotel
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) kosher salt
- Tostadas, cheese, cilantro, sour cream, diced avocado for serving

## Method

1. Place chicken & seasoning in crockpot
2. Cook on high for 2-3 hours until tender
3. Remove any bones and shred the meat
4. Add veggies, beans, Rotel, salt
5. Cook on high for another 2 hours
6. Serve with toppings
