# Black Bean Brownies (GF)

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert, Gluten free
- Source: @bdylanhollis
- Serves: 16

## Description

Fudgy gluten-free brownies made with black beans, cocoa and oats.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Bake: 25–35 min
Cool: 10 min
Total: 55 min

## Ingredients

- 425 g (15 oz) can black beans, drained and rinsed
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) cocoa powder
- 180 ml (3/4 cup) quick oats
- 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) salt
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) honey
- 45 ml (3 tbsp) sugar
- 60 ml (1/4 cup) butter, melted
- 5 ml (1 tsp) vanilla extract
- 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) baking powder
- 1 egg
- 10 ml (2 tsp) psyllium husk powder
- 160 ml (2/3 cup) chocolate chips
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) chopped walnuts (optional)

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)
2. Grease an 8x8 pan OR brownie pan
   - OR line with parchment paper

### Directions

1. Add all ingredients except chocolate chips & walnuts to food processor
2. Blend until smooth
3. Stir in chocolate chips & walnuts
4. Pour into pan
5. Bake for 25-35 minutes
6. Let cool at least 10 minutes before serving
   - Optionally sprinkle with some more chocolate chips before it cools
