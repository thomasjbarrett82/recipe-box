# Black Bean Soup

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Soup, High fiber
- Image: /images/black-bean-soup.jpg
- Emoji: 🫘
- Serves: 10

## Description

Hearty vegetable soup of black beans, navy beans, lentils, roasted corn and butternut squash.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Cook: 1 h 30 min
Total: 1 h 50 min

## Ingredients

- 480 ml (2 cups) roasted corn
- 480 ml (2 cups) butternut squash, cubed
- 45 ml (3 tbsp) olive oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 4 celery stalks, chopped
- 1.9 L (8 cups) vegetable stock
- 1 can black beans, drained & rinsed
- 1 can navy beans, drained & rinsed
- 240 ml (1 cup) lentils, rinsed
- 1 (795 g / 28 oz) can diced tomatoes
- 2 bay leaves
- 5 ml (1 tsp) basil
- 5 ml (1 tsp) thyme
- 5 ml (1 tsp) oregano
- 5 ml (1 tsp) smoked paprika
- Salt & pepper to taste
- Garnish: diced bacon & hot sauce

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oil in stock pot over medium high heat.

### Directions

1. Add onions & celery to pot and sauté until translucent.
2. Add garlic and continue for 1 minute.
3. Deglaze with some stock, then add the rest of the stock.
4. Stir in beans, tomatoes, squash, and corn.
5. Add herbs and bring to a boil.
6. Reduce to a simmer and cover.
7. Cook for 1 ½ hours, stirring occasionally to prevent sticking.
8. Add stock if too much liquid is absorbed.
9. Serve with bacon & hot sauce on the side.
