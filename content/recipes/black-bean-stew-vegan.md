# Black Bean Stew (Vegan)

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Stew, Spicy, Instant Pot, Vegetarian, Vegan, High fiber
- Serves: 6

## Description

Dry black beans pressure-cooked with peppers, jalapeños, tomatoes, cumin and a splash of orange juice.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Cook: 1 h 30 min
Total: 1 h 50 min

## Ingredients

- 455 g (1 lb) black beans
- 720 ml (3 cups) water
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) EVOO
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 onion, minced
- 2-3 stalks celery, chopped
- 2-3 carrots, chopped
- 1 red & 1 green bell pepper, chopped
- 1-2 jalapeno peppers, diced
- 1 can diced tomatoes
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) cumin
- 5 ml (1 tsp) chili powder
- 240 ml (1 cup) OJ
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) kosher salt
- 5 ml (1 tsp) black pepper
- (Optional) diced avocado for serving

## Method

1. Cook beans & water in instant pot for 35 minutes
2. Natural release for 20 minutes
3. Add remaining ingredients
4. Cook on high pressure for 20 minutes
5. Natural release for 10 minutes
