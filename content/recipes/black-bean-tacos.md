# Black Bean Tacos

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Quick, High fiber
- Source: Instagram
- Image: /images/black-bean-tacos.jpg
- Serves: 6

## Description

A quick black bean taco filling simmered with smoked paprika, cumin and tomato paste.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Cook: 15 min
Total: 25 min

## Ingredients

- 15 ml (1 tbsp) EVOO
- 1 onion, diced
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) carrot, shredded
- 3-4 garlic cloves, minced
- 5 ml (1 tsp) smoked paprika
- 5 ml (1 tsp) oregano
- 5 ml (1 tsp) cumin
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) tomato paste
- 2 cans black beans, drained and rinsed
- 240 ml (1 cup) stock
- S&P to taste

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat large pan over medium heat and add EVOO

### Directions

1. Saute onion and carrots for 5 mins
2. Add garlic for 1 min
3. Add spices & tomato paste for 1 min
4. Add beans & stock
5. Bring to boil and cook for 5 mins
6. Roughly mash and serve with taco shells and toppings
