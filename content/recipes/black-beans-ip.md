# Black Beans (IP)

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Side, Instant Pot, High fiber
- Serves: 14

## Description

Dry black beans pressure-cooked with chorizo, onion, garlic and bay leaves.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Cook: 1 h 15 min
Total: 1 h 25 min

## Ingredients

- 15 ml (1 tbsp) oil
- 170 g (6 oz) chorizo, diced
- 455 g (1 lb) dry black beans
- 1 onion, diced
- 6 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 bay leaves
- 1.9 L (2 quarts) chicken stock
- Salt & pepper
- Fresh cilantro, chopped

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat the pressure cooker to sauté

### Directions

1. Add oil to cooker and heat to shimmering
2. Add chorizo and onion, and cook until browned
3. Add the remaining ingredients
4. Close lid and set to high pressure
5. Cook for 45 minutes
6. Turn off and allow pressure to release naturally
7. Remove lid and discard the bay leaves
8. Season with salt & pepper to taste
9. Serve with cilantro sprinkled over the top
