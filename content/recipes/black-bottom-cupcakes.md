# Black Bottom Cupcakes

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert
- Source: Tara
- Image: /images/black-bottom-cupcakes.jpg
- Serves: 18

## Description

Chocolate cupcakes with a cream cheese and chocolate chip center.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Bake: 20 min
Total: 40 min

## Ingredients

### Filling

- 170 g (6 oz) chocolate chips
- 225 g (8 oz) cream cheese
- 1 egg
- 80 ml (1/3 cup) sugar
- 0.6 ml (1/8 tsp) salt

### Cupcake

- 360 ml (1 ½ cups) flour
- 60 ml (¼ cup) cocoa powder
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) salt
- 240 ml (1 cup) sugar
- 5 ml (1 tsp) baking soda
- 240 ml (1 cup) water
- 120 ml (½ cup) oil
- 5 ml (1 tsp) vanilla

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)
2. Prepare double boiler on stove
3. Place liners in cupcake pan

### Directions

1. Melt chips in double boiler
2. Remove from heat and stir in remaining filling ingredients
3. In large bowl, combine flour, cocoa, salt, sugar, & baking soda
4. Stir in water, oil, & vanilla until smooth
5. Fill each liner 1/3 full with dough, 15 ml (1 tbsp) filling, then top with dough
6. Bake about 20 minutes
7. Remove and let cool before serving
