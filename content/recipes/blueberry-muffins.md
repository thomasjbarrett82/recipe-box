# Blueberry Muffins

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Breakfast, Vegetarian, High fiber
- Source: Tara
- Image: /images/blueberry-muffins.jpg
- Emoji: 🫐
- Serves: 12

## Description

Wholesome blueberry muffins made with whole wheat flour, bran cereal and honey.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Bake: 20 min
Total: 35 min

## Ingredients

- 300 ml (1 ¼ cups) whole wheat flour
- 1.25 ml (¼ tsp) salt
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) baking powder
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) cinnamon
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) nutmeg
- 360 ml (1 ½ cups) Kellog’s All-Bran Buds cereal
- 300 ml (1 ¼ cups) skim milk
- 2 egg whites
- 120 ml (½ cup) honey
- 60 ml (¼ cup) canola oil
- 5 ml (1 tsp) lemon peel, grated
- 240 ml (1 cup) blueberries

## Method

### Prep

1. Fill a 2.9 L (12 cups) muffin tin with liners
2. Heat oven to 205 °C (400 °F)

### Directions

1. Stir together flour, salt, baking powder, cinnamon, and nutmeg
2. In a large bowl mix cereal & milk
3. Let stand for 2+ minutes to soften the cereal
4. Beat egg whites and add to cereal mixture
5. Add the honey, canola oil, lemon peel, and blueberries and mix
6. Add the dry ingredients and stir until fully mixed
7. Pour into muffin tin liners
8. Bake for 20 minutes
