# Blueberry Zucchini Cake with Lemon Buttercream

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert
- Source: The View from Great Island
- Image: /images/blueberry-zucchini-cake-lemon-buttercream.jpg
- Serves: 20

## Description

Moist zucchini and blueberry sheet cake topped with lemon buttercream.

## Cook Times

Prep: 25 min
Bake: 50 min
Cool: 1 h
Total: 2 h 15 min

## Ingredients

### Cake

- 3 large eggs
- 240 ml (1 cup) vegetable oil
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) vanilla extract
- 540 ml (2 1/4 cups) sugar
- 480 ml (2 cups) zucchini, shredded and drained
- 720 ml (3 cups) A/P flour
- 5 ml (1 tsp) salt
- 5 ml (1 tsp) baking powder
- 1.25 ml (1/4 tsp) baking soda
- 470 ml (1 pint) blueberries, tossed in 5 ml (1 tsp) flour

### Buttercream

- 225 g (2 sticks) unsalted butter at room temp
- 960 ml (4 cups, 1 bag) powdered sugar
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) fresh lemon juice

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)
2. Grease and flour a 9x13 baking dish

### Directions

1. Cake
   - Beat eggs, oil, vanilla, sugar in large bowl until combined
   - Fold zucchini into mix
   - Whisk flour, salt, baking powder & soda in another bowl
   - Add dry mix to wet mix and stir enough to combine
   - Fold in the blueberries and pour into baking dish
   - Bake for 50 minutes until just golden brown and a toothpick comes out clean
   - Remove and let cool on a rack
2. Frosting
   - Cream the softened butter in food processor or stand mixer
   - Add 480 ml (2 cups) sifted sugar and combine until smooth
   - Add lemon juice and remaining sugar until smooth
   - Add small amounts of water if the frosting is too stiff
3. Frost the cooled cake and store in the fridge
