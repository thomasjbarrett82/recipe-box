# Bourbon Crisp

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert
- Image: /images/bourbon-crisp.jpg
- Serves: 6

## Description

Peaches and blueberries with lemon and bourbon, baked under a granola and brown sugar topping.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Bake: 30–40 min
Total: 1 h

## Ingredients

### Fruit Mixture

- 3 ripe peaches — pitted, peeled, and sliced
- 240 ml (1 cup) fresh blueberries, washed
- ½ lemon, juiced
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) Kentucky bourbon
- 5 ml (1 tsp) lemon zest
- 45 ml (3 tbsp) brown sugar
- 5 ml (1 tsp) ground cinnamon
- 5 ml (1 tsp) ground nutmeg

### Topping

- 240 ml (1 cup) granola
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) brown sugar
- 1 pinch kosher salt
- 60 ml (4 tbsp) cold butter, cubed

## Method

### Prep

1. Preheat oven to 190 °C (375 °F).

### Directions

1. Toss the fruit mixture together into a greased cooking pan until evenly combined.
2. Next, add the topping ingredients in a mixing bowl, cutting the butter into the dry ingredients until you get a course crumble.
3. Spread the topping over the top of the fruit mixture and bake for 30-40 minutes or until browned and crispy.
4. Cool slightly before serving. Serve with vanilla ice cream if desired.
