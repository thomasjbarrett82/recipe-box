# Bourbon Glazed Steak Tips

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main
- Image: /images/bourbon-glazed-steak-tips.jpg
- Serves: 6

## Description

Steak tips marinated in Worcestershire, soy, balsamic, honey and bourbon, then seared and glazed.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Marinate: 4 h
Cook: 10 min
Total: 4 h 25 min

## Ingredients

- 905 g–1.4 kg (2–3 lb) Top Blade Roast, Flat Iron or Sirloin
- 60 ml (¼ cup) Worcestershire sauce
- 60 ml (¼ cup) soy sauce
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) balsamic vinegar
- 60 ml (¼ cup) plus 30 ml (2 tbsp) honey
- 120 ml (½ cup) plus 30 ml (2 tbsp) bourbon
- Spray oil
- Salt to taste

## Method

### Prep

1. Cut beef into 1-inch cubes.
2. If using Top Blade Roast, make sure to cut away and discard the thick membrane that runs through the middle of the roast.
3. Combine Worcestershire sauce, soy sauce, balsamic vinegar, 60 ml (¼ cup) honey, and 120 ml (½ cup) bourbon in a bowl or zip top bag.
4. Add the steak tips and refrigerate overnight, or at least 4 hours.

### Directions

1. Heat a heavy cast iron skillet to medium-high heat.
2. Spray with a little oil.
3. Drain the excess marinade from steak tips and discard.
4. Place steak tips in the hot skillet. Cook 5-7 minutes until color develops.
5. Mix remaining bourbon and honey in a small bowl
6. Pour over steak tips to glaze.
7. Continue cooking until glaze has caramelized and darkened to a rich brown
8. The tips should have enough salt from the soy sauce, but you can taste them at this stage and add more salt if needed.
9. Remove from heat, allowing 2-3 minutes to cool, then serve.
