# Breakfast Cookies

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Breakfast
- Source: Tara
- Image: /images/breakfast-cookies.jpg
- Emoji: 🍪
- Serves: 30

## Description

Crisp cookies made with oatmeal and Rice Krispies.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Bake: 20 min
Total: 40 min

## Ingredients

- 2 eggs
- 240 ml (1 cup) sugar (240 g), plus extra
- 240 ml (1 cup) brown sugar (240 g)
- 840 ml (3 ½ cups) flour
- 5 ml (1 tsp) baking powder
- 5 ml (1 tsp) baking soda
- 5 ml (1 tsp) vanilla extract
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) salt
- 240 ml (1 cup) oatmeal
- 240 ml (1 cup) Rice Krispies
- 240 ml (1 cup) shortening
- 240 ml (1 cup) butter, melted

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)
2. Set out two small plates with water and sugar

### Directions

1. Mix all ingredients in a large bowl
2. Dish out dough into balls on cookie sheet with parchment paper
3. Dip a glass into water and then sugar and press down on each cookie
4. Bake for 5-7 minutes until golden
5. Remove and cool before serving
