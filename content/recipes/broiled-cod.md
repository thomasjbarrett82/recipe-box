# Broiled Cod

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Quick
- Source: Tara
- Image: /images/broiled-cod.jpg
- Emoji: 🐟
- Serves: 4

## Description

Cod fillets broiled under a lemony mayo, shallot and thyme topping.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Broil: 5–7 min
Total: 20 min

## Ingredients

- 4 fillets cod (or other white fish)
- EVOO
- 120 ml (½ cup) mayo
- 10 ml (2 tsp) lemon zest
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) lemon juice
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) minced shallots
- 7.5 ml (1 ½ tsp) anchovy paste
- 10 ml (2 tsp) fresh thyme

## Method

### Prep

1. Preheat the broiler

### Directions

1. Lay the fillets on a pan and brush with EVOO on both sides
2. Lay the fillets skin side down
3. Mix the remaining ingredients
4. Spread the sauce over the fish
5. Broil until just cooked through, 3-7 minutes, depending on thickness
