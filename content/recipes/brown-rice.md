# Brown Rice

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Side, Instant Pot, Vegetarian, Vegan
- Serves: 4

## Description

Simple brown rice, cooked on the stovetop or in the Instant Pot.

## Cook Times

Prep: 5 min
Cook: 40 min
Total: 45 min

## Ingredients

- 240 ml (1 cup) brown rice
- 960 ml (4 cups) water (stovetop) OR 260 ml (1.1 cups) water (Instant Pot)
- 5 ml (1 tsp) salt

## Method

1. Stovetop:
   - Bring water in a pot to a boil
   - Add rice and salt
   - Keep at low boil with the lid half on
   - Cook for 30 minutes
   - Drain any remaining water
   - Cover and leave off heat for 5-10 minutes
2. Instant Pot
   - Cook on high/porridge for 15-20 minutes
   - Natural release for 5 minutes
3. Fluff with fork before serving
