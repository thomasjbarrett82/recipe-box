# Brown Sugar Glazed Carrots

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Side, Vegetarian, Favorite
- Source: Tom
- Image: /images/brown-sugar-glazed-carrots.jpg
- Serves: 6

## Description

Carrots roasted with butter, brown sugar and garlic salt until glazed.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Roast: 25 min
Total: 35 min

## Ingredients

- 905 g (2 lb) carrots
- 60 ml (1/4 cup) brown sugar
- 5 ml (1 tsp) garlic salt
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) butter, melted
- 1.25 ml (1/4 tsp) black pepper

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat air fryer or roaster oven to 205 °C (400 °F)

### Directions

1. Cut carrots to similar size pieces
2. Toss with remaining ingredients in bowl
3. Spread on greased pan
4. Roast 15 mins
5. Toss and roast another 10 mins
6. Toss to cover with glaze and serve warm
