# Brown Sugar Whipped Cream

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert, Quick
- Image: /images/brown-sugar-whipped-cream.jpg
- Serves: 6

## Description

Whipped cream sweetened with brown sugar and flavored with vanilla bean.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Total: 10 min

## Ingredients

- 180 ml (¾ cup) heavy cream (170 g / 6 oz)
- 60 ml (¼ cup) light brown sugar (55 g / 2 oz)
- 0.6 ml (1/8 tsp) kosher salt
- Seeds from 1 vanilla bean

## Method

1. Whisk the ingredients on high until fluffy & thick, about 5 minutes
