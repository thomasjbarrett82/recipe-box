# Buns

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Bread
- Serves: 12

## Description

Soft homemade yeast buns for burgers and sandwiches.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Rise: 1 h 30 min
Bake: 12–15 min
Total: 2 h 5 min

## Ingredients

- 240 ml (1 cup) water
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) butter
- 1 egg
- 425 g white bread flour + 120 ml (½ cup) flour
- 60 g sugar
- 1.25 ml (¼ tsp) kosher salt
- 7 g (¼ oz) active yeast

## Method

### Prep

1. Place ingredients in bread maker on dough cycle
2. Grease a large baking sheet

### Directions

1. After dough cycle completes, move to bowl with 120 ml (½ cup) flour
2. Remove “pinches” of dough and place on baking sheet
3. Cover & place in warm place until doubled in size
4. Heat oven to 190 °C (375 °F)
5. Bake for 12-15 minutes until golden
6. Brush butter over tops while still warm
