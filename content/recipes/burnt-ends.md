# Burnt Ends

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Grill, Smoker
- Source: Hey Grill Hey
- Image: /images/burnt-ends.jpg
- Emoji: 🔥
- Serves: 7

## Description

Smoked brisket point, cubed and tossed with BBQ sauce and brown sugar, then smoked again until caramelized.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Dry brine: 12–24 h
Smoke: 9–16 h
Total: 21 h 30 min

## Ingredients

- 1.4–2.7 kg (3–6 lb) brisket point, trimmed from whole
- 60 ml (4 tbsp) spice rub (15 ml (1 tbsp) each: salt, pepper, garlic powder, smoked paprika)
- 240 ml (1 cup) beef stock
- charcoal and wood chunks
- disposable aluminum pan
- 240 ml (1 cup) BBQ sauce
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) brown sugar

## Method

### Prep

1. Season brisket with rub and refrigerate for 12-24 hours
2. Heat smoker to 107 °C (225 °F) using snake method

### Directions

1. Position thermometer in middle of brisket
2. Add brisket to grate over pan, fat side up
3. Cover and adjust until temp is 107–135 °C (225–275 °F)
4. Smoke until brisket reaches 71–77 °C (160–170 °F)
   - 6-10 hours
   - Spritz with beef stock every hour
5. Remove brisket and wrap with single parchment and double foil
6. Return to grill, fat side up, and continue until 91–93 °C (195–200 °F)
   - 2-4 hours
   - Alternately, move to 107 °C (225 °F) oven to finish cooking
7. Remove brisket to cutting board
8. Unwrap and drain any liquid into pan
9. Cut into 1.5 inch cubes
10. Move cubes and any juice to pan and toss with BBQ sauce & sugar
11. Move pan back to smoker
12. Continue at 107 °C (225 °F) for 1-2 hours until BBQ starts caramelizing
13. Remove and let rest for 10 minutes before serving
