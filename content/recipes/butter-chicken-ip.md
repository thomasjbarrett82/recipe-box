# Butter Chicken (Instant Pot)

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Instant Pot
- Source: Two Sleevers
- Serves: 4

## Description

Chicken thighs pressure-cooked in a spiced tomato sauce, finished with butter, cream and garam masala, with broccoli.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Cook: 35 min
Total: 45 min

## Ingredients

- 15 + 15 ml (1 + 1 tbsp) EVOO
- 455 g (1 lb) boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- 395 g (14 oz) can diced tomatoes
- 4-5 cloves garlic, sliced
- 10 ml (2 tsp) ginger, minced
- 5 ml (1 tsp) turmeric
- 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) cayenne pepper
- 5 ml (1 tsp) smoked paprika
- 5 ml (1 tsp) kosher salt
- 5 ml (1 tsp) garam masala
- 5 ml (1 tsp) cumin
- 60 ml (1/4 cup) butter, cubed
- 60 ml (1/4 cup) heavy cream or Greek yogurt
- 5 ml (1 tsp) garam masala
- 1 bag frozen broccoli, thawed

## Method

1. Heat IP on saute
2. Add 15 ml (1 tbsp) EVOO and brown the chicken
3. Remove chicken and set to the side
4. Add 15 ml (1 tbsp) EVOO and spices, cook until fragrant
5. Stir in tomatoes and put chicken thighs on top
6. Close lid and cook on high pressure for 10 mins
7. Natural release for 10 mins before opening
8. Remove chicken and set aside
9. Add butter, cream, garam masala to sauce
10. Blend sauce with an immersion blender
11. Rough chop chicken and add it back to the sauce
12. Add broccoli and let sit for a few mins to warm up
13. Serve over rice or veggies
