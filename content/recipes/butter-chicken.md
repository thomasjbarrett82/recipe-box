# Butter Chicken

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main
- Source: Two Sleevers
- Serves: 4

## Description

Chicken thighs simmered in a spiced tomato sauce, finished with butter, cream and garam masala, with broccoli.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Cook: 40 min
Total: 50 min

## Ingredients

- 15 + 15 ml (1 + 1 tbsp) EVOO
- 455 g (1 lb) boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- 395 g (14 oz) can diced tomatoes
- 4-5 cloves garlic, sliced
- 10 ml (2 tsp) ginger, minced
- 5 ml (1 tsp) turmeric
- 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) cayenne pepper
- 5 ml (1 tsp) smoked paprika
- 5 ml (1 tsp) kosher salt
- 5 ml (1 tsp) garam masala
- 5 ml (1 tsp) cumin
- 60 ml (1/4 cup) butter, cubed
- 60 ml (1/4 cup) heavy cream or Greek yogurt
- 5 ml (1 tsp) garam masala
- 1 bag frozen broccoli, thawed

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat large pot over medium heat

### Directions

1. Add 15 ml (1 tbsp) EVOO and brown chicken in pot
2. Remove the chicken to a plate for the next step
3. Add 15 ml (1 tbsp) EVOO and spices to the pot
4. Cook for 1 minute until fragrant
5. Add tomatoes to the pot
6. Add the chicken back to the pot on top of the tomatoes
7. Turn down heat to low-medium and cover
8. Cook for 20-30 minutes until chicken is cooked through
9. Remove chicken and set aside to rest
10. Add butter, cream, garam masala to sauce
11. Blend sauce with an immersion blender, or mash it
12. Rough chop the chicken and add it back to the sauce
13. Add broccoli and let sit for a few mins to warm up
14. Serve over rice or veggies
