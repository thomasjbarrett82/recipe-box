# Butternut Squash & Chickpea Curry

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Vegetarian, Vegan, High fiber
- Source: The Hidden Veggies
- Image: /images/butternut-squash-chickpea-curry.jpg
- Emoji: 🍛
- Serves: 8

## Description

Roasted butternut squash, chickpeas and red lentils in a curry-spiced tomato sauce.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Cook: 50 min
Total: 1 h 10 min

## Ingredients

- 720–960 ml (3–4 cups) butternut squash, peeled & cubed
- EVOO
- 1 yellow onion, minced
- 5 ml (1 tsp) kosher salt
- 5 cloves garlic, minced
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) fresh ginger, peeled and grated
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) curry powder
- 5 ml (1 tsp) garam masala (optional)
- 5 ml (1 tsp) cumin
- 5 ml (1 tsp) turmeric
- 5 ml (1 tsp) red pepper flakes (optional)
- 360 ml (1.5 cups) red lentils
- 425 g (15 oz) can chickpeas, drained
- 425 g (15 oz) can diced tomatoes
- 720 ml (3 cups) vegetable broth
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) water
- 395 g (14 oz) can coconut milk
- 285 g (10 oz) bag frozen spinach, thawed and drained
- Basmati rice for serving
- Naan bread for dipping (optional)

## Method

### Prep

1. Cook rice (or other grain) and set aside
2. Heat oven to 205 °C (400 °F)
3. Heat large pot over med heat

### Directions

1. Toss squash with some EVOO and spread on baking sheet
2. Sprinkle with salt and bake for 25-35 minutes until golden brown
3. In large pot, heat EVOO and add onions
4. Saute for 5 minutes
5. Add spices and warm in the oil until fragrant
6. Add lentils, tomatoes with juice, chickpeas, broth, and water to pot
7. Bring to a slow boil and then cover and simmer for 10 mins
8. Add coconut milk and simmer for 5 more mins
9. Add spinach and squash to the pot and stir well
10. Serve hot
