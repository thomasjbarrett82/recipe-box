# Cabbage Soup

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Soup, Dutch oven, High fiber
- Image: /images/cabbage-soup.jpg
- Emoji: 🥬
- Serves: 10

## Description

Light vegetable soup of cabbage, green beans, peppers and tomatoes in a smoky paprika broth.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Cook: 35 min
Total: 55 min

## Ingredients

- 15 ml (1 tbsp) EVOO or lard
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 onion, minced
- 225 g (½ lb) carrots, sliced
- ½ bunch celery, sliced
- 1 bell pepper, de-seeded & diced
- 225 g (½ lb) green beans, chopped
- 795 g (28 oz) diced tomatoes
- 225 g (8 oz) can tomato sauce OR 115 g (4 oz) can tomato paste
- 1 head cabbage, chopped
- 1.4 L (6 cups) vegetable broth
- 7.5 ml (½ tbsp) smoked paprika
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) Italian seasoning
- 5 ml (1 tsp) kosher salt
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) lemon juice (or apple cider vinegar)
- Salt and pepper, to taste

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat large pot or Dutch oven

### Directions

1. Sauté garlic & onion with oil
2. Add carrots, celery, and bell peppers and cook for 5 minutes
3. Add remaining ingredients, except for lemon juic
4. Bring to a boil and drop to a simmer for 20-30 minutes
5. Add lemon juice
6. Add salt and pepper, to taste
