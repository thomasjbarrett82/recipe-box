# Candied Yams

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Side, Vegetarian, High fiber, Holiday
- Serves: 8

## Description

Yams baked with butter, brown sugar and warm spices, optionally topped with marshmallows.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Bake: 40 min
Total: 55 min

## Ingredients

- 1.4 kg (3 lb) yams, sliced 1/2" thick rounds
- 5 ml (1 tsp) nutmeg
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) cinnamon
- 1.25 ml (1/4 tsp) cloves
- 180 ml (3/4 cup) brown sugar
- 55 g (1/2 stick) butter, melted
- 5 ml (1 tsp) vanilla
- 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) of salt
- 1 bag baby marshmallows (optional)

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)

### Directions

1. Put yams into large pot and cover with water
2. Place pot over medium heat and cook until barely tender, ~5 minutes
3. Drain water and move yams to greased casserole dish
4. In bowl, mix spices, butter, sugar, and vanilla
5. Pour sauce over yams and gently toss
6. Cover with marshmallows
7. Bake uncovered until marshmallows are golden brown and potatoes are tender, 30-40 minutes
