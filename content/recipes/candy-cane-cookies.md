# Candy Cane Cookies

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert, Holiday
- Source: Tara
- Image: /images/candy-cane-cookies.jpg
- Serves: 24

## Description

Twisted red and white almond-vanilla cookies shaped like candy canes.

## Cook Times

Prep: 40 min
Bake: 8–10 min
Total: 1 h

## Ingredients

- 120 ml (½ cup) shortening
- 120 ml (½ cup) butter, softened
- 1 egg
- 7.5 ml (1 ½ tsp) almond extract
- 5 ml (1 tsp) vanilla extract
- 600 ml (2 ½ cups) flour
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) salt
- Red food coloring

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)

### Directions

1. In large bowl, cream together shortening, butter, egg, & extracts
2. Mix in the flour & salt
3. Split dough into half
4. Stir in food coloring into one half
5. Spoon out onto cookie sheet
6. Bake for 8-10 minutes
7. Remove and let cool
