# Caramel Popcorn

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Appetizer, Quick
- Source: Tara
- Image: /images/caramel-popcorn.jpg
- Serves: 14

## Description

Easy caramel corn made in the microwave.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Microwave: 5 min
Total: 15 min

## Ingredients

- 240 ml (1 cup) brown sugar
- 60 ml (¼ cup) butter
- 60 ml (¼ cup) white corn syrup
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) salt
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) baking soda
- 2.8–3.8 L (3–4 quarts) popped corn

## Method

1. In medium bowl, combine sugar, butter, syrup, and salt
2. Microwave and stir, 30 seconds at a time, until boiling
3. Stir in baking soda
4. Place popped corn into large brown bag
5. Pour mixture over popcorn
6. Shake the bag to thoroughly combine
7. Microwave for 1 ½ minutes
8. Shake again to stir up the mixture
9. Microwave for another 1 ½ minutes
10. Pour out onto cookie sheet to cool
