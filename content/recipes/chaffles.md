# Chaffles

- Serves: 3

## Description

Low-carb waffles made from egg and Cheddar in a mini waffle maker.

## Cook Times

Prep: 5 min
Cook: 10 min
Total: 15 min

## Ingredients

- 3 eggs
- 240 ml (1 cup) shredded Cheddar
- 3 scallions, chopped
- For 2 mini-waffles: 1 egg, 60 ml (1/4 cup) cheese, 1 scallion

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat nonstick waffle iron
2. Mix ingredients in bowl

### Directions

1. Lightly grease waffle iron before pouring in "batter"
2. Pour in less batter than you think is needed, the egg will expand
3. Cook for 4-5 minutes until egg is fully cooked
4. Serve with any sweet or savory waffle toppings
