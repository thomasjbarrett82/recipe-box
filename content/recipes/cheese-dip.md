# Cheese Dip

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Appetizer, Slow cooker, Vegetarian
- Image: /images/cheese-dip.jpg
- Emoji: 🧀
- Serves: 24

## Description

Velveeta and Rotel melted together in the crockpot. Two ingredients, always gone.

## Cook Times

Prep: 5 min
Slow cook: 1 h
Total: 1 h 5 min

## Ingredients

- 1 box Velveeta, roughly cubed
- 2 cans diced tomatoes with chilis

## Method

1. Put Velveeta in a small crockpot
2. Turn on HIGH for 1 hour to melt
3. Stir in tomatoes & chilis thoroughly
4. Turn to LOW or WARM for serving
5. Serve with tortilla chips
