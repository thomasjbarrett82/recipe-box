# Cheeseball

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Appetizer, Holiday
- Source: Tara
- Image: /images/cheeseball.jpg
- Serves: 20

## Description

Cream cheese ball with chopped dried beef, Worcestershire and onion powder.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Chill: 1 h
Total: 1 h 15 min

## Ingredients

- 2 packages dried beef
- 2 (225 g / 8 oz) cream cheese
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) Worcestershire
- 5 ml (1 tsp) onion powder
- 5 ml (1 tsp) Accent

## Method

1. Chop dried beef, then split in half
2. Mix ½ of beef with remaining ingredients into a ball
3. Roll the ball in remaining beef to cover
4. Serve with crackers
