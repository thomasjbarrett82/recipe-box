# Cheesecake

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert
- Image: /images/cheesecake.jpg
- Emoji: 🍰
- Serves: 12

## Description

Classic baked cheesecake on a graham cracker crust, served with fruit preserves.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Bake: 50 min
Chill: 4 h
Total: 5 h 10 min

## Ingredients

- 480 ml (2 cups) crushed graham crackers
- 55 g (½ stick) melted butter
- 3 (225 g / 8 oz) bars cream cheese, softened
- 300 ml (1 ¼ cups) sugar
- 480 ml (2 cups) sour cream
- 2 large eggs
- 10 ml (2 tsp) pure vanilla extract
- 240 ml (1 cup) fruit preserves

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat the oven to 175 °C (350 °F)

### Directions

1. Mix the crackers and butter
2. Press the mixture into a 9 inch pie or cheesecake pan
3. Bake for 5 minutes
4. Beat the eggs, sour cream, sugar, cream cheese, and vanilla
5. Pour the mixture over the crust
6. Bake for 45 minutes until it starts to turn golden brown on top
7. Remove and let cool in the fridge for at least 4 hours
8. Spread the preserves over the cheesecake before serving
