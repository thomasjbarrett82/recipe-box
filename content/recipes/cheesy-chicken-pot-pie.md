# Cheesy Chicken Pot Pie

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main
- Serves: 6

## Description

Chicken, vegetables and Cheddar in a creamy sauce, baked under a crescent roll crust.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Cook: 50 min
Total: 1 h 5 min

## Ingredients

- 15 ml (1 tbsp) oil
- 120 ml (½ cup) chopped onion
- 120 ml (½ cup) chopped celery
- 240 ml (1 cup) carrots, sliced
- 240 ml (1 cup) green beans
- 480 ml (2 cups) chicken, cooked and chopped
- 1 can cream of chicken soup
- 1 can chicken gravy
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) sage
- 240 ml (1 cup) shredded Cheddar
- 1 tube pre-made crescent rolls

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat large pan over medium heat
2. Heat oven to 190 °C (375 °F)

### Directions

1. Add oil, onion, and celery to pan
2. Cook for 3-5 minutes
3. Stir in carrots, green beans, chicken, soup, gravy, & sage
4. Cook until bubbly
5. Stir in ¾ of the cheese
6. Spoon into greased, deep 1.9 L (2 quarts) casserole dish
7. Place crescent rolls over dish to cover
8. Bake for 40 minutes
9. Sprinkle remaining cheese over crust
10. Bake 5 minutes until cheese is melted
