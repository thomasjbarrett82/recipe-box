# Cherry Pie

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert
- Image: /images/cherry-pie.jpg
- Serves: 8

## Description

Double-crust cherry pie thickened with tapioca starch.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Chill: 30 min
Bake: 65 min
Total: 4 h 55 min

## Ingredients

- 795 g (28 oz) pitted cherries (fresh or frozen)
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) lemon juice
- 240 ml (1 cup) sugar
- 5 ml (1 tsp) kosher salt
- 80 ml (1/3 cup) tapioca starch

## Method

### Prep

1. Prep the pie dough for a 2-crust pie
2. Pre-heat oven to 205 °C (400 °F)

### Directions

1. Whisk the sugar, salt, and starch together
2. Whisk in the lemon juice
3. Mix with the cherries until fully combined
4. Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes
5. Pour into prepared pie crust
6. Cover with second crust
7. Place pie on a parchment-lined baking sheet
8. Bake for 50 minutes
9. Tent with foil
10. Bake for 15 minutes
11. Remove and let cool for ~3 hours
12. Serve with whipped cream or vanilla ice cream
