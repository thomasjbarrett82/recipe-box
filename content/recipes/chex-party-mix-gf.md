# Chex Party Mix (GF)

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Appetizer, Quick, Gluten free
- Image: /images/chex-party-mix-gf.jpg
- Serves: 10

## Description

Gluten-free party mix of Corn Chex, Rice Chex, Crispix, pretzels and popcorn in seasoned butter.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Microwave: 5 min
Total: 15 min

## Ingredients

- 480 ml (2 cups) Corn Chex
- 480 ml (2 cups) Rice Chex
- 480 ml (2 cups) Crispix
- 480 ml (2 cups) Glutino pretzel twists (or other gluten-free variety)
- 90 ml (6 tbsp) butter (or substitute)
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) Worcestershire
- 22.5 ml (1 ½ tbsp) Lowry’s
- 3.75 ml (¾ tsp) garlic powder
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) onion powder
- 480 ml (2 cups) Act II Butter Lover’s popcorn, popped

## Method

1. In large bowl, mix together cereals and pretzels
2. In small bowl, melt butter and pour over mixture
3. Stir in seasonings until evenly coated
4. Microwave for 3 minutes, 1 minute at a time, stirring
5. Lay out on paper towels to cool
6. Mix in popcorn when cooled and serve in large bowl
