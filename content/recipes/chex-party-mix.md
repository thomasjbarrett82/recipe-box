# Chex Party Mix

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Appetizer, Quick, Holiday
- Source: Tara
- Image: /images/chex-party-mix.jpg
- Serves: 12

## Description

Classic microwave Chex mix with nuts, pretzels and bagel chips in seasoned butter.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Microwave: 10 min
Total: 20 min

## Ingredients

- 720 ml (3 cups) Corn Chex
- 720 ml (3 cups) Rice Chex
- 720 ml (3 cups) Wheat Chex
- 240 ml (1 cup) mixed nuts
- 240 ml (1 cup) mini pretzels
- 240 ml (1 cup) bite-size bagel chips
- 90 ml (6 tbsp) butter or margarine, melted
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) Worcestershire
- 7.5 ml (1 ½ tsp) seasoned salt
- 3.75 ml (¾ tsp) garlic powder
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) onion powder

## Method

1. Mix all ingredients together in a large microwavable bowl
2. Microwave on high 5-6 minutes, stirring every minute
3. Spread on paper towels to cool before serving
