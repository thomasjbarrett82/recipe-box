# Chicken & Biscuits

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Favorite
- Image: /images/chicken-biscuits.jpg
- Emoji: 🍗
- Serves: 6

## Description

Creamy chicken and vegetable filling topped with buttermilk biscuits and baked.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Cook: 40 min
Total: 55 min

## Ingredients

- 30 ml (2 tbsp) EVOO
- 905 g (2 lb) chicken breast or thigh
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) butter
- 1 onion, diced
- 60 ml (¼ cup) flour
- 480 ml (2 cups) chicken broth
- 60 ml (¼ cup) cream
- 1 package frozen mixed vegetables
- Salt & pepper
- 1 package buttermilk biscuits, quartered

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat EVOO in large cast iron skillet over med-high heat
2. Heat oven to 205 °C (400 °F)

### Directions

1. Cook chicken in skillet until browned
2. Remove and set aside
3. Shred chicken after it cools
4. Melt butter in skillet
5. Cook onions for 3-4 minutes until tender
6. In small bowl, mix flour, broth, & cream
7. Stir into skillet
8. Bring to a boil, stirring constantly
9. Cook until thickened, 1-2 minutes
10. Add chicken and vegetables and heat through
11. Arrange quartered biscuits evenly over the top
12. Move to oven and bake until biscuits are golden brown
