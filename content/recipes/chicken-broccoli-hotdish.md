# Chicken Broccoli Hotdish

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Minnesota, Favorite
- Image: /images/chicken-broccoli-hotdish.jpg
- Emoji: 🥦
- Serves: 6

## Description

Chicken, broccoli and rice baked in a creamy, cheesy sauce.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Bake: 1 h
Total: 1 h 20 min

## Ingredients

- 455 g (1 lb) chicken breast or thighs, cooked and chopped
- 240 ml (1 cup) Cheddar, shredded
- 1 package frozen broccoli
- 480 ml (2 cups) rice, cooked
- 1 can cream of celery
- 1 can cream of mushroom
- 240 ml (1 cup) milk
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1 small onion, minced
- Salt & pepper

## Method

### Prep

1. Cook and chop chicken
2. Cook rice
3. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)

### Directions

1. Combine everything in a greased casserole dish
2. Cook uncovered for 1 hour
3. Let cool at least 5 minutes before serving
