# Chicken & Broccoli Stir Fry

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main
- Image: /images/chicken-broccoli-stir-fry.jpg
- Serves: 4

## Description

Chicken and broccoli in a light soy and ginger sauce, served over rice.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Cook: 15 min
Total: 35 min

## Ingredients

- 455 g (1 lb) boneless, skinless chicken breast, sliced thin
- 1 ½ + 7.5 ml (½ tbsp) cornstarch
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) dry sherry
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) kosher salt
- 1.25 ml (¼ tsp) black pepper
- 60 ml (4 tbsp) low-sodium soy sauce
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) chicken broth
- 10 ml (2 tsp) sugar or other sweetener
- 1.25 ml (¼ tsp) ginger powder
- Dash of crushed red pepper
- 340 g (12 oz) steamed broccoli florets
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) oil
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 720 ml (3 cups) cooked rice

## Method

### Prep

1. In a wide bowl, combine 22.5 ml (1 ½ tbsp) cornstarch, sherry, salt, & pepper for dredging the chicken.
2. In a small bowl, whisk 7.5 ml (½ tbsp) cornstarch, soy sauce, broth, sugar, ginger, red pepper, and set aside.
3. Heat wok or large heavy skillet over medium-high heat.

### Directions

1. Dredge the chicken strips in the mixture and set aside.
2. Heat oil in the pan.
3. Add the chicken and stir-fry until just done.
4. Add garlic, broccoli, and sauce to pan.
5. Stir-fry until broccoli is heated through.
6. Serve over rice.
