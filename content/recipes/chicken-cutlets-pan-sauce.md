# Chicken Cutlets with Pan Sauce

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Quick
- Serves: 4

## Description

Thin chicken cutlets, pan-fried and finished with a white wine, lemon and parsley pan sauce.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Cook: 15 min
Total: 30 min

## Ingredients

- 240 ml (1 cup) a/p flour
- 680 g (1.5 lb) boneless, skinless chicken
- Kosher salt & black pepper
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) EVOO
- 45 ml (3 tbsp) + 15 ml (1 tbsp) butter
- 120 ml (½ cup) white wine
- 120 ml (½ cup) chicken stock
- 120 ml (½ cup) fresh parsley, chopped
- 5 ml (1 tsp) lemon juice

## Method

### Prep

1. Preheat oven to 93 °C (200 °F) with a large platter or sheet inside

### Directions

1. Put the flour in a shallow bowl next to the stove
2. Pound the cutlets flat between 2 sheets of plastic wrap
3. Blot dry and sprinkle with salt & pepper
4. Heat a heavy skillet over med-high heat and add EVOO and 45 ml (3 tbsp) butter
5. Working one at a time, coat the chicken in flour and add to the skillet
6. Work in batches to avoid overcrowding
7. Flip after 3-4 minutes when browned
8. Transfer to oven after 3-4 more minutes
9. Deglaze the skillet with the wine
10. Add stock and cook until reduced to 60 ml (¼ cup)
11. Add 15 ml (1 tbsp) butter, parsley, & lemon juice and turn off the heat
12. If any juices accumulated on the platter, add to the sauce
13. Season sauce to taste and serve over chicken
