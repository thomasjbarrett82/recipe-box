# Chicken & Dumplings

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Soup
- Image: /images/chicken-dumplings.jpg
- Emoji: 🥟
- Serves: 6

## Description

A whole stewing chicken simmered into a rich broth, then topped with fluffy parsley dumplings.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Cook: 2 h 45 min
Total: 3 h 5 min

## Ingredients

- 1 whole stewing chicken, cut up
- 3 -4 sprigs parsley
- 4 celery stalk, chopped
- 1 carrot, diced
- 120 ml (½ cup) onion, diced
- 10 ml (2 tsp) salt
- 1.25 ml (¼ tsp) pepper
- Cold water

### Dumplings

- 360 ml (1 ½ cups) flour
- 15 ml (3 tsp) baking powder
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) salt
- 180 ml (¾ cup) milk
- 5 ml (1 tsp) parsley, minced

## Method

1. Add chicken, veggies, and seasoning to large pot
2. Barely cover with cold water, heat to boiling, skimming
3. Once clear, cover and simmer until fork tender, about 2 ½ hours
4. Remove chicken and shred, then return to pot
5. In bowl, combine flour, baking powder, salt, and parsley
6. Add milk and stir until just moistened
7. Drop batter by teaspoon into pot
8. Replace cover and boil gently for 12 minutes
