# Chicken Enchiladas (Instant Pot)

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Spicy, Instant Pot
- Image: /images/chicken-enchiladas-ip.jpg
- Emoji: 🌯
- Serves: 7

## Description

Chicken cooked in a chipotle tomato sauce in the Instant Pot, then rolled into enchiladas and baked.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Cook: 55 min
Total: 1 h 15 min

## Ingredients

- 15 ml (1 tbsp) EVOO
- 905 g (2 lb) chicken breast or thighs, skinless
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) chili powder
- 7.5 ml (½ tbsp) oregano
- 7.5 ml (½ tbsp) cumin
- 1 jalapeno pepper, minced
- 425 g (15 oz) diced tomatoes
- 1 onion, minced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 chipotle chilies + 15 ml (1 tbsp) adobo sauce
- 240 ml (1 cup) chicken stock
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) lime juice
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) soy sauce
- Salt and pepper
- 6-8 large tortilla shells
- 115 g (4 oz) Cheddar, shredded
- 115 g (4 oz) cotija or Mozzarella, shredded

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 190 °C (375 °F)

### Directions

1. Combine EVOO, chicken, spices, pepper, tomatoes, onion, garlic, chilies, adobo, and stock in Instant Pot
2. Seal and cook on high pressure for 15 minutes
3. Quick-release the pressure
4. Transfer chicken to a large bowl and let cool
5. Add the lime juice and soy sauce to the enchilada sauce
6. Blend until smooth
7. Season with salt & pepper
8. Shred the cooled chicken in the bowl, discarding bones if needed
9. Stir in 120 ml (½ cup) of sauce
10. Assemble enchiladas by scooping meat mix into tortillas and placing in the casserole dish seam-side down, then layer cheese and sauce over the top
11. Bake for 25 minutes
12. Remove and let cool 5 minutes before serving
