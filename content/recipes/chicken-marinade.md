# Chicken Marinade

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main
- Serves: 6

## Description

An all-purpose balsamic, soy and Dijon marinade for chicken breasts.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Marinate: 4–24 h
Total: 4 h 10 min

## Ingredients

- 120 ml (½ cup) EVOO
- 120 ml (½ cup) balsamic or apple cider vinegar
- 60 ml (¼ cup) soy sauce
- 60 ml (¼ cup) Worcestershire sauce
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) lemon juice
- 180 ml (¾ cup) brown sugar
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) Italian seasoning
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) Dijon mustard
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) kosher salt
- 5 ml (1 tsp) black pepper
- 10 ml (2 tsp) garlic powder
- 5 ml (1 tsp) onion powder
- 6 chicken breasts (1.4–1.8 kg / 3–4 lb)

## Method

### Prep

1. Combine everything except chicken and whisk together
   - Optional: remove 120 ml (½ cup) of marinade for basting later
2. Place chicken in large Ziploc bag and pour marinade over top
3. Remove air and close securely
4. Marinate for 4-24 hours before cooking
