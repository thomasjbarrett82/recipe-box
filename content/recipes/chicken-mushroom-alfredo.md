# Chicken & Mushroom Alfredo

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Pasta
- Serves: 6

## Description

Chicken and mushrooms in a garlicky Parmesan cream sauce over fettuccine.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Cook: 25 min
Total: 35 min

## Ingredients

- 455 g (1 lb) boneless, skinless chicken breast
- 455 g (1 lb) sliced crimini mushrooms
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 + 30 ml (2 tbsp) olive oil
- 115 g (1 stick) butter
- 240 ml (1 cup) heavy cream
- 480 ml (2 cups) shredded Parmesan
- 1.25 ml (¼ tsp) cayenne pepper
- 5 ml (1 tsp) parsley
- 455 g (1 lb) fettuccine, cooked

## Method

### Prep

1. Place large pan over med-high

### Directions

1. Heat 30 ml (2 tbsp) oil in pan
2. Season chicken with salt & pepper and place in pan
3. Cook 5 minutes on each side until no longer pink
4. Remove (leaving drippings) and let cool before chopping
5. Add 30 ml (2 tbsp) oil to pan
6. Cook mushrooms and garlic for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally
7. Season liberally with salt & pepper
8. Remove from pan and add to chicken
9. Melt butter in pa
10. Add cream and bring to a slow boil over medium heat
11. Add small handfuls of Parmesan to sauce, stirring constantly and waiting for cheese to melt before adding more
12. Pour over pasta
13. Add chicken mixture to pasta and stir to combine
