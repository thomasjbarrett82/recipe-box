# Chicken & Mushroom Stew

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Stew, Slow cooker
- Image: /images/chicken-mushroom-stew.jpg
- Emoji: 🍄
- Serves: 4

## Description

Chicken, mushrooms, carrots, celery and peas slow-cooked in broth with onion soup mix.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Slow cook: 6–7 h
Total: 6 h 15 min

## Ingredients

- 455 g (1 lb) chicken breast
- 5 ml (1 tsp) olive oil
- 225 g (8 oz) baby bella mushrooms
- 1 onion, chopped
- 240 ml (1 cup) carrots, chopped
- 240 ml (1 cup) peas
- 3 stalks celery, chopped
- 1 packet dry onion soup mix
- 480 ml (2 cups) fat-free chicken broth
- 3 cloves garlic
- 5 ml (1 tsp) salt
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) black pepper

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat a medium pan over medium-high heat

### Directions

1. Heat oil in pan
2. Sautee chicken on both sides until browned, remove and cube
3. Add chicken and remaining ingredients to crockpot
4. Cover and cook on LOW for 6-7 hours
