# Chicken Noodle Soup

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Soup
- Source: Adam Ragusea
- Image: /images/chicken-noodle-soup.jpg
- Serves: 10

## Description

Homemade chicken noodle soup started from a whole chicken, with vegetables, turmeric and noodles or rice.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Cook: 2 h 30 min
Total: 2 h 50 min

## Ingredients

- 1 whole chicken, 1.4–1.8 kg (3–4 lb)
- Water
- Whole old aromatics (onion, carrot, celery, etc.)
- 1.4–1.8 kg (3–4 lb) vegetables, rough chopped (garlic, onions, carrots, zucchini, celery, etc.)
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) kosher salt
- 5 ml (1 tsp) turmeric
- Optional: 225 g (1/2 lb) dry noodles or 120 ml (1/2 cup) dry rice
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) rice vinegar
- Fresh parsley, chopped

## Method

1. Put whole chicken in large pot, along with organs and any old aromatic vegetables
2. Cover with water and lid, and bring to a boil
3. Simmer 1-2 hours until chicken is fall-apart tender
4. Skim off any foam
5. Pull out meat and place on plate to cool slightly (any veggies go in compost)
6. Chop and put all veggies & spices into the pot and return to simmer
7. Hand pull apart the meat and throw away any bones and cartilage
8. Rough chop the meat and return to the pot
9. Add noodles/rice and simmer for 30-45 mins until veggies are soft
10. Add vinegar and parsley
11. Season with salt & pepper to taste
