# Chicken Satay with Nut Sauce

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Grill
- Source: Anil
- Image: /images/chicken-satay-nut-sauce.jpg
- Emoji: 🍢
- Serves: 6

## Description

Chicken marinated in coconut milk, curry and turmeric, grilled on skewers and served with a peanut or nut butter sauce.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Marinate: 2–24 h
Cook: 15 min
Total: 2 h 35 min

## Ingredients

- 60 ml (1/4 cup) coconut milk
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) soy sauce
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) curry powder
- 5 ml (1 tsp) turmeric
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) ginger, grated
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) brown sugar
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) fish sauce
- 5 ml (1 tsp) kosher salt
- 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) black pepper
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) EVOO
- 905 g (2 lb) boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cubed

### Sauce

- 45 ml (3 tbsp) nut butter
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) soy sauce
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) lime juice
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) brown sugar
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) chili garlic sauce
- 5 ml (1 tsp) ginger, grated
- Water to change sauce consistency, if needed

## Method

### Prep

1. Mix together marinade ingredients in large Ziploc bag
2. Add chicken, toss to cover, and refrigerate 2-24 hours
3. Whisk sauce ingredients together right before cooking

### Directions

1. Heat large pan or grill to med-high heat
2. Discard extra marinade
3. Cook chicken until browned and 74 °C (165 °F) internal temp
4. Serve immediately with sauce
