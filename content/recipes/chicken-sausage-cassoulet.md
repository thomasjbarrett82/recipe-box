# Chicken & Sausage Cassoulet

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Stew, Slow cooker, High fiber
- Image: /images/chicken-sausage-cassoulet.jpg
- Serves: 5

## Description

Slow-cooked white beans, chicken and ring sausage in a tomato and herb broth.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Slow cook: 5–10 h
Total: 5 h 15 min

## Ingredients

- 425 g (15 oz) can northern beans
- 1 can tomato juice
- 2 carrots, peeled and chopped
- 2 stalks celery, chopped
- ½ yellow onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 5 ml (1 tsp) instant chicken bouillon granules
- 5 ml (1 tsp) ground basil
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) ground oregano
- 455 g (1 lb) chicken, skin removed
- 455 g (1 lb) ring sausage, sliced

## Method

1. In crockpot, combine beans, juice, carrots, celery, onion, garlic, granules, basil, and oregano
2. Place chicken and sausage on top of mixture
3. Cover, cook on LOW 10 hours or HIGH 5 hours until chicken is fork-tender
