# Chicken & Sausage Gumbo

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Stew, Spicy
- Serves: 8

## Description

Chicken and andouille gumbo built on an oven-baked roux, with peppers, celery and okra.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Cook: 2 h 15 min
Total: 2 h 35 min

## Ingredients

- 455 g (1 lb) Andouille or Italian sausage
- 455 g (1 lb) chicken, chopped
- 60 ml (4 tbsp) olive oil
- 60 ml (4 tbsp) flour
- 1.9 L (8 cups) chicken broth/stock
- 1 green bell pepper, diced
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 3 stalks celery, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 can diced tomatoes
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) kosher salt
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) black pepper
- 5 ml (1 tsp) thyme
- 5 ml (1 tsp) basil
- 5 ml (1 tsp) oregano
- 1.25 ml (¼ tsp) cayenne pepper
- 455 g (1 lb) shrimp, peeled, deveined, tails removed
- 1 package sliced okra (thawed, if frozen)

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)
2. Heat oven-safe pan or large pot over med-high

### Directions

1. Brown sausage in pan or pot, remove with slotted spoon and set aside
2. Brown chicken in pan or pot, remove with slotted spoon and set aside
3. Add oil and flour to pan and whisk together until smooth
4. Place in oven for 1 – 1 ½ hours, stirring occasionally, until roux has taken on a brown color
5. Once roux is desired color, remove from oven and place over medium heat
   - Move to large pot if not already
6. Add onions, celery, peppers, and garlic and cook for 5 minutes
7. Add remaining ingredients except shrimp and okra
8. Bring to a boil and simmer for 30 minutes
9. Add shrimp and okra and cook for 10 minutes until shrimp is done
10. Serve over rice
