# Chicken Shawarma

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Instant Pot
- Serves: 8

## Description

Lemony, spice-rubbed chicken thighs with red onion, cooked in the Instant Pot and served with cucumber salad.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Marinate: 1 h
Cook: 25 min
Total: 1 h 45 min

## Ingredients

### Chicken

- 120 ml (½ cup) EVOO
- 60 ml (¼ cup) lemon juice
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) smoked paprika
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) turmeric
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) cumin
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) cinnamon
- 5 ml (1 tsp) red pepper flakes
- 5 cloves garlic, minced
- 10 ml (2 tsp) black pepper
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) kosher salt
- 1 small red onion, diced
- 1.8 kg (4 lb) chicken thighs, boneless and skinless

### Greek Salad

- 1 English cucumber, cubed
- 1 small red onion, diced
- 1 green bell pepper, diced
- 480 ml (2 cups) fresh tomatoes, diced
- 360 ml (1 ½ cups) feta cheese, crumbled
- 180 ml (¾ cup) kalamata olives, pitted
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 60 ml (¼ cup) red wine vinegar
- 60 ml (¼ cup) EVOO
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) lemon juice
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) oregano
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) black pepper

### Serving

- Plain Greek yogurt
- Hummus
- Pita wraps

## Method

### Prep

1. Combine Greek Salad ingredients in bowl and set in fridge for serving
2. Combine Shawarma ingredients in large Ziploc bag
3. Let Shawarma marinate in fridge for at least 1 hour

### Directions

1. Cook Shawarma in Instant Pot for 10 minutes and let release naturally
2. Serve with Greek salad, yogurt, hummus, and pita wraps
