# Chicken Stir-Fry

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Quick
- Serves: 6

## Description

Chicken and frozen stir-fry vegetables in a simple soy, garlic and ginger sauce.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Cook: 15 min
Total: 25 min

## Ingredients

- 905 g (2 lb) chicken breasts or thighs, cut small
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) cornstarch
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) soy sauce
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) oil
- 1 bag frozen vegetables

### Sauce

- 2-3 cloves minced garlic
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) rice vinegar
- 45 ml (3 tbsp) soy sauce
- 60 ml (¼ cup) water
- 5 ml (1 tsp) ground ginger
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) cornstarch

## Method

### Prep

1. Mix chicken, cornstarch, soy sauce in a bowl and rest for 20+ minutes
2. Heat oil in large skillet or wok over med-high heat

### Directions

1. Cook chicken in skillet until no longer pink, about 5 minutes each side
2. Remove and set aside, chopping when cool enough to handle
3. Deglaze the skillet with a bit of water, then add the veggies
4. Cook for 5-6 minutes, until veggies are cooked through
5. While cooking, in a small bowl stir together the remaining ingredients until cornstarch is dissolved
6. Return chopped chicken to skillet and stir together
7. Add sauce to skillet and bring to a boil to thicken
8. Serve over rice
