# Chicken Tenders

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Quick
- Source: Judy
- Serves: 2

## Description

Three-ingredient chicken tenders coated in mayo and Shake 'n Bake, then air-fried.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Air fry: 7–8 min
Total: 20 min

## Ingredients

- 2 chicken breasts
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) mayo
- 60 ml (4 tbsp) Shake & Bake

## Method

### Prep

1. Cut chicken into tenders or nuggets
2. Heat air fryer to 190 °C (375 °F)

### Directions

1. Add chicken and mayo to large ziploc bag
2. Shake to fully coat chicken
3. Add Shake & Bake and shake to fully coat
4. Air fry for 7-8 minutes on each side
