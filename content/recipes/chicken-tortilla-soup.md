# Chicken Tortilla Soup

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Soup, Spicy, High fiber, Favorite
- Image: /images/chicken-tortilla-soup.jpg
- Emoji: 🥣
- Serves: 8

## Description

Chicken simmered with jalapeños, garlic and chickpeas, served with avocado, lime and tortilla chips.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Cook: 35 min
Total: 50 min

## Ingredients

- 1 + 15 ml (1 tbsp) olive oil
- 905 g (2 lb) boneless chicken breast or thigh
- 1.9 L (8 cups) chicken broth
- 2 jalapeños, minced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) black pepper
- 60 ml (¼ cup) fresh cilantro, chopped
- 2 can chickpeas, drained
- 1 avocado, diced
- 1 lime, halved
- Tortilla chips

## Method

### Prep

1. Set large pot over medium heat

### Directions

1. Heat 15 ml (1 tbsp) oil in pot
2. Place chicken in pot and cook until browned
3. Remove and set aside
4. Place jalapeños and garlic in pot, sauté for 5 minutes
5. Deglaze with a bit of broth, then add remaining broth, pepper, and cilantro
6. Shred the chicken and return to the pot
7. Bring to boil, add the beans
8. Turn heat to low
9. Cover and simmer for 30 minutes
10. Add avocado and squeeze lime into pot
11. Serve with chips
