# Chicken Wild Rice Hotdish

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Instant Pot, Minnesota
- Serves: 6

## Description

Wild rice, chicken, celery, peppers and mushrooms baked with cream soups, a Minnesota classic.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Soak: overnight
Cook: 1 h 15 min
Total: 1 h 35 min

## Ingredients

- 240 ml (1 cup) uncooked wild rice
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) + 15 ml (1 tbsp) EVOO
- 480 ml (2 cups) celery, diced
- 1 large onion, diced
- 1 bell pepper, seeded and diced
- 225 g (8 oz) fresh mushrooms, sliced
- 45 ml (3 tbsp) soy sauce
- 455 g (1 lb) chicken, diced
- 425 g (15 oz) can cream of mushroom
- 425 g (15 oz) can cream of chicken
- 240 ml (1 cup) chicken stock

## Method

### Prep

1. Rinse the wild rice with water and soak overnight
2. Alternately, pre-cook the wild rice in a rice or pressure cooker
3. Heat a large skillet over med-high heat
4. Grease a 13” x 9” casserole dish
5. Pre-heat oven to 165 °C (325 °F)

### Directions

1. Heat 15 ml (1 tbsp) EVOO in the skillet
2. Add the celery, onion, & pepper
3. Cook for 5-8 minutes until starting to brown
4. Remove and set aside, leaving any juices
5. Add the mushrooms & soy sauce to the skillet
6. Cook for 5-8 minutes until soft and starting to brown
7. Remove and set aside
8. Add the remaining 15 ml (1 tbsp) EVOO & chicken to the skillet
9. Cook until the chicken is cooked through
10. Add all ingredients to the casserole dish and mix together
11. Bake for 1 hour
