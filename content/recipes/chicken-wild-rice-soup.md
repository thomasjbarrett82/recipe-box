# Chicken & Wild Rice Soup

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Soup, Dutch oven, Minnesota, Favorite
- Source: Serious Eats
- Image: /images/chicken-wild-rice-soup.jpg
- Serves: 10

## Description

Creamy chicken and wild rice soup, thickened with gluten-free flour.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Cook: 1 h
Total: 1 h 20 min

## Ingredients

- 15 ml (1 tbsp) oil
- 905 g–1.4 kg (2–3 lb) chicken thighs
- 60 ml (4 tbsp) butter
- 4 celery stalks, chopped
- 1 large carrot, sliced
- 1 onion, diced
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) kosher salt
- 5 ml (1 tsp) black pepper
- 3-4 cloves garlic, minced
- 5 ml (1 tsp) thyme (OR 15 ml (1 tbsp) fresh thyme)
- 60 ml (1/4 cup) GF flour
- 2.8 L (3 quarts) chicken stock (homemade is best)
- 240 ml (1 cup) uncooked wild rice
- 0.6 ml (1/8 tsp) baking soda
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) heavy cream
- 5 ml (1 tsp) lemon zest + 30 ml (2 tbsp) lemon juice

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat large Dutch oven over medium heat

### Directions

1. Add oil to pot and brown chicken thighs
2. Remove to plate, shred, and set aside
   - Remove bones if present
3. Melt butter in pot
4. Add celery, carrot, onion, salt, pepper
5. Cook until softened
6. Add garlic & thyme for 1 minute
7. Add flour and stir until starting to stick
8. Pour in stock and stir until flour is fully incorporated
9. Add rice and baking soda
10. Bring to boil over med-high heat
11. Reduce heat to simmer and add chicken back in
12. Cover and cook for 40 minutes
13. Stir in heavy cream and lemon
14. Garnish with some fresh parsley or celery leaves
