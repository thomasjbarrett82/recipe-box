# Chicken Wings

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Appetizer, Spicy
- Source: Serious Eats
- Serves: 3

## Description

Crispy baked or air-fried wings, dry-brined with baking powder and tossed in buttery hot sauce.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Dry brine: 1–24 h
Cook: 20–30 min
Total: 1 h 40 min

## Ingredients

- 905 g (2 lb) chicken wings
- 10 ml (2 tsp) baking powder
- 10 ml (2 tsp) kosher salt
- 10 ml (2 tsp) cornstarch
- 60 ml (1/4 cup) butter, melted
- 60 ml (1/4 cup) hot sauce (Frank's Red Hot or other)
- Blue cheese/ranch dressing for dipping
- Carrots and/or celery sticks

## Method

### Prep

1. Mix baking powder, salt, and cornstarch
2. Pat chicken wings dry and toss with mixture to coat
3. Place on rack in fridge for 1-24 hours
4. Heat air fryer to 205 °C (400 °F)

### Directions

1. Place wings in air fryer leaving some space between each wing
2. Cook for 20-30 minutes until golden brown and cooked through
3. Whisk melted butter and hot sauce in large bowl
4. Add wings to bowl and toss to combine
5. Serve immediately with dressing and veggies
