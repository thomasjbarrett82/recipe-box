# Chicken & Yellow Rice (Instant Pot)

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Instant Pot, High fiber
- Serves: 6

## Description

Chicken, brown rice, peas and corn cooked together with turmeric in the Instant Pot.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Cook: 45 min
Total: 55 min

## Ingredients

- 5 ml (1 tsp) olive oil
- 905 g (2 lb) chicken breast or thigh, cubed
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 240 ml (1 cup) carrots, sliced
- 480 ml (2 cups) chicken broth
- 480 ml (2 cups) brown rice
- 240 ml (1 cup) frozen peas
- 240 ml (1 cup) frozen corn
- 5 ml (1 tsp) ground turmeric
- 5 ml (1 tsp) ground oregano
- 5 ml (1 tsp) salt
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) black pepper
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) hot sauce

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat olive oil in IP over Sauté

### Directions

1. Brown chicken in olive oil, remove and set aside
2. Add onion and carrots, cook until onion is translucent
3. Add remaining ingredients and mix together
4. Arrange chicken on top of the mixture
5. Close lid and seal
6. Cook on MANUAL for 25 minutes
7. Allow pressure to lower naturally for 15 minutes
8. Vent any remaining pressure
9. Mix chicken into the rice & vegetables before serving
