# Chicken Zucchini Rice Hotdish

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Minnesota
- Source: All Recipes
- Image: /images/chicken-zucchini-rice-hotdish.jpg
- Emoji: 🥒
- Serves: 8

## Description

Zucchini, rice and chicken bound with eggs and two cheeses, baked under bread crumbs.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Bake: 45 min
Total: 1 h 5 min

## Ingredients

- 120 ml (1/2 cup) butter (1 stick)
- 905 g (2 lb) zucchini, shredded or diced
- 360 ml (1 1/2 cups) cooked rice
- 455 g (1 lb) chicken, cooked and shredded
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) Parmesan, shredded
- 240 ml (1 cup) Cheddar, shredded
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 240 ml (1 cup) bread crumbs

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat large pan over medium
2. Heat oven to 220 °C (425 °F)

### Directions

1. Melt butter in pan and add zucchini
2. Cook for 5 minutes
3. Stir in rice, chicken, cheeses, eggs
4. Sprinkle bread crumbs over top
5. Transfer to oven and bake until golden brown and bubbly
6. Remove and let cool 5 minutes before serving
