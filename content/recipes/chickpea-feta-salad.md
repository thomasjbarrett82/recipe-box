# Chickpea Feta Salad

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Side, Quick, Vegetarian, High fiber
- Source: Instagram
- Image: /images/chickpea-feta-salad.jpg
- Serves: 8

## Description

Chickpeas, cherry tomatoes, red onion, cilantro and feta in a lemon-honey dressing.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Rest: 15 min
Total: 30 min

## Ingredients

- 2 cans chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 1 red onion, diced
- 80 ml (1/3 cup) fresh cilantro, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 lemon, zest and juice
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) hemp hearts
- 115 g (4 oz) feta cheese
- 225 g (8 oz) cherry tomatoes, halved
- 45 ml (3 tbsp) EVOO
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) honey
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) kosher salt
- 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) black pepper

## Method

1. Mix everything in a large bowl
2. Let sit for 15 mins before serving
