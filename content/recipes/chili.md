# Chili

## Description

Easy ground beef chili with fajita vegetables, beans in chili sauce and Rotel, simmered in a Dutch oven or crockpot.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Cook: 2–3 h
Total: 2 h 15 min

## Ingredients

- 455 g (1 lb) ground beef
- 1-2 packages of fajita veggies (onions & bell peppers)
- 4-6 cans beans with chili sauce (mix of bean types)
- 2 cans Rotel
- 2 packages chili seasoning
- OPTIONAL: 1 or 2 jalapenos, diced
- OPTIONal: 240 ml (1 cup) tortilla chips, crushed

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat Dutch oven on medium
2. OR turn crockpot on low and heat a pan on medium

### Dutch oven

1. Brown beef in pot
2. Add remaining ingredients (except chips)
3. Bring just to a low boil
4. Add tortilla chips, if using them
5. Turn to low and cover
6. Simmer for 2-3 hours

### Slow cooker

1. Brown beef in pan
2. Combine with all remaining ingredients in crockpot
3. Cook on low for 6-8 hours

Serve with cheese and sour cream
