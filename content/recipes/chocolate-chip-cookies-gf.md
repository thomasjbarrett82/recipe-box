# Chocolate Chip Cookies (GF)

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert, Gluten free
- Source: My Gluten-Free Kitchen
- Image: /images/chocolate-chip-cookies-gf.jpg
- Emoji: 🍪
- Serves: 18

## Description

Gluten-free chocolate chip cookies made with cream cheese and extra yolks; resting the dough gives them a chewy, bakery-style texture.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Chill: 4–48 h
Bake: 10–15 min
Total: 4 h 50 min

## Ingredients

- 280 grams A/P gluten free flour
- 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) xanthan gum (if flour doesn't have any)
- 5 ml (1 tsp) baking soda
- 55 g (2 oz) cream cheese, room temperature
- 180 ml (3/4 cup) salted butter, melted (OR 180 ml (3/4 cup) unsalted butter + 5 ml (1 tsp) salt)
- 240 ml (1 cup) brown sugar
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) sugar
- 10 ml (2 tsp) vanilla extract
- 2 egg yolks
- 480 ml (2 cups) chocolate chips

## Method

### Prep

1. Whisk flour, xanthan gum, baking soda in bowl
2. Place cream cheese, butter, sugars in stand mixer bowl
3. Use paddle on medium speed for ~2 minutes
4. Add vanilla extract and egg yolks and combine
5. Stir in flour mix on low speed until just combined
6. Add chocolate chips on low speed until just combined
7. Cover bowl with wrap and refrigerate for 4-48 hours

### Directions

1. Remove dough from fridge 30 minutes before cooking (room temp)
2. Heat oven to 190 °C (375 °F)
3. Line cookie sheets with parchment paper or silicone liners
4. Use cookie scoop and evenly space dough balls on pans
5. Leave plenty of space for the cookies to spread out
6. Bake for 10-15 minutes until edges are set and just browned
7. Centers will look undercooked, that's OK
8. Remove and let sit on pan for 2-3 minutes before moving to cooling rack
