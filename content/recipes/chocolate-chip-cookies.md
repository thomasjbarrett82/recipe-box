# Chocolate Chip Cookies (Toll House)

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert, Quick
- Serves: 20

## Description

The classic Toll House chocolate chip cookie, as cookies or a pan bar.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Bake: 10 min
Total: 25 min

## Ingredients

- 540 ml (2 ¼ cups) flour
- 5 ml (1 tsp) baking soda
- 5 ml (1 tsp) salt
- 180 ml (¾ cup) white sugar (180 g)
- 180 ml (¾ cup) brown sugar (180 g)
- 240 ml (1 cup) butter, softened (2 sticks)
- 5 ml (1 tsp) vanilla
- 2 eggs
- 340 g (12 oz) chocolate chips
- 240 ml (1 cup) chopped nuts (optional)

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 190 °C (375 °F)

### Directions

1. Mix flour, baking soda, and salt in medium bowl
2. Mix sugars together in large bowl
3. Add butter and cream until light & fluffy
4. Add in vanilla
5. Add eggs
6. Slowly add flour
7. Scrape sides and mix thoroughly
8. Fold in chocolate chips
9. Drop by spoonful’s onto cookie sheet with parchment paper
10. Bake for 10 minutes until golden brown
11. Remove and let cool
12. Makes approximately 40 cookies

### Pan cookie variation

1. Spread dough into greased pan
2. Bake for 20-25 minutes until golden brown
