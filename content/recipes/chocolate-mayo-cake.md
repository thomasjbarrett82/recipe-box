# Chocolate Mayo Cake

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert
- Source: @bdylanhollis
- Serves: 15

## Description

A moist, dairy-light chocolate cake made with mayonnaise and water or leftover coffee.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Bake: 30 min
Total: 40 min

## Ingredients

- 480 ml (2 cups) flour
- 240 ml (1 cup) sugar
- 10 ml (2 tsp) baking soda
- 5 ml (1 tsp) vanilla extract
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) cocoa powder
- 240 ml (1 cup) mayo
- 240 ml (1 cup) water OR old coffee

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)
2. Grease and flour 9x13 baking dish

### Directions

1. Mix ingredients in large bowl
2. Pour into baking dish
3. Bake for 30 mins or until toothpick comes out clean
4. Allow to cool completely before frosting
