# Chocolate Pie

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert
- Serves: 8

## Description

A rich baked chocolate custard pie in a graham cracker crust.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Bake: 40–50 min
Cool: 2 h
Total: 3 h

## Ingredients

### Crust

- 360 ml (1 ½ cups) graham cracker crumbs
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) sugar
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) brown sugar
- 115 g (1 stick) butter, melted

### Filling

- 115 g (1 stick) butter
- 115 g (4 oz) Scharffen Berger’s baking chocolate
- 300 ml (1 ¼ cups) sugar
- 1 (115 g / 4 oz) can evaporated milk
- 0.6 ml (1/8 tsp) ground nutmeg
- 2 eggs

## Method

### Prep

1. Combine crust ingredients in medium bowl
2. Firmly pack crust mixture into a 9” or 10” pie pan
3. Bake at 190 °C (375 °F) for 8 minutes
4. Remove and place in the fridge to cool

### Directions

1. Melt the butter and chocolate in a saucepan over low heat
2. Remove from heat and whisk in the sugar, milk, and nutmeg
3. Add the eggs, stirring constantly to keep from scrambling
4. Pour the filling into the pie shell
5. Bake for 30-40 minutes at 175 °C (350 °F) until the filling is set
6. Let cool completely
7. Serve with high quality whipped cream
