# Chocolate Pudding Pie (Allergen-Free)

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert
- Image: /images/chocolate-pudding-pie-allergen-free.jpg
- Serves: 8

## Description

Chocolate pudding pie that can be made free of common allergens, with a cookie, almond and coconut crust.

## Cook Times

Prep: 30 min
Cook: 25 min
Chill: 4 h
Total: 4 h 55 min

## Ingredients

### Crust

- 140 g (5 oz) chocolate cookie wafers OR allergen-free cookie wafers
- 60 ml (¼ cup) ground almonds
- 45 ml (3 tbsp) unsalted butter OR butter substitute, melted
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) water
- 60 ml (¼ cup) shredded coconut

### Filling

- 180 ml (¾ cup) sugar
- 60 ml (¼ cup) + 30 ml (2 tbsp) unsweetened cocoa powder
- 60 ml (¼ cup) cornstarch
- 480 ml (2 cups) skim milk OR milk substitute
- 55 g (2 oz) semisweet chocolate, chopped
- 10 ml (2 tsp) vanilla extract

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)

### Directions

1. Combine cookies & almonds in food processor.
2. Pulse until mixture is finely ground.
3. Add melted butter and water.
4. Pulse once or twice.
5. Stir in coconut by hand.
6. Pour crumb mixture into 9 in. pie pan.
7. Using fingertips, push evenly into bottom and up sides of pan.
8. Bake for 12-15 minutes until crust hardens slightly.
9. In heavy saucepan, stir together sugar, cocoa, & cornstarch.
10. Turn heat to medium-high.
11. Gradually add milk, stirring constantly, until pudding thickens and boils.
12. Reduce heat to medium-low and add chocolate.
13. Heat for 5 more minutes until chocolate is incorporated.
14. Remove from heat and let cool for 5 minutes.
15. Stir in vanilla and pour into prepared pie shell.
16. Refrigerate until completely set, 4 hours or overnight.

## Notes

Use a pre-made chocolate or graham crust to save time, if desired.
