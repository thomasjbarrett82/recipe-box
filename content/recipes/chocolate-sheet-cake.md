# Chocolate Sheet Cake

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert
- Image: /images/chocolate-sheet-cake.jpg
- Emoji: 🍫
- Serves: 24

## Description

A big, tender chocolate sheet cake made with buttermilk, for a crowd.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Bake: 20 min
Total: 35 min

## Ingredients

- 480 ml (2 cups) flour
- 480 ml (2 cups) sugar
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) salt
- 60 ml (4 tbsp) baking cocoa
- 5 ml (1 tsp) baking soda
- 120 ml (½ cup) oil
- 120 ml (½ cup) butter, melted (1 stick)
- 240 ml (1 cup) water
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 120 ml (½ cup) buttermilk
- 5 ml (1 tsp) vanilla
- Chocolate frosting for decoration

## Method

### Prep

1. Grease a large baking sheet or dish
2. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)

### Directions

1. Whisk together the dry ingredients in a large bowl
2. Mix the wet ingredients in another bowl
3. Stir the wet into the dry thoroughly
4. Bake 20 minutes until no wet remains
5. Remove and let cool before frosting
