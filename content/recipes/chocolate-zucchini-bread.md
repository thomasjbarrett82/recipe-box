# Chocolate Zucchini Bread

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Bread
- Source: @bdylanhollis
- Image: /images/chocolate-zucchini-bread.jpg
- Emoji: 🍫
- Serves: 10

## Description

Moist chocolate quick bread with shredded zucchini.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Bake: 50 min
Total: 1 h 5 min

## Ingredients

- 240 ml (1 cup) flour
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) cocoa powder
- 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) baking powder
- 60 ml (1/4 cup) butter, melted
- 60 ml (1/4 cup) vegetable oil
- 240 ml (1 cup) brown sugar
- 2 eggs
- 360 ml (1 1/2 cups) zucchini, shredded with skin on

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)
2. Grease bread pan

### Directions

1. Mix flour, cocoa, baking powder in bowl
2. Whisk butter, oil, sugar, eggs in another bowl
3. Combine wet & dry with zucchini in large bowl
4. Bake for 50 mins
5. Let cool before serving
