# Christmas Crack

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert, Holiday
- Source: @bdylanhollis
- Serves: 20

## Description

Saltine toffee bark: crackers baked under butter and brown sugar toffee, topped with melted chocolate and walnuts.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Cook: 15 min
Chill: 1 h
Total: 1 h 25 min

## Ingredients

- 1 roll crackers (saltine or GF)
- 240 ml (1 cup) butter
- 240 ml (1 cup) brown sugar
- 1 bag chocolate chips
- 1 bag crushed walnuts (or anything else you want for topping)

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)
2. Heat small pot over med heat

### Directions

1. Lay crackers flat in aluminum foil lined pan
2. Boil butter & brown sugar in pot for 5 minutes
3. Pour over crackers and spread
4. Bake for 7 minutes
5. Remove and spread chocolate chips over everything
6. Sprinkle toppings over everything
7. Transfer to fridge for at least 1 hour
8. Remove from foil
9. Use a large knife to crack into small squares
