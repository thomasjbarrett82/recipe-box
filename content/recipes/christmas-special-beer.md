# Christmas Special Beer

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Drink, Holiday
- Serves: About 50

## Description

A five-gallon homebrew holiday ale spiced with vanilla, anise, nutmeg, cinnamon and cloves.

## Cook Times

Prep: 30 min
Brew: 1 h 30 min
Ferment and condition: about 4 weeks
Total: 2 h

## Ingredients

- ~18.9 L (5 gallons) filtered water
- 170 g (6 oz) crystal malt
- 170 g (6 oz) chocolate malt
- 1.4 kg (3 lb) plain amber malt extract
- 1.8 kg (4 lb) can pale malt extract
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) maltodextrin
- 1.25 ml (¼ tsp) + 1.25 ml (¼ tsp) pure vanilla extract
- 5 ml (1 tsp) Irish moss
- 1.25 ml (¼ tsp) + 1.25 ml (¼ tsp) anise
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) gypsum
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) kosher salt
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) nutmeg
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) cinnamon
- 1.25 ml (¼ tsp) ground cloves
- 30 g (1 oz) Northern Brewer hops
- 180 ml (¾ cup) amber malt extract

## Method

1. Bring one gallon of water to ~66 °C (150 °F)
2. Steep crystal and chocolate malts for 30 minutes in filter bag
3. Remove grains, add 1.9 L (½ gallon) water and bring to a boil
4. Add amber & pale malt extracts
5. Boil for 45 minutes
6. Add maltodextrin and seasonings and continue boiling for 15 minutes
7. Add the second 1.25 ml (¼ tsp) of vanilla & anise and remove from heat
8. Pour into the bucket and add enough water to make 18.9 L (5 gallons)
9. Pitch the yeast at ~27 °C (80 °F)
10. Move into carboy after initial fermentation settles down
11. Should be ready for bottling after ~3 weeks
12. Prime for bottling with 180 ml (¾ cup) malt extract
13. Should be ready to drink ~2 weeks after bottling
