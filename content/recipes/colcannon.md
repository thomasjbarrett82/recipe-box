# Colcannon

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Side, Favorite, Vegetarian
- Source: Spruce Eats
- Image: /images/colcannon.jpg
- Emoji: 🥔
- Serves: 8

## Description

Irish mashed potatoes with butter, kale or cabbage and green onions.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Cook: 30 min
Total: 45 min

## Ingredients

- 1.4 kg (3 lb) yellow or russet potatoes, peeled and quartered
- 1 + 15 ml (1 tbsp) kosher salt
- 2 + 60 ml (4 tbsp) butter
- 720 ml (3 cups) chopped kale or cabbage
- 4 green onions, sliced OR 1 small onion, diced
- 240 ml (1 cup) milk
- 5 ml (1 tsp) mustard powder
- Black pepper to taste
- OPTIONAL: chunks of bacon or ham to mix in at end

## Method

1. Put potatoes and 15 ml (1 tbsp) salt in large pot, cover with water
2. Bring to boil and cook until tender
3. Drain in colander and set aside
4. Melt 30 ml (2 tbsp) butter in pot
5. Add greens and cook until soft and tender
6. Add milk and mustard powder, bring to boil
7. Turn off heat and return potatoes to pot
8. Mix in remaining butter and mash until smooth
9. Add more milk for desired consistency, if needed
