# Coleslaw

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Side, Quick, Vegetarian
- Source: Serious Eats
- Image: /images/coleslaw.jpg
- Emoji: 🥗
- Serves: 12

## Description

Crunchy cabbage and carrot slaw in a tangy mayo, cider vinegar and Dijon dressing.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Rest: 5–10 min
Total: 30 min

## Ingredients

- 1.4–1.8 kg (3–4 lb) head of green cabbage, finely shredded
- 1 red onion, finely sliced
- 1 large carrot, peeled and shredded
- 60 ml (1/4 cup) fresh parsley, chopped
- 240 ml (1 cup) + 45 ml (3 tbsp) white sugar
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) kosher salt
- 240 ml (1 cup) mayo
- 60 ml (1/4 cup) apple cider vinegar
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) Dijon mustard
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) black pepper

## Method

### Prep

1. Combine cabbage, onion, carrot, parsley, 240 ml (1 cup) sugar, kosher salt in large bowl
2. Let rest 5-10 minutes
3. Transfer to large colander and rinse under cold water
4. Dry with salad spinner or blotting with towels in sheet pan
5. Return to large bowl

### Directions

1. Whisk mayo, vinegar, mustard, pepper, 45 ml (3 tbsp) sugar in bowl
2. Stir dressing into cabbage mixture
3. Taste and adjust seasoning as desired
4. Rest in fridge for 1+ hour before serving
