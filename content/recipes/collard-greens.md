# Collard Greens

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Side, High fiber
- Source: Tom
- Serves: 4

## Description

Southern-style collards braised with ham hocks, liquid smoke, creole seasoning and a splash of vinegar.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Cook: 1 h 10 min
Total: 1 h 25 min

## Ingredients

- 30 ml (2 tbsp) butter
- 225 g (1/2 lb) ham hocks
- 1/2 onion, minced
- 1 bunch (455–680 g / 1–1.5 lb) collard greens, washed, trimmed, roughly chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 480 ml (2 cups) chicken stock
- 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) liquid smoke
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) creole seasoning
- 5 ml (1 tsp) hot sauce
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) cider vinegar
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) sugar

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat large pot over medium

### Directions

1. Melt butter until it starts to foam
2. Cook ham hocks and onion in pot for 5 minutes
3. Add enough water to cover the ham hocks
4. Bring to a boil, cover and simmer for 1 hour until tender
5. Stir in remaining ingredients, adding water if needed
   - Add greens in batches, if needed
6. Cover and simmer for 2+ hours until soft and tender
7. Remove ham hocks, shred and return meat to pot
8. Season to taste
