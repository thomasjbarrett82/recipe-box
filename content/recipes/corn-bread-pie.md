# Corn Bread Pie

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Bread
- Image: /images/corn-bread-pie.jpg
- Serves: 6

## Description

Browned pork sausage baked under a sweet cornbread topping in a pie plate.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Bake: 25 min
Total: 40 min

## Ingredients

- 455 g (1 lb) fresh pork sausage
- 240 ml (1 cup) yellow cornmeal
- 240 ml (1 cup) flour
- ¼ sugar
- 20 ml (4 tsp) baking powder
- 1 egg
- 240 ml (1 cup) milk

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 220 °C (425 °F)

### Directions

1. Brown pork sausage, and reserve 60 ml (¼ cup) drippings
2. Sift together cornmeal, flour, sugar and baking powder
3. Add egg, milk, and drippings and combine thoroughly
4. Fold sausage into mixture
5. Turn batter into a greased 9-inch pie plate
6. Bake for 25 minutes until done
7. Serve with butter and salt & pepper
