# Corn Chowder

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Soup, Spicy, Vegetarian, High fiber
- Serves: 4

## Description

Creamy corn and potato chowder with poblano, jalapeños and red pepper.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Cook: 40 min
Total: 1 h

## Ingredients

- 2 poblano, deseeded and halved lengthwise
- 2 jalapenos, deseeded and halved lengthwise
- 1 red bell pepper, deseeded and halved lengthwise
- 2 Yukon gold or red potatoes, peeled and chunked
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) EVOO
- 1 small yellow onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 stalks celery, diced
- 5 ml (1 tsp) salt
- 720 ml (3 cups) corn kernels
- 480 ml (2 cups) vegetable stock
- 240 ml (1 cup) milk
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) black pepper
- 5 ml (1 tsp) oregano

## Method

### Prep

1. Roast the chilis and pepper under the broiler and chop

### Directions

1. Boil the potatoes until tender
2. Drain and set aside
3. Heat the EVOO in a large saucepan
4. Add the onion, garlic, and celery and sauté
5. Stir in the chilis and potatoes
6. Add in remaining ingredients
7. Simmer until thickened (25-30 minutes)
