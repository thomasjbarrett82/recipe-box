# Corn Soup

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Soup, Dutch oven, Vegetarian
- Serves: 5

## Description

Corn soup thickened with cornmeal, with potato, peppers and a little jalapeño.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Cook: 55 min
Total: 1 h 10 min

## Ingredients

- 15 ml (1 tbsp) butter
- 1 small onion, minced
- 2 sticks celery, minced
- 1 bell pepper, minced
- 1 potato, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 jalapeno, minced
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) cornmeal
- 960 ml (4 cups) corn
- 960 ml (4 cups) vegetable broth

## Method

### Directions

1. Melt butter in Dutch oven over medium heat
2. Add onion, celery, bell pepper, and potato
3. Stir and cover for 5 minutes
4. Add garlic and jalapeno
5. Stir and cover for 5 minutes
6. Add cornmeal, corn, and broth
7. Bring to a boil, and then let simmer for 30 minutes
8. Salt and pepper to taste

### Pressure cooker

1. After adding all ingredients, cover and cook for 10 minutes
