# Corn Sugar Cookies

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert
- Source: @bdylanhollis
- Image: /images/corn-sugar-cookies.jpg
- Serves: 12

## Description

Buttery sugar cookies with cornmeal for a little crunch.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Freeze: 30 min
Bake: 8–10 min
Total: 55 min

## Ingredients

- 120 ml (1/2 cup) butter
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) sugar
- 1 egg
- 5 ml (1 tsp) vanilla extract
- 180 ml (3/4 cup) cornmeal
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) flour

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 190 °C (375 °F)

### Directions

1. Cream butter and sugar in large bowl
2. Mix in egg and vanilla
3. Stir in cornmeal and flour
4. Form into log using aluminum foil
5. Transfer to freezer for 30 mins to firm up
6. Remove and roll in cornmeal
7. Slice into cookies
8. Arrange on parchment lined pan
9. Bake for 8-10 minutes
10. Let cool before serving
