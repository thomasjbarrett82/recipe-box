# Cornbread

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Bread
- Image: /images/cornbread.jpg
- Serves: 9

## Description

Skillet cornbread made with buttermilk, with optional jalapeños, Cheddar, bacon and corn.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Bake: 35 min
Total: 50 min

## Ingredients

- 120 ml (½ cup) brown sugar
- 480 ml (2 cups) cornmeal
- 240 ml (1 cup) flour
- 10 ml (2 tsp) baking powder
- 2 eggs
- 240 ml (1 cup) buttermilk
- 60 ml (¼ cup) melted butter
- 5 ml (1 tsp) salt

### OPTIONAL

- 2 jalapeños, minced
- 240 ml (1 cup) shredded Cheddar
- 225 g (½ lb) cooked bacon, crumbled
- 240 ml (1 cup) sweet corn kernels

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)

### Directions

1. Mix the dry ingredients in large bowl
2. Mix the wet ingredients in medium bowl
3. Fold the wet into the dry
4. Pour into greased 9x9 baking dish or cast-iron pan
5. Bake for 35 minutes until done
