# Cornstarch Cookies

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert
- Source: @bdylanhollis
- Image: /images/cornstarch-cookies.jpg
- Serves: 12

## Description

Three-ingredient melt-in-your-mouth cookies made from sweetened condensed milk, butter and cornstarch.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Bake: 15–20 min
Total: 35 min

## Ingredients

- 120 ml (1/2 cup) sweetened condensed milk
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) butter, softened
- 480 ml (2 cups) cornstarch

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 165 °C (325 °F)

### Directions

1. Mix milk & butter in large bowl
2. Stir in cornstarch with handle until dough consistency
3. Scoop into small balls on parchment lined pan
4. Bake for 15-20 minutes
5. Let cool completely before serving
