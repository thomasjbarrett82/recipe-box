# Cowboy Caviar

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Appetizer, Quick, High fiber
- Source: @mariannas_pantry
- Image: /images/cowboy-caviar.jpg
- Emoji: 🤠
- Serves: 10

## Description

A big, colorful bean salad of black beans, corn, peppers, cucumber, avocado and cotija in a tangy yogurt dressing.

## Cook Times

Prep: 25 min
Total: 25 min

## Ingredients

- 1 bag frozen corn
- 2 cans black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 large cucumber, diced
- 2 bell peppers, diced
- 1/2 onion, diced
- 1 avocado, diced
- 225 g (8 oz) cherry tomatoes, halved
- 115 g (4 oz) package cotija cheese, crumbled
- 2 jalapenos, deseeded and diced
- 1-2 cloves garlic, minced
- 30–45 ml (2–3 tbsp) lime juice
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) plain Greek yogurt
- 45 ml (3 tbsp) taco seasoning
- Salt and pepper to taste
- OPTIONAL: 455 g (1 lb) chicken, cooked and diced

## Method

1. Mix everything in a very large bowl
2. Refrigerate for at least an hour before serving
3. Can also be used as a dip for tortilla chips
