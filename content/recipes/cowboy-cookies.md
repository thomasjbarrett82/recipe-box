# Cowboy Cookies

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert
- Source: @bdylanhollis
- Image: /images/cowboy-cookies.jpg
- Serves: 30

## Description

Loaded oatmeal cookies with coconut, pecans and chocolate chips.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Bake: 10–12 min
Total: 35 min

## Ingredients

- 240 ml (1 cup) coconut, shredded
- 240 ml (1 cup) pecans, crushed
- 480 ml (2 cups) rolled oats
- 240 ml (1 cup) butter
- 240 ml (1 cup) brown sugar
- 240 ml (1 cup) sugar
- 5 ml (1 tsp) vanilla extract
- 2 eggs
- 480 ml (2 cups) flour
- 5 ml (1 tsp) baking soda
- 480 ml (2 cups) chocolate chips

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)

### Directions

1. Spread coconut, pecans, & oats on parchment lined pan
2. Bake for 10 minutes to toast ingredients
3. Cream butter and sugars in large bowl
4. Mix in vanilla & eggs
5. Mix in flour & baking soda
6. Fold in toasted ingredients
7. Fold in chocolate chips
8. Scoop into small balls on parchment lined pan
9. Bake for 11-12 minutes
10. Let cool before serving
