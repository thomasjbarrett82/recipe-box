# Crab Dip

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Appetizer
- Image: /images/crab-dip.jpg
- Emoji: 🦀
- Serves: 12

## Description

Cold, creamy crab dip with cream cheese, lemon and Worcestershire.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Chill: 30 min
Total: 40 min

## Ingredients

- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1 can minced crab
- 225 g (8 oz) cream cheese, softened
- 10 ml (2 tsp) lemon juice
- 10 ml (2 tsp) Worcestershire
- Salt and pepper

## Method

1. Mix all ingredients in a bowl
2. Salt and pepper to taste
3. Let chill in fridge for at least 30 minutes
