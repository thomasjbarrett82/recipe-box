# Cranberry Sauce

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Side, Vegetarian, Vegan, Holiday
- Source: Serious Eats
- Image: /images/cranberry-sauce.jpg
- Emoji: 🍒
- Serves: 9

## Description

Fresh cranberry sauce with orange juice and cinnamon.

## Cook Times

Prep: 5 min
Cook: 15 min
Cool: 30 min
Total: 50 min

## Ingredients

- 340 g (12 oz) bag fresh cranberries
- 240 ml (1 cup) sugar
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) water
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) OJ
- 5 ml (1 tsp) cinnamon
- Pinch of salt

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat 2.8 L (3 quarts) pot or sauce pan over medium

### Directions

1. Combine all ingredients and bring to a boil
2. Simmer, stirring occasionally, until berries start to pop
3. Roughly smash the berries with a spoon and continue until jam-like consistency
4. Remove from heat and cool for 30 minutes
5. Stir in water until desired consistency
6. Serve or store in fridge for a few days before serving
