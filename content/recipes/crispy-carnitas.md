# Crispy Carnitas

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Instant Pot
- Serves: 11

## Description

Pork roast pressure-cooked with beer, garlic, citrus and cumin, then shredded and broiled until crisp.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Cook: 1 h 15 min
Total: 1 h 35 min

## Ingredients

- 1.8–2.3 kg (4–5 lb) lean boneless pork roast, trimmed fat, chunked
- Salt & pepper
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) EVOO
- 240 ml (1 cup) beer (or chicken stock)
- 1 head of garlic, minced
- 120 ml (½ cup) OJ
- 60 ml (¼ cup) lime juice
- 5 ml (1 tsp) dried oregano
- 5 ml (1 tsp) ground cumin
- 5 ml (1 tsp) salt
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) black pepper

## Method

1. Season the pork roast with salt & pepper, set aside
2. Heat the EVOO in pressure cooker (Sauté)
3. Mix remaining sauce ingredients in a bowl, set aside
4. Saute the roast in the oil
5. Stir in the sauce and toss to combine
6. Close lid and set to High Pressure (or Meat setting) for 45 minutes
7. Let vent naturally for 15 minutes before opening
8. Shred the pork
9. Transfer to a large baking sheet
10. Broil for 15 minutes, ladling juices over it, until crispy
