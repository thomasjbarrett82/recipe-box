# Crispy Roast Turkey with Gravy

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Holiday, Favorite
- Image: /images/crispy-roast-turkey-gravy.jpg
- Emoji: 🦃
- Serves: 17

## Description

Roast turkey on a bed of vegetables with crisp skin, plus gravy made from the neck and drippings.

## Cook Times

Prep: 30 min
Roast: 3–4 h
Rest: 20 min
Total: 3 h 50 min

## Ingredients

- 3 large onions, chopped
- 3 large carrots, peeled & chopped
- 4 stalks celery, chopped
- 12 thyme sprigs
- 1 5.4–6.4 kg (12–14 lb) turkey, butterflied
- Turkey neck, backbone, and giblets, roughly chopped
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) oil
- Kosher salt & black pepper
- 950 ml (1 quart) chicken or turkey broth
- 2 bay leaves
- 45 ml (3 tbsp) butter
- 60 ml (4 tbsp) flour

## Method

### Prep

1. Preheat oven to 230 °C (450 °F)
2. Line a rimmed baking sheet with aluminum foil

### Directions

1. Spread 2/3 of vegetables and thyme across the baking sheet
2. Lay the turkey flat over the vegetables with the wings tucked underneath
3. Rub 15 ml (1 tbsp) oil over the turkey and season liberally with salt & pepper
4. Transfer to the oven
5. Once finished roasting, remove and rest for 20 minutes before carving
6. Heat the remaining 15 ml (1 tbsp) oil in a medium saucepan over med-high heat
7. Add chopped turkey parts and sauté until browned
8. Add remaining 1/3 vegetables and cook until soft and browned
9. Add broth, remaining thyme, and bay leaves
10. Bring to a boil and then reduce to a simmer
11. Simmer for 45 minutes, then strain out solids using a mesh strainer
12. Save off liquid in a separate container
13. Return the pot to the stove and melt the butter
14. Add flour and cook into a roux
15. Add the liquid back to the pot in a slow stream until re-incorporated
16. Add any additional juices from the roasted turkey, if desired
17. Bring to a boil and then simmer until reduced to a gravy texture
