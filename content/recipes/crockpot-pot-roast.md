# Crockpot Pot Roast

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Slow cooker
- Image: /images/crockpot-pot-roast.jpg
- Serves: 7

## Description

A four-ingredient pot roast slow-cooked with onion mushroom soup mix and broth.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Slow cook: 8–10 h
Total: 8 h 40 min

## Ingredients

- 1.4–1.8 kg (3–4 lb) chuck or arm roast
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) chicken broth
- 1 packet onion mushroom soup mix
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) cornstarch

## Method

1. Remove excess fat from roast
2. Add roast to crockpot
3. Add broth and soup mix over the roast
4. Cover and cook on LOW for 8-10 hours
5. Remove 240–480 ml (1–2 cups) of liquid and make a cornstarch slurry
6. Add slurry to pot and cook on HIGH for 30-45 minutes
