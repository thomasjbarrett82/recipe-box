# Cuban Chicken & Vegetables

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Grill, High fiber
- Serves: 5

## Description

Chicken, squash, peppers, mushrooms and potatoes in an orange juice, cumin and oregano mojo marinade, roasted or grilled.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Cook: 15–30 min
Total: 50 min

## Ingredients

- 680–905 g (1 ½–2 lb) chicken, any kind
- 2 bell peppers, cubed
- 480 ml (2 cups) butternut squash, cubed
- 240 ml (1 cup) sliced mushrooms
- ½ onion, chopped
- 3 potatoes, cubed
- 120 ml (½ cup) OJ
- 60 ml (¼ cup) fresh cilantro, chopped
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) olive oil
- 10 ml (2 tsp) ground cumin
- 10 ml (2 tsp) garlic, minced
- 5 ml (1 tsp) dried oregano
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) kosher salt
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) black pepper

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 260 °C (500 °F) or a grill to med-high

### Directions

1. Lay out 2 large pieces of aluminum foil
2. Place half of chicken and veggies on each foil
3. Whisk remaining ingredients in bowl
4. Pour half of mix over each foil
5. Tent and crimp foils
6. Place in oven or on grill
7. Cook for 15 minutes
8. Open tents and toss ingredients
9. Cook for 15 more minutes until chicken cooked and veggies are tender
