# Curried Lentils & Sweet Potatoes

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Vegetarian, High fiber
- Image: /images/curried-lentils-sweet-potatoes.jpg
- Serves: 5

## Description

Lentils, sweet potatoes and peas simmered in a curry-spiced vegetable broth.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Cook: 40 min
Total: 55 min

## Ingredients

- 15 ml (1 tbsp) olive oil
- 1 onion, peeled & diced
- 5 ml (1 tsp) minced garlic
- 240 ml (1 cup) dried lentils
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) curry powder
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) cumin
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) kosher salt
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) black pepper
- 960 ml (4 cups) vegetable broth
- 455 g (1 lb) fresh sweet potatoes, peeled & diced ½ inch
- 600 ml (2 ½ cups) sweet peas (or 1 frozen bag)
- Sour cream (optional)

## Method

### Prep

1. Sort lentils, discard debris, and rinse & drain
2. Heat large, heavy pan/pot over medium heat

### Directions

1. Add oil to pan
2. Add onion to pan and cook 5 minutes, stirring frequently
3. Add garlic, cumin, curry powder, salt, and pepper
4. Cook for 1-2 minutes until fragrant
5. Add broth, potatoes, and lentils
6. Bring to a boil, then cover and simmer
7. Cook until potatoes and lentils are tender, ~30 minutes
8. Stir in peas and heat through
9. Garnish with sour cream, if desired.
