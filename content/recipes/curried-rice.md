# Curried Rice

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Side, Quick
- Image: /images/curried-rice.jpg
- Emoji: 🍛
- Serves: 4

## Description

Rice simmered with onion and curry powder in chicken stock.

## Cook Times

Prep: 5 min
Cook: 25 min
Total: 30 min

## Ingredients

- 15 ml (1 tbsp) EVOO
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) butter
- ½ onion, diced
- 60 ml (4 tbsp) curry powder
- 480 ml (2 cups) chicken stock
- 240 ml (1 cup) rice, uncooked
- Salt & pepper, to taste

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat medium saucepan over med-low heat

### Directions

1. Melt EVOO and butter
2. Add onions and curry powder
3. Cook 4-5 minutes until onions are translucent, stirring occasionally
4. Add stock
5. Bring to a boil
6. Add in rice and salt & pepper
7. Stir and cover
8. Reduce heat to low and let simmer for 20 minutes
9. When water is fully absorbed, fluff with a fork and serve
