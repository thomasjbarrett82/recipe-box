# Cut Out Cookies

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert, Holiday
- Image: /images/cut-out-cookies.jpg
- Emoji: ⭐
- Serves: 24

## Description

Simple butter sugar cookies for rolling and cutting into shapes.

## Cook Times

Prep: 30 min
Chill: 1 h
Bake: 6–7 min
Total: 1 h 40 min

## Ingredients

- 240 ml (1 cup) butter
- 240 ml (1 cup) sugar
- 1 egg
- 5 ml (1 tsp) vanilla
- 10 ml (2 tsp) baking powder
- 720 ml (3 cups) flour

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 190 °C (375 °F)
2. Line baking pan with parchment (optional)

### Directions

1. In large bowl, cream sugar and butter
2. Mix in egg and vanilla
3. In medium bowl, combine baking powder and flour
4. Stir dry into wet, avoiding overmixing
5. Drop by spoonful onto baking pan
6. Bake for 6-7 minutes
