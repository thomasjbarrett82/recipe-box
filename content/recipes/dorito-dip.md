# Dorito Dip

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Appetizer, Quick
- Serves: 12

## Description

Velveeta melted with chicken gumbo soup and green chiles, for dipping Doritos.

## Cook Times

Prep: 5 min
Microwave: 2 min
Total: 10 min

## Ingredients

- 1 can chicken gumbo soup
- 340 g (¾ lb) Velveeta cheese, cut up
- 1 small can green chilies, drained & chopped

## Method

1. Mix all ingredients in a bowl
2. Microwave for 1 minute at a time, stirring in between, until melted
3. Serve with Doritos or tortilla chips
