# Dutch Oven Pot Roast

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Dutch oven, High fiber
- Image: /images/dutch-oven-pot-roast.jpg
- Serves: 7

## Description

Seared chuck roast braised in the oven with onion mushroom soup mix, potatoes and carrots.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Braise: 3 h 15 min
Total: 3 h 35 min

## Ingredients

- 30 ml (2 tbsp) EVOO
- 1.4–1.8 kg (3–4 lb) chuck or arm roast
- Kosher salt & black pepper
- Beef or chicken broth
- 1 packet onion mushroom soup mix
- 3-4 large potatoes, cut into 1 inch chunks
- 5 large carrots, cut into 1 inch chunks

## Method

### Prep

1. Season the roast with salt & pepper
2. Heat oven to 350
3. Heat a large Dutch oven on high

### Directions

1. Place EVOO in pot to heat up
2. Add roast to pot and sear for 5 minutes
3. Flip and sear for 5 minutes
4. Sprinkle soup mix over the top of the meat
5. Pour enough broth to come halfway up sides of meat
6. Cover and transfer to oven
7. Bake for 90 minutes
8. Check the liquid in the pot, add more broth if needed
9. Bake for 60 minutes
10. Cover the meat with potatoes and carrots
11. Season vegetables with salt & pepper
12. Bake for 45 minutes until potatoes and carrots are soft
