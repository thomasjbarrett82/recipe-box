# Easter Ham

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Holiday
- Serves: About 45

## Description

Bone-in ham baked in Dr. Pepper and glazed with brown sugar, mustard and warm spices.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Bake: 3–3½ h
Total: 3 h 45 min

## Ingredients

- 6.8–8.2 kg (15–18 lb) bone-in ham
- 2 cans Dr. Pepper
- 720 ml (3 cups) brown sugar
- 120 ml (½ cup) brown mustard
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) apple cider vinegar
- 10 ml (2 tsp) ginger
- 5 ml (1 tsp) cinnamon
- 5 ml (1 tsp) nutmeg
- 5 ml (1 tsp) cloves

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 150 °C (300 °F)

### Directions

1. Place ham in large roasting pan and tent with foil
2. Pour 1 can of Dr. Pepper into the pan
3. Bake for 3 – 3 ½ hours
4. Heat the remaining ingredients in a small saucepan until bubbling
5. Cook until reduced to a glaze (~15 minutes)
6. Remove the foil and increase heat to 205 °C (400 °F)
7. Glaze the ham in 5 minute intervals at least 2 or 3 times until glossy
8. Remove and let rest 15+ minutes before carving
