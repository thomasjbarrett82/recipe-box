# Easy Taco Skillet

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, High fiber, Favorite
- Source: Taste of Home
- Image: /images/easy-taco-skillet.jpg
- Serves: 6

## Description

Taco-seasoned beef or chicken with peppers, corn and beans, layered with tortillas and cheese and finished under the broiler.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Cook: 25 min
Total: 35 min

## Ingredients

- 455 g (1 lb) ground beef or chicken (cubed)
- 1 red onion, chopped
- 1 green bell pepper, cored and chopped
- 1 package taco seasoning
- 1 can corn, drained
- 1 can black or kidney beans, drained and rinsed
- 10 corn tortillas, cut into 1 inch pieces
- 1 (225 g / 8 oz) bottle taco sauce
- 360 ml (1.5 cups) cheddar, shredded

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat large skillet over med heat
2. Turn on broiler to 230 °C (450 °F) (optional)

### Directions

1. Brown meat in skillet
2. Add onion and pepper and cook for 2-3 minutes
3. Mix in seasoning, corn, beans, tortillas, and sauce
4. Heat up for 1-2 minutes, stirring
5. Sprinkle cheese over top
6. Transfer under broiler for 6-8 minutes until cheese is melted (optional)
7. Serve as-is or with taco toppings
