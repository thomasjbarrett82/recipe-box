# Egg Bake

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Breakfast
- Image: /images/egg-bake.jpg
- Emoji: 🥚
- Serves: 12

## Description

Make-ahead breakfast casserole of eggs, sausage, spinach, mushrooms, Swiss and Cheddar.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Bake: 45–50 min
Total: 1 h 20 min

## Ingredients

- 12 eggs
- 480 ml (2 cups) milk
- 1 package frozen spinach, thawed
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) Italian seasoning
- 225 g (8 oz) mushrooms, diced
- 1 onion, diced
- 455 g (1 lb) pork sausage
- 1 package Swiss, shredded
- 240 ml (1 cup) Cheddar, shredded

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)

### Directions

1. Combine all ingredients in greased casserole dish
2. Bake for 45-50 minutes until set
3. Remove and let cool for at least 10 minutes before serving
