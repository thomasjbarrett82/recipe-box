# Eggnog

- Serves: 9

## Description

Homemade eggnog cooked gently on the stove with cinnamon, cloves, nutmeg and vanilla, then chilled.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Cook: 15 min
Chill: 1 h
Total: 1 h 25 min

## Ingredients

- 5 eggs, separated
- 240 ml (1 cup) + 60 ml (1/4 cup) sugar
- 480 ml (2 cups) whole milk
- 240 ml (1 cup) heavy cream
- 5 ml (1 tsp) cinnamon
- 4 cloves
- 5 ml (1 tsp) nutmeg
- 5 ml (1 tsp) vanilla extract

## Method

1. Whisk egg yolks and 240 ml (1 cup) sugar in large bowl
2. Add milk, cream, cinnamon, and cloves to large pot
3. Scald over medium heat (don't boil)
4. Temper the eggs with some of the milk
5. Add eggs to pot
6. Cook over low heat until thick
7. Strain into large bowl
8. Whisk egg whites and 60 ml (1/4 cup) sugar until fluffy
9. Fold into eggnog with nutmeg and vanilla
10. Chill in fridge for at least 1 hour before serving with rum or bourbon
