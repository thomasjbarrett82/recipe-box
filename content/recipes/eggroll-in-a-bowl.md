# Eggroll in a Bowl

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Quick, High fiber
- Source: That Midwestern Mom
- Image: /images/eggroll-in-a-bowl.jpg
- Emoji: 🥢
- Serves: 4

## Description

All the flavor of an egg roll without the wrapper: sausage and coleslaw mix stir-fried with ginger, soy and sweet chili sauce.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Cook: 20 min
Total: 30 min

## Ingredients

- 455 g (1 lb) ground sausage
- 1 onion, diced
- 2-3 stalks celery, diced
- 3-4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 inch fresh ginger, grated
- soy sauce
- sesame oil
- rice wine vinegar
- sweet chili sauce
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) fish sauce
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) Worcestershire sauce
- 2 large bags coleslaw mix (OR 2 head shredded cabbage with 1 bag shredded carrots)
- 480 ml (2 cups) rice, cooked

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat very large pot over medium-high heat

### Directions

1. Brown and crumble the sausage
2. Add onion and celery and cook for 3 minutes
3. Mix in garlic, ginger, and sauces to taste
4. Add cabbage & carrots and cook until wilted
5. Serve with rice
