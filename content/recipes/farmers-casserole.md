# Farmer's Casserole

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Breakfast, Holiday
- Serves: 8

## Description

Overnight breakfast casserole of hash browns, ham, Monterey Jack and eggs.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Bake: 40–50 min
Chill: overnight
Total: 1 h 5 min

## Ingredients

- 720 ml (3 cups) hash browns
- 240 ml (1 cup) Monterey Jack, shredded
- 240 ml (1 cup) ham, cubed
- 60 ml (¼ cup) green onion, diced
- 4 eggs, beaten
- 340 g (12 oz) can evaporated milk
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) black pepper
- 5 ml (1 tsp) salt

## Method

### Prep

1. The night prior, grease 9x13 baking dish
2. Before cooking, heat oven to 350

### Directions

1. Spread hash browns evenly on bottom of dish
2. Sprinkle with cheese, ham, and onion
3. Combine milk, eggs, salt, and pepper in small bowl
4. Pour over baking dish
5. Refrigerate overnight
6. Bake for 40-50 minutes until set
7. Let rest before serving
