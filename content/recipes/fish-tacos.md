# Fish Tacos

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Quick, High fiber
- Serves: 4

## Description

Spice-rubbed white fish with a honey-lime slaw, corn and jalapeño.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Cook: 10 min
Total: 25 min

## Ingredients

- 45 ml (3 tbsp) EVOO
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) lime juice
- 10 ml (2 tsp) chili powder
- 5 ml (1 tsp) paprika
- 5 ml (1 tsp) cumin
- 5 ml (1 tsp) salt
- 680 g (1 ½ lb) cod or other white fish
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) vegetable oil
- 60 ml (¼ cup) mayo
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) lime juice
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) honey
- 480 ml (2 cups) shredded cabbage
- 240 ml (1 cup) corn kernels
- 1 jalapeño, diced
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) salt
- Sour cream, lime wedges, diced avocado, fresh cilantro, and tortillas for serving

## Method

### Prep

1. Mix EVOO, lime juice, chili powder, paprika, cumin, salt in large bag/bowl
2. Add cod and toss to combine
3. Set aside in fridge to marinate for 15+ minutes
4. Mix mayo, lime juice, honey, cabbage, corn, jalapeño, salt in large bowl
5. Set aside in fridge

### Directions

1. Heat vegetable oil in large skillet over med-high heat
2. Remove fish from marinade and add to pan in 1 layer
3. Cook 4 minutes per side
4. Remove to large bowl and continue cooking in batches
5. Flake fish with a fork
6. Serve with tortillas, coleslaw, and other toppings
