# Fisherman's Eggs

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Breakfast, Quick
- Image: /images/fishermans-eggs.jpg
- Serves: 1

## Description

Sardines and eggs baked together with lemon pepper and hot sauce, served with toast.

## Cook Times

Prep: 5 min
Bake: 12 min
Total: 20 min

## Ingredients

- 1 can sardines in oil
- Lemon pepper
- 2-3 eggs
- Hot sauce
- Toast

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat the oven to 400

### Directions

1. Place a small cast iron or other oven-safe pan in the oven for 4 minutes
2. Roughly mash the sardines in a bowl with the lemon pepper
3. Spread the sardines in the pan
4. Place back in the oven for 4 minutes
5. Crack the eggs over the sardines
6. Back in the oven for another 4 minutes
7. Remove and sprinkle some hot sauce over everything
8. Eat out of the pan with toast
