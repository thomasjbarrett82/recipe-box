# Focaccia Bread (GF)

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Bread, Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten free
- Source: Tom
- Serves: 8

## Description

Gluten-free focaccia with rosemary and garlic, using a long cold rise for flavor.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Cold rise: 8–48 h
Rise: 60–90 min
Bake: 25–30 min
Total: 10 h 20 min

## Ingredients

- 250 g warm water, 43–46 °C (110–114 °F)
- 5 ml (1 tsp) active dry yeast
- 5 ml (1 tsp) + 15 ml (1 tbsp) sugar
- 280 g Caputo Fioreglut flour
- 5 ml (1 tsp) + 5 ml (1 tsp) kosher salt
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) + 30 ml (2 tbsp) EVOO
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) fresh or dried rosemary
- 2 cloves garlic, minced

## Method

### Prep

1. Mix water, yeast, 5 ml (1 tsp) sugar in mixing bowl and let sit for 10 minutes
2. Add flour, 15 ml (1 tbsp) sugar, 5 ml (1 tsp) salt to bowl
3. Mix for a few minutes to thoroughly combine (dough will be wet) and rest for 5 minutes
4. Add 15 ml (1 tbsp) EVOO to another bowl
5. Transfer dough ball to second bowl and roll to cover with EVOO
6. Cover and store in fridge for 8-48 hours for cold proof

### Directions

1. Grease bottom and sides of 10" cast iron pan with a little EVOO
2. Transfer dough to pan and gently stretch to fill
3. Cover and let rise for 60-90 minutes until doubled in size
4. Heat oven to 220 °C (425 °F)
5. Combine 30 ml (2 tbsp) EVOO, 5 ml (1 tsp) salt, rosemary, garlic in small bowl
6. Use fingers to dimple the bread (don't collapse it or punch it down)
7. Drizzle topping evenly over the entire pan
8. Bake for 25-30 minutes until it pulls away from pan sides and golden brown on top
9. Remove and let cool for at least 5 minutes before serving
