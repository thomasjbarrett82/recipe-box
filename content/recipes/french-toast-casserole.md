# French Toast Casserole

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Breakfast, Vegetarian, Holiday
- Image: /images/french-toast-casserole.jpg
- Emoji: 🍞
- Serves: 8

## Description

Cubed bread soaked in a spiced custard, with optional pecans, baked until puffed and golden.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Bake: 45–55 min
Rest: 10 min
Total: 1 h 25 min

## Ingredients

- 1 loaf unsliced bread
- 45 ml (3 tbsp) brown sugar
- Optional: 2 handfuls of chopped pecans

### Custard

- 480 ml (2 cups) half & half
- 5 eggs
- 45 ml (3 tbsp) brown sugar
- 5 ml (1 tsp) cinnamon
- 5 ml (1 tsp) nutmeg
- 5 ml (1 tsp) vanilla extract
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) brown alcohol (any)
- 45 ml (3 tbsp) melted butter

## Method

1. Slice bread into 1-inch cubes
2. Spread on pan and bake at 77 °C (170 °F) 10 minutes to dry out
3. Grease 9x13 casserole dish
4. Add bread to dish
5. Whisk custard in bowl
6. Pour over all bread, tossing gently to combine
7. Sprinkle with brown sugar (and pecans, if using)
8. Bake at 175 °C (350 °F) for 35-45 minutes until firm
9. Let rest at least 10 minutes before serving with fruit and syrup

Note: can be prepped the night before
