# Fridge Pickled Banana Peppers

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Side, Vegetarian, Vegan
- Image: /images/fridge-pickled-banana-peppers.jpg
- Emoji: 🫑
- Serves: 30

## Description

Quick refrigerator pickles of sliced banana peppers with garlic, celery seed and peppercorns.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Cook: 5 min
Chill: 1 day
Total: 20 min

## Ingredients

- 905 g (2 lb) large banana peppers (can also use other types of peppers or vegetables)
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 960 ml (4 cups) water
- 480 ml (2 cups) white vinegar
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) salt
- 5 ml (1 tsp) celery seeds
- 5 ml (1 tsp) peppercorns

## Method

1. Wash and slice the peppers into ¼ inch rounds
2. Drop into 1 or 2 jars and add garlic
3. Bring the water to a boil
4. Add vinegar and spices to water
5. Stir until dissolved
6. Pour over the peppers to cover
7. Add a lid and refrigerate for at least 2 days
