# Fried Chicken Sandwiches

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main
- Image: /images/fried-chicken-sandwiches.jpg
- Emoji: 🍔
- Serves: 4

## Description

Pickle-brined chicken thighs in a buttermilk-and-flour crust, fried and served on soft potato rolls.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Marinate: 1–12 h
Fry: 20 min
Total: 1 h 40 min

## Ingredients

- 1 jar sliced dill pickles
- 4 boneless skinless chicken thighs, flattened
- Kosher salt & black pepper
- 240 ml (1 cup) buttermilk
- 360 ml (1½ cups) self-rising flour (substitute 360 ml (1½ cups) flour, 15 ml (1 tbsp) baking powder, 5 ml (1 tsp) kosher salt)
- 950 ml (1 quart) frying oil
- 4 soft potato rolls or hamburger buns (toasted)

## Method

### Prep

1. Pour 180 ml (¾ cup) pickle juice into a large plastic baggie
2. Add 15 ml (1 tbsp) salt, 5 ml (1 tsp) pepper, and chicken
3. Seal and let marinate for 1-12 hours
4. Heat oil to 220 °C (425 °F) before cooking

### Directions

1. Remove chicken from bag and discard juice, pat dry with paper towels
2. Pour buttermilk into medium bowl
3. Add flour and 15 ml (1 tbsp) pepper to a second bowl
4. Transfer 45 ml (3 tbsp) buttermilk to flour and work into the flour with fingertips
5. Transfer chicken to buttermilk and turn to coat
6. Transfer chicken to flour and press down for a thick layer of flour on it
7. Move to a plate and repeat for remaining pieces
8. Carefully lower chicken into oil, one piece at a time
9. Cook without moving until well-browned, 2-3 minutes
10. Flip with tongs and cook for 2-3 more minutes
11. Transfer to plate with a paper towel on it for drainage
12. Assemble sandwiches (bun – pickles – chicken – bun)
