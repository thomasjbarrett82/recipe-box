# Fried Rice

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Quick, Vegetarian
- Serves: 3

## Description

Quick fried rice with egg, peas, carrot and scallions; best with cold leftover rice.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Cook: 10 min
Total: 20 min

## Ingredients

- 480 ml (2 cups) cooked white rice (fully cooled)
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) vegetable oil
- 1 small onion, diced
- 1 carrot, diced
- 2 scallions, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 5 ml (1 tsp) soy sauce
- 5 ml (1 tsp) sesame oil
- 1 large egg
- 115 g (4 oz) frozen peas

## Method

1. Break up rice into individual grains
2. Heat 7.5 ml (½ tbsp) oil in large wok until smoking
3. Toast half of the rice in wok, stirring rapidly, until chewy
4. Set aside in a large bowl and repeat with remaining rice
5. Return the first batch of rice and press up the sides
6. Add 7.5 ml (½ tbsp) oil to open space in the middle
7. Add onion, carrot, scallions, and garlic
8. Cook for 1 minute
9. Toss with rice to combine
10. Add soy sauce and sesame oil, and toss to coat
11. Push rice to the side and add remaining 7.5 ml (½ tbsp) oil
12. Break egg into the oil and season with salt
13. Scramble the egg and then toss with rice
14. Add frozen peas and continue to toss until fully thawed
15. Serve immediately
