# Frittata

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Breakfast, Vegetarian
- Image: /images/frittata.jpg
- Serves: 4

## Description

Potato, spinach, pepper and onion frittata finished with fresh mozzarella and basil.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Cook: 25 min
Total: 40 min

## Ingredients

- EVOO or butter
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 720 ml (3 cups) spinach leaves
- 6 eggs
- kosher salt & black pepper
- ½ yellow onion, minced
- 1 bell pepper, minced
- 2 red potatoes, peeled and diced
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) fresh basil, diced
- 60 ml (¼ cup) fresh mozzarella, shredded

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat the broiler with the grill directly underneath

### Directions

1. Sauté the garlic in a large pan until softened
2. Add spinach and cook until wilted
3. Transfer to a bowl and set aside
4. Whisk the eggs with salt and pepper and set aside
5. Sauté the onion until softened
6. Add the bell pepper and potatoes and cook until starting to brown
7. Spread everything evenly in the pan
8. Spread the spinach over the potatoes
9. Sprinkle with basil
10. Pour the eggs over everything and sprinkle with cheese
11. Cook until slightly set (2 minutes)
12. Place under broiler until browned and puffy and completely set
13. Cut into wedges and serve immediately

Feel free to substitute other cheeses, and other vegetables and/or meats for the spinach, onion, and bell pepper
