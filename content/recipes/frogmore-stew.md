# Frogmore Stew

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Stew, Spicy
- Image: /images/frogmore-stew.jpg
- Serves: 10

## Description

Lowcountry boil of potatoes, spicy sausage, corn and shrimp cooked in seasoned beer broth.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Cook: 40 min
Total: 55 min

## Ingredients

- 3.8 L (4 quarts) water
- 1 bottle beer
- 180 ml (¾ cup) Old Bay or other seafood seasoning
- 905 g (2 lb) red potatoes, diced
- 905 g (2 lb) spicy sausage, chopped
- 12 ears corn, husked and quartered
- 905 g (2 lb) large shrimp, peeled & deveined, tails removed
- 905 g (2 lb) crab legs or other desired seafood

## Method

1. Heat water, beer, and Old Bay in large pot until boiling
2. Add potatoes and cook 15 minutes
3. Add sausage and cook for 5 minutes
4. Add corn and cook for 10 minutes
5. Add seafood and cook for 5 minutes, until done
6. Drain and serve
