# Frozen Corn

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Side, Vegetarian, Vegan, Minnesota
- Source: Grandma Judy Munson
- Serves: 32

## Description

How to blanch and freeze fresh sweet corn at the height of the season.

## Cook Times

Prep: 1 h
Cook: 10 min
Total: 1 h 10 min

## Ingredients

- 3.8 L (16 cups) fresh corn, cut off the cob
- 720 ml (3 cups) water
- 180 ml (3/4 cup) sugar
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) kosher salt

## Method

1. Stir all ingredients in a large bowl
2. Separate into containers and freeze
