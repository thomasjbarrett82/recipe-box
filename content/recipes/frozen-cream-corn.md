# Frozen Cream Corn

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Side, Vegetarian, Minnesota
- Source: Grandma Judy Munson
- Image: /images/frozen-cream-corn.jpg
- Emoji: 🌽
- Serves: 36

## Description

Fresh corn baked with butter and half and half, then frozen in portions.

## Cook Times

Prep: 1 h
Bake: 1 h
Total: 2 h

## Ingredients

- 3.8 L (16 cups) fresh corn, cut off the cob
- 455 g (1 lb) butter
- 470 ml (1 pint) half and half

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)

### Directions

1. Stir all ingredients in a large roaster or dish
2. Bake for 1 hour
3. Let cool and move to fridge to chill for an hour
4. Separate into containers and freeze
