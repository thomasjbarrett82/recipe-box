# Garlic Angel Hair Chicken

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Pasta
- Serves: 6

## Description

Angel hair with chicken, broccoli, carrots and lots of garlic in a light broth sauce.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Cook: 20 min
Total: 35 min

## Ingredients

- 15 ml (1 tbsp) + 15 ml (1 tbsp) olive oil
- 905 g (2 lb) chicken breast, cubed
- 1 carrot, sliced thin
- 1 package broccoli, thawed
- 4-6 cloves garlic, minced
- 340 g (12 oz) angel hair pasta, cooked
- 240 ml (1 cup) chicken broth
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) basil
- ¼ Parmesan (optional)

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat large pan over med-high heat

### Directions

1. Heat 15 ml (1 tbsp) oil in pan
2. Cook chicken until browned, remove and set aside
3. Heat remaining oil & cook carrots for 5 minutes
4. Add garlic, broccoli, and cook for 5 more minutes
5. Add broth, basil, and Parmesan
6. Return chicken to pan
7. Simmer for 5 minutes
8. Serve over the pasta
