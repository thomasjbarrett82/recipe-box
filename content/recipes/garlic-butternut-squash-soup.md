# Garlic Butternut Squash Soup

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Soup, Vegetarian, Vegan, High fiber
- Image: /images/garlic-butternut-squash-soup.jpg
- Serves: 6

## Description

Roasted butternut squash and garlic blended into a smooth soup.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Cook: 1 h 10 min
Total: 1 h 25 min

## Ingredients

- 2 medium butternut squash (or other winter squash)
- 12-15 cloves garlic, peeled
- 2 + 30 ml (2 tbsp) olive oil
- ½ yellow onion
- 1.4 L (6 cups) vegetable broth
- Salt & pepper
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) sliced green onion

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)

### Directions

1. Cut squash in half length-wise
2. Place on baking pan cut-side up and drizzle with 30 ml (2 tbsp) olive oil
3. Toss garlic and onion with remaining oil
4. Spread on baking pan around squash
5. Roast until golden brown, 50-60 minutes
6. Remove from oven and let cool
7. Chop onion and remove squash flesh from skin and chop
8. Transfer to large pot and add broth
9. Puree with an immersion blender until barely chunky
10. OR use food processor or blender
11. Add green onions
12. Season to taste
