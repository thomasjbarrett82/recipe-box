# Garlic Parmesan Butter Beans

- Source: Tom

- Emoji: 🫘
- Serves: 6

## Description

Butter beans simmered in a creamy garlic, Parmesan and cream cheese sauce.

## Cook Times

Prep: 5 min
Cook: 20 min
Total: 25 min

## Ingredients

- 30 ml (2 tbsp) butter
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 5 ml (1 tsp) Italian seasoning
- 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) black pepper
- 5 ml (1 tsp) smoked paprika
- 240 ml (1 cup) broth
- 115 g (4 oz) cream cheese (room temperature)
- 2 (425 g / 15 oz) cans butter beans, drained and rinsed
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) parmesan

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat large pan over medium heat

### Directions

1. Melt butter and add seasonings for 1 minute
2. Stir in broth and cream cheese until melted
3. Stir in beans and simmer uncovered for 10-15 minutes
4. Stir in parmesan and serve warm
