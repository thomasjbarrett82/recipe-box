# General Tso's Chicken

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Spicy, Dutch oven, Favorite
- Image: /images/general-tsos-chicken.jpg
- Emoji: 🥡
- Serves: 4

## Description

Crispy fried chicken in a sweet, tangy and spicy General Tso's sauce.

## Cook Times

Prep: 30 min
Fry: 20 min
Total: 50 min

## Ingredients

### Marinade

- 1 egg white
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) soy sauce
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) white wine
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) vodka
- 1.25 ml (¼ tsp) baking soda
- 45 ml (3 tbsp) cornstarch
- 455 g (1 lb) boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into ½ - ¾ inch chunks

### Dry Coat

- 120 ml (½ cup) flour
- 120 ml (½ cup) cornstarch
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) baking powder
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) kosher salt

### Sauce

- 45 ml (3 tbsp) soy sauce
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) white wine
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) rice vinegar
- 45 ml (3 tbsp) chicken stock
- 60 ml (4 tbsp) sugar
- 5 ml (1 tsp) sesame oil
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) cornstarch
- 10 ml (2 tsp) + 1.4 L (1.5 quarts) oil
- 10 ml (2 tsp) minced garlic
- 10 ml (2 tsp) minced ginger
- 10 ml (2 tsp) minced scallions
- 8 small dried red Chinese or Arbol chilies (or 1.25 ml (¼ tsp) dried red pepper flakes)

## Method

1. Whisk marinade ingredients together, set aside half
2. Add chicken and set aside
3. Combine dry coat ingredients, then add half of marinade and whisk until clumpy
4. Whisk soy sauce, wine, vinegar, stock, sugar, sesame oil, & cornstarch in a small bowl
5. Combine 10 ml (2 tsp) oil, garlic, ginger, scallions, & chilies in large skillet
6. Cook over medium heat 3-4 minutes until soft and aromatic
7. Add sauce mixture and stir until boiling and thick
8. Transfer to a bowl to stop cooking, but don’t wipe out the skillet
9. Heat 1.4 L (1.5 quarts) oil to 175 °C (350 °F) in a large wok or Dutch oven
10. Transfer chicken from marinade to dry coat, pressing to fully coat
11. Add chicken to oil one piece at a time
12. Cook until crispy, about 4 minutes each, and move to towel-lined plate to drain
13. Return chicken to empty skillet and add the sauce
14. Toss until fully coated
15. Serve over white rice
