# Goulash Supreme

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Pasta, Dutch oven, Minnesota
- Serves: 8

## Description

American goulash: ground beef and macaroni in a seasoned tomato sauce, made in one pot.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Cook: 50 min
Rest: 30 min
Total: 1 h 35 min

## Ingredients

- 905 g (2 lb) lean ground beef
- 2 onions, chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 can chicken stock
- 2 (425 g / 15 oz) cans tomato sauce
- 2 (425 g / 15 oz) cans diced tomatoes
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) Italian seasoning
- 3 bay leaves
- 45 ml (3 tbsp) soy sauce
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) kosher salt
- 5 ml (1 tsp) black pepper
- 5 ml (1 tsp) seasoned salt
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) smoked paprika
- 480 ml (2 cups) uncooked elbow macaroni

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat Dutch oven over med-high

### Directions

1. Brown the ground beef
2. Transfer beef to a bowl on the side
3. Add onions and garlic to the drippings and sauté until tender
4. Return beef and add everything except the pasta to the pot
5. Cover the pot and let cook for 15-20 minutes
6. Add the pasta and turn down to a simmer for 30 minutes
7. Remove from heat and remove the bay leaves
8. Let rest for 30 minutes before serving
