# Graham Cracker Pie

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert, Minnesota, Favorite
- Image: /images/graham-cracker-pie.jpg
- Emoji: 🥧
- Serves: 8

## Description

An old-fashioned pie of vanilla custard in a graham cracker crust, topped with meringue and more crumbs.

## Cook Times

Prep: 30 min
Bake: 30 min
Chill: 4 h
Total: 5 h

## Ingredients

### Crust

- 420 ml (1 ¾ cups) graham cracker crumbs
- 120 ml (8 tbsp, 1 stick) butter, melted
- 80 ml (1/3 cup) sugar

### Filling

- 240 ml (1 cup) sugar
- 60 ml (¼ cup) cornstarch
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) kosher salt
- 480 ml (2 cups) whole milk
- 4 large egg yolks
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) butter, soft and cut into pieces
- 10 ml (2 tsp) vanilla extract

### Meringue

- 4 large egg whites
- 1.25 ml (¼ tsp) cream of tarter OR white vinegar
- 90 ml (6 tbsp) sugar
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) vanilla extract

## Method

### Prep

1. Separate egg whites from yolks
2. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)

### Directions

1. In medium bowl mix crumbs, butter, and sugar, and reserve 30 ml (2 tbsp)
2. Spread remaining crust in pie pan and bake for 10 minutes
3. In heavy saucepan mix sugar, cornstarch, salt, milk, and egg yolks
4. Place over medium heat and whisk until gently boiling and thick (5-6 mins)
5. Remove from heat and whisk in butter and vanilla
6. Pour filling into crust and set aside
7. In mixing bowl beat egg whites until foamy
8. Add cream of tarter and sugar, continue mixing until stiff peaks form
9. Gently fold in the vanilla extract and spread mixture over pie filling
10. Sprinkle with reserved crumbs and bake for 12-15 mins until lightly browned
11. Chill in fridge for at least 4 hours before serving
