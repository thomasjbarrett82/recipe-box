# Grandma Kleven's Flatbread

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Bread, Holiday, Minnesota
- Source: Judy Munson
- Serves: 16

## Description

Grandma Kleven's thin, crisp Norwegian flatbread made with buttermilk.

## Cook Times

Prep: 1 h
Bake: 1 h
Total: 2 h

## Ingredients

- 240 ml (1 cup) butter, melted
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) sugar
- 240 ml (1 cup) buttermilk
- 1.25 ml (1/4 tsp) salt
- 480 ml (2 cups) flour

## Method

### Prep

1. Mix ingredients together and put in fridge overnight
2. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)

### Directions

1. Roll in flour until thin
2. Bake on cookie sheet until browned
3. Cut while still warm
