# Grandma Millie's Tea

## Description

Grandma Millie's spiced instant tea mix of Tang, tea, lemonade and cinnamon. Stir a spoonful into hot water.

## Cook Times

Prep: 5 min
Total: 5 min

## Ingredients

- 480 ml (2 cups) Tang
- 240 ml (1 cup) sugar
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) instant black tea
- 1 package unsweetened lemonade mix
- 5 ml (1 tsp) cinnamon
- 1.25 ml (1/4 tsp) nutmet

## Method

### Prep

1. Mix all ingredients and store in airtight container

### Directions

1. Mix to taste in a cup of hot water
