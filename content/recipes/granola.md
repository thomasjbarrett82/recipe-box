# Granola

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Breakfast, Vegetarian
- Source: Judy Munson
- Image: /images/granola.jpg
- Serves: 16

## Description

Homemade granola with oats, almonds, coconut, flax and dried fruit, sweetened with honey.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Bake: 20–30 min
Total: 40 min

## Ingredients

- 960 ml (4 cups) old-fashioned oats
- 240 ml (1 cup) sliced almonds
- 240 ml (1 cup) shredded coconut
- 120 ml (½ cup) ground flax or wheat germ
- 5 ml (1 tsp) cinnamon
- 240 ml (1 cup) dried fruit
- 60 ml (¼ cup) EVOO
- 120 ml (½ cup) honey
- 5 ml (1 tsp) vanilla extract
- 5 ml (1 tsp) kosher salt

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 150 °C (300 °F)

### Directions

1. Mix dry ingredients in large bowl
2. Mix wet ingredients in medium bowl to combine
3. Pour over dry ingredients and fold together
4. Bake for 20-30 minutes until browned, stirring frequently
5. Remove and let cool
6. Store in airtight container
