# Grape Salad

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Side, Vegetarian, Minnesota, Favorite
- Image: /images/grape-salad.jpg
- Emoji: 🍇
- Serves: 16

## Description

Grapes folded into sweetened sour cream and cream cheese, topped with brown sugar and walnuts.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Chill: 1 h
Total: 1 h 15 min

## Ingredients

- 225 g (8 oz) sour cream
- 225 g (8 oz) cream cheese, softened
- 120 ml (½ cup) sugar
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) vanilla extract
- 905 g (2 lb) black & green grapes
- 120 ml (½ cup) brown sugar
- 120 ml (½ cup) walnuts, chopped

## Method

1. Add the cream cheese, sour cream, sugar, and vanilla to a large mixing bowl
2. Blend with a mixer until smooth and creamy
3. Fold in grapes until well combined
4. Top with brown sugar and walnuts
5. Let chill for at least an hour before serving
