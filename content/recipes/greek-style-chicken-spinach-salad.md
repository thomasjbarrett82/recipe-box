# Greek-style Chicken Spinach Salad

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, High fiber
- Source: The Plant Slant
- Image: /images/greek-style-chicken-spinach-salad.jpg
- Emoji: 🥗
- Serves: 6

## Description

Meal-prep salad of rotisserie chicken, spinach, chickpeas, rice and quinoa with a Greek yogurt dressing.

## Cook Times

Prep: 30 min
Cook: 20 min
Total: 50 min

## Ingredients

- 1 whole rotisserie chicken
- 1 large red onion, diced
- 2 large cucumbers, sliced
- 1 bag spinach, washed and rough chopped (~ 720 ml (3 cups))
- 480 ml (2 cups) cherry tomatoes
- 2 cans chickpeas, drained
- 360 ml (1.5 cups) white rice, cooked (180 ml (3/4 cup) uncooked)
- 360 ml (1.5 cups) quinoa, cooked (240 ml (1 cup) uncooked)
- 85 g (3 oz) package crumbled feta

### Dressing

- 360 ml (1.5 cups) plain Greek yogurt
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) honey
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) lemon juice
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) oregano
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) garlic powder
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) cumin
- dash of salt and pepper

## Method

### Prep

1. Break down the chicken, reserving bones and skin
2. Place bones and skin in pot and cover with water
3. Bring to boil, cover with lid and turn down to low
4. Simmer for 2 hours and strain out solids

### Directions

1. Cook quinoa and rice in rice cooker with 720 ml (3 cups) chicken stock
2. Meanwhile, combine dressing ingredients in bowl, adding a little water if too thick
3. Once grains are finished, combine everything in a very large bowl
4. Divide into meal prep containers and refrigerate
