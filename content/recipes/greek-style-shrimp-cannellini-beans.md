# Greek-style Shrimp & Cannellini Beans

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, High fiber
- Image: /images/greek-style-shrimp-cannellini-beans.jpg
- Serves: 4

## Description

Shrimp roasted with cannellini beans, cherry tomatoes, green beans and olives, finished with feta.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Bake: 15–20 min
Total: 35 min

## Ingredients

- 455 g (1 lb) shrimp, peeled, deveined, & detailed
- 1 can cannellini or northern beans, rinsed & drained
- 470 ml (1 pint) cherry tomatoes, halved
- 170 g (6 oz) fresh green beans, halved
- 60 ml (¼ cup) Kalamata olives, sliced
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) olive oil
- 10 ml (2 tsp) lemon zest
- 5 ml (1 tsp) minced garlic
- 1.25 ml (¼ tsp) oregano
- 1.25 ml (¼ tsp) kosher salt & black pepper
- Garnish: crumbled feta cheese

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 260 °C (500 °F)

### Directions

1. Lay out 4 large pieces of aluminum foil
2. Put all ingredients in large bowl & toss to mix
3. Place ¼ of shrimp mixture in each foil and wrap up
4. Place packets on large baking pan
5. Bake in oven for 15-20 minutes until cooked through
6. Transfer to serving plates and garnish with feta cheese
