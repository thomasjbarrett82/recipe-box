# Green Chicken Chili

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Stew, Spicy, Dutch oven, High fiber
- Source: Closet Cooking
- Image: /images/green-chicken-chili.jpg
- Serves: 6

## Description

Chicken and white bean chili with salsa verde, jalapeño, zucchini and corn.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Cook: 40 min
Total: 55 min

## Ingredients

- 15 ml (1 tbsp) EVOO
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 jalapeno, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) ground cumin
- 720–960 ml (3–4 cups) chicken broth
- 480 ml (2 cups) salsa verde
- 455 g (1 lb) chicken, shredded
- 2 (425 g / 15 oz) cans white beans, drained and rinsed
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) oregano
- 1 medium zucchini, shredded or diced
- 285 g (10 oz) bag frozen corn
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) fresh cilantro, chopped (optional)

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat large pan or Dutch oven over med-high

### Directions

1. Heat EVOO and add onion & jalapeno for 5 minutes
2. Add garlic and cumin for 1 minute
3. Add broth, salsa, chicken, beans, oregano
4. Bring to a boil and then simmer for 20 minutes
5. Add zucchini and corn and simmer for 10 minutes
6. Season to taste and serve
