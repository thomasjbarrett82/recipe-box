# Guacamole

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Appetizer, Quick, Vegetarian, Vegan, Favorite
- Image: /images/guacamole.jpg
- Serves: 8

## Description

Chunky guacamole with garlic, red onion, jalapeño, cilantro and a little cumin.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Total: 15 min

## Ingredients

- 3 avocados
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- ½ red onion, minced
- 1 jalapeno, minced
- 60 ml (¼ cup) cilantro, chopped
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) lemon juice
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) cumin
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) chili powder
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) black pepper
- Kosher salt

## Method

1. Pit and mash the avocado in a bowl
2. Stir in the remaining ingredients
3. Salt to taste
