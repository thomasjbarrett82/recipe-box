# Halal Cart Chicken and Rice with White Sauce

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Dutch oven
- Image: /images/halal-cart-chicken-rice-white-sauce.jpg
- Serves: 6

## Description

Street-cart chicken marinated in lemon, oregano and cumin, over turmeric rice with creamy white sauce.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Marinate: 1–4 h
Cook: 35 min
Total: 1 h 55 min

## Ingredients

### Chicken

- 30 ml (2 tbsp) lemon juice
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) fresh oregano
- 5 ml (1 tsp) cumin
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 120 ml (½ cup) EVOO
- Salt & pepper
- 905 g (2 lb) boneless skinless chicken, chopped
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) EVOO

### Rice

- 30 ml (2 tbsp) butter
- 5 ml (1 tsp) turmeric
- 5 ml (1 tsp) cumin
- 360 ml (1 ½ cups) white rice
- 600 ml (2 ½ cups) chicken broth

### White Sauce

- 120 ml (½ cup) mayo
- 120 ml (½ cup) plain Greek yogurt
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) sugar
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) vinegar
- 5 ml (1 tsp) lemon juice
- 60 ml (¼ cup) fresh parsley
- Salt & pepper

### Serving

- Shredded lettuce
- 2 large tomatoes, diced
- Pita bread, toasted, buttered, and sliced

## Method

### Prep

1. Blend marinade and reserve half for later
2. Marinate chicken for 1-4 hours before cooking
3. Combine white sauce ingredients in a bowl and refrigerate

### Directions

1. Heat some oil in a large skillet or Dutch Oven over med-high heat
2. Cook chicken until well browned
3. Remove and set aside with the reserved marinade
4. Melt butter in skillet
5. Add spices & rice and stir to combine and lightly toast
6. Add chicken broth and bring to a boil
7. Cover and simmer for 15 minutes
8. Remove from heat and let sit another 10-15 minutes until fully absorbed
9. Return chicken and juices to the skillet and cook until heated through
10. Serve with lettuce, tomatoes, pita slices, and white sauce
