# Ham & Bean Soup

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Soup, Slow cooker, High fiber
- Image: /images/ham-bean-soup.jpg
- Serves: 10

## Description

Ham and 15-bean soup slow-cooked with tomatoes, liquid smoke and a little hot sauce.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Soak: overnight
Slow cook: 6 h
Total: 6 h 15 min

## Ingredients

- 905 g–1.4 kg (2–3 lb) ham, shredded or diced
- 1 sweet onion, diced
- 395 g (14 oz) can Italian-style diced tomato
- 1 bag 15 bean mix (with seasoning)
- 1.9 L (8 cups) chicken stock
- 5 ml (1 tsp) Liquid Smoke
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) hot sauce
- 2 cloves garlic, minced

## Method

### Prep

1. Cover beans with 2 inches of water, let sit overnight, drain and rinse

### Directions

1. Add all ingredients into crockpot
2. Cook on LOW for 6 hours
