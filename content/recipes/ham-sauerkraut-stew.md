# Ham and Sauerkraut Stew

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Stew, Dutch oven, High fiber
- Image: /images/ham-sauerkraut-stew.jpg
- Emoji: 🍖
- Serves: 5

## Description

Ham, bacon, potatoes and sauerkraut stewed with kidney beans and carrots.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Cook: 50 min
Total: 1 h 10 min

## Ingredients

- 115 g (4 oz) bacon, chopped
- 340 g (12 oz) ham, diced
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) butter
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 onions, chopped
- 2 potatoes, peeled & chopped
- 1 large carrot, chopped
- 1 bay leaf
- 960 ml (4 cups) chicken stock
- 480 ml (2 cups) homemade sauerkraut (or 1 (425 g / 15 oz) can)
- 1 (425 g / 15 oz) white or red kidney beans, drained & rinsed
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) Italian seasoning
- Kosher salt & black pepper

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat Dutch oven over med-high heat

### Directions

1. Brown the bacon, remove and set aside
2. Brown the ham in the bacon drippings, remove and set aside
3. Add butter to drippings
4. Add garlic, onions, potatoes, carrots, & bay leaf
5. Cover and cook to soften for 10 minutes
6. Return meat to pot, add stock, sauerkraut, beans, & seasoning
7. Cover and simmer for another 30 minutes
8. Season with salt & pepper before serving
