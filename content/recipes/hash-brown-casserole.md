# Hash Brown Casserole

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Side, Holiday, Minnesota, Favorite
- Image: /images/hash-brown-casserole.jpg
- Serves: 12

## Description

Cheesy hash brown casserole with sour cream and a crunchy corn flake topping.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Bake: 55 min
Total: 1 h 10 min

## Ingredients

- 905 g (2 lb) hash browns, thawed
- 1 can cream of mushroom soup
- 60 ml (¼ cup) + 30 ml (2 tbsp) butter, melted
- 470 ml (1 pint) sour cream
- 120 ml (½ cup) milk
- 120 ml (½ cup) green onions, chopped
- 5 ml (1 tsp) salt
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) pepper
- 5 ml (1 tsp) oregano
- 480 ml (2 cups) shredded Cheddar
- 240 ml (1 cup) corn flakes, crushed

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)

### Directions

1. Pour 60 ml (¼ cup) butter into 9x13” baking dish and spread around
2. In medium bowl, mix soup, sour cream, milk, onions, and spices
3. Layer ½ of hash browns, sour cream mix, and cheese in dish
4. Layer remaining ingredients on top
5. Cover with crushed cornflakes
6. Drizzle with 30 ml (2 tbsp) butter
7. Bake for 55 minutes
