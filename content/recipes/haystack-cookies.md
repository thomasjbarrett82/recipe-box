# Haystack Cookies

- Serves: 15

## Description

No-bake clusters of chow mein noodles and peanuts in melted chocolate and butterscotch.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Set: 30 min
Total: 45 min

## Ingredients

- 170 g (6 oz) chocolate chips
- 170 g (6 oz) butterscotch chips
- 140 g (5 oz) chow mein noodles
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) salted peanuts

## Method

1. Melt chips in double boiler
2. Remove from heat
3. Add noodles & peanuts
4. Dollop onto waxed paper to cool before serving
