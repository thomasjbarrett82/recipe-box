# High Fiber Gluten Free Bread

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Bread, Gluten free
- Source: hallysdailylife
- Image: /images/high-fiber-gluten-free-bread.jpg
- Serves: 12

## Description

A sandwich loaf made with psyllium husk and gluten-free flour.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Rise: 1–2 h
Bake: 40–60 min
Total: 2 h 30 min

## Ingredients

- 60 ml (1/4 cup) psyllium husk
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) apple cider vinegar
- 240 ml (1 cup) water
- 10 ml (2 tsp) active dry yeast
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) honey
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) warm water
- 480 ml (2 cups) GF baking flour
- 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) baking powder
- 5 ml (1 tsp) kosher salt

## Method

1. Soak psyllium, vinegar, water in bowl to form gel
2. Soak yeast, honey, water in bowl and let bloom
3. Mix flour, baking powder, salt in medium bowl
4. Mix the two wet bowls into the dry
5. Knead dough for 5 mins and let rest for 1-2 hours
6. Heat oven to 205 °C (400 °F)
7. Transfer dough to oiled & floured bread pan
8. Bake at 205 °C (400 °F) for 40-60 mins until done
9. Let cool 10 mins before serving
