# Home-made Bailey's

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Drink, Quick, Holiday
- Image: /images/homemade-baileys.jpg
- Serves: 21

## Description

Homemade Irish cream liqueur blended from cream, sweetened condensed milk, Irish whiskey, coffee and cocoa.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Total: 10 min

## Ingredients

- 240 ml (1 cup) heavy cream
- 1 (395 g / 14 oz) can sweetened condensed milk
- 400 ml (1 2/3 cups) Irish whiskey
- 5 ml (1 tsp) instant coffee
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) cocoa powder
- 5 ml (1 tsp) vanilla extract
- 5 ml (1 tsp) almond extract (optional)

## Method

1. Blend all ingredients on high for 1 minute.
2. Store in fridge in sealed container.
3. Shake before using.
4. Will keep for up to 2 months.
