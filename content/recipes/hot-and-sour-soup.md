# Hot and Sour Soup

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Soup, Spicy
- Source: Tom
- Image: /images/hot-and-sour-soup.jpg
- Emoji: 🍲
- Serves: 7

## Description

Hot and sour soup with mushrooms, bamboo shoots, water chestnuts, tofu and egg ribbons, spiked with chili garlic sauce.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Cook: 20 min
Total: 35 min

## Ingredients

- 1.9 L (8 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
- 225 g (8 oz) shiitake or baby bella mushroom, thinly sliced
- 225 g (8 oz) can bamboo shoots
- 225 g (8 oz) can water chestnuts
- 60 ml (1/4 cup) soy sauce
- 10 ml (2 tsp) ground ginger
- 5 ml (1 tsp) garlic powder
- 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) chili garlic sauce
- 10 ml (2 tsp) kosher salt or 5 ml (1 tsp) MSG
- 60 ml (1/4 cup) cornstarch
- 2 eggs, whisked
- 225 g (8 oz) firm tofu (or cooked chicken or pork), cut into 1/4" cubes or matchsticks
- 8 green onions, thinly sliced
- 5 ml (1 tsp) sesame oil
- 60 ml (1/4 cup) rice vinegar

## Method

### Prep

1. Reserve 60 ml (1/4 cup) broth and whisk together with cornstarch for later
2. Place pot over medium heat

### Directions

1. Add broth, mushrooms, bamboo, chestnuts, soy sauce, and seasonings to pot
2. Bring to a low boil
3. Add cornstarch mixture and stir for 1-2 minutes until thickened
4. Stir in circular motion and drizzle eggs to make ribbons
5. Add remaining ingredients and serve
