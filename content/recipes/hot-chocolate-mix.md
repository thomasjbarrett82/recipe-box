# Hot Chocolate Mix

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Drink, Quick, Holiday
- Image: /images/hot-chocolate-mix.jpg
- Serves: 32

## Description

A big batch of hot cocoa mix from powdered milk, creamer, sugar and chocolate drink mix.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Total: 10 min

## Ingredients

- 170 g (6 oz) Coffee mate
- 120 ml (½ cup) powdered sugar
- 710 g (25 oz) powdered milk (makes 7.6 L (8 quarts))
- 455 g (1 lb) powdered chocolate drink mix

## Method

### Prep

1. Mix all ingredients together
2. Store in airtight container

### Directions

1. Add 60 ml (¼ cup) mix to 240 ml (1 cup) hot water or milk
