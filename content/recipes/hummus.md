# Hummus

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Appetizer, Quick, Vegetarian, Vegan
- Image: /images/hummus.jpg
- Serves: 7

## Description

Smooth hummus with tahini or sunflower butter, lemon, garlic, cumin and cilantro.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Total: 10 min

## Ingredients

- 1 can garbanzo beans, drained and rinsed
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) tahini OR sunflower butter
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) lemon juice
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 5 ml (1 tsp) cumin
- 5 ml (1 tsp) cilantro
- Salt & pepper to taste
- 60 ml (¼ cup) olive oil

## Method

1. Place all ingredients in blender or food processor
2. Blend thoroughly
3. Add more oil to get desired consistency
4. Serve with crackers, tortilla chips, or toasted pita bread
