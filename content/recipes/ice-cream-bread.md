# Ice Cream Bread

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Bread
- Source: @bdylanhollis
- Serves: 10

## Description

A three-ingredient quick bread made from melted ice cream, flour and baking powder.

## Cook Times

Prep: 5 min
Bake: 40–45 min
Total: 50 min

## Ingredients

- 480 ml (2 cups) full fat ice cream, softened
- 360 ml (1 1/2 cups) flour
- 7.5 ml (1 1/2 tsp) baking powder

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)
2. Grease bread pan

### Directions

1. Mix ice cream, flour, & baking powder in large bowl
2. Transfer to bread pan
3. Bake for 40-45 minutes
4. Let cool before serving
