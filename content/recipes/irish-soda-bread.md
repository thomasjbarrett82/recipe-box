# Irish Soda Bread

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Bread, Holiday
- Image: /images/irish-soda-bread.jpg
- Emoji: 🍀
- Serves: 12

## Description

Rustic, crusty soda bread made with buttermilk and no yeast, with optional oats, raisins, seeds or herbs.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Bake: 40 min
Cool: 30 min
Total: 1 h 25 min

## Ingredients

- 565 g A/P flour + 30–45 ml (2–3 tbsp) for dusting
- 7.5 ml (1.5 tsp) baking soda
- 7.5 ml (1.5 tsp) kosher salt
- 480 ml (2 cups) buttermilk
- OR 460 ml (1.9 cups) milk + 15 ml (1 tbsp) vinegar, mix and let sit 10 minutes

### Optional ingredients

- Add 240 ml (1 cup) oats and reduce flour by 120 ml (½ cup)
- Raisins
- Seeds
- Herbs

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 230 °C (450 °F)

### Directions

1. Line tray with baking paper, or rub oil on inside of cast iron pan
2. Whisk flour, baking soda, salt in a large mixing bowl
3. Add buttermilk and stir until too hard to stir any more
4. Dust work surface with flour and move dough from bowl
5. Sprinkle with more flour
6. Gently knead no more than 8 times
7. Use only enough added flour to make the dough manageable
8. Bring together into a ball
9. Transfer to tray/pan and pat into 1” thick disk
10. Cross cut the top
11. Bake 20 minutes
12. Turn down oven to 200 °C (390 °F)
13. Bake 20 minutes until bread sounds hollow when tapped
14. Transfer to cooling rack
15. Let cool at least 30 minutes before slicing
