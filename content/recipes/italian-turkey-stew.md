# Italian Turkey Stew

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Stew, Dutch oven, High fiber
- Source: Mayo Clinic
- Image: /images/italian-turkey-stew.jpg
- Emoji: 🦃
- Serves: 5

## Description

Ground turkey, zucchini, carrots and cannellini beans in a tomato and herb stock, with baby spinach stirred in.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Cook: 30 min
Total: 45 min

## Ingredients

- 30 ml (2 tbsp) EVOO
- 455 g (1 lb) ground turkey
- 1 onion, diced
- 4 stalks celery, diced
- 2 medium carrots, thinly sliced
- 3-4 medium zucchini, shredded
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) dried mixed herbs
- 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) black pepper
- 480 ml (2 cups) chicken stock
- 395 g (14 oz) tomatoes, diced
- 395 g (14 oz) cannellini beans, rinsed
- 480 ml (2 cups) Baby spinach, shreddedb

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat large pot or Dutch oven over medium heat

### Directions

1. Heat EVOO in pot
2. Cook turkey until browned and crumlbed, remove and set aside
3. Add onion, carrots, celery to pan and cook for 5 minutes
4. Add zucchini, herbs, and black pepper and cook for 2-3 minutes
5. Add stock, tomatoes, beans
6. Bring to boil and reduce heat to cook for 10-15 minutes
7. Stir in spinach and cook for 3-5 minutes until wilted
8. Serve with Parmesan
