# Jambalaya

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Stew, Spicy, Dutch oven, Favorite
- Image: /images/jambalaya.jpg
- Emoji: 🥘
- Serves: 8

## Description

Oven-baked jambalaya with andouille, chicken and shrimp, peppers, celery and cayenne.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Cook: 1 h 5 min
Total: 1 h 25 min

## Ingredients

- 15 ml (1 tbsp) lard or EVOO
- 225–455 g (½–1 lb) Andouille sausage, sliced
- 455 g (1 lb) chicken thighs, de-boned & chopped (optional)
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 240 ml (1 cup) celery, diced
- 2 bell peppers, diced
- 60 ml (4 tbsp) garlic, minced
- 480 ml (2 cups) chicken stock
- 1 can V8
- 5 ml (1 tsp) oregano
- 5 ml (1 tsp) thyme
- 10 ml (2 tsp) smoked paprika
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) cayenne pepper
- 1 can diced tomatoes, with liquid
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) hot sauce
- 480 ml (2 cups) uncooked rice
- 455 g (1 lb) shrimp, uncooked, peeled and de-veined, tail-off
- Salt & pepper, to taste

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat large Dutch oven over medium high heat
2. Heat oven to 165 °C (325 °F)

### Directions

1. Brown the sausage in lard and remove
2. Brown the chicken, chop and remove
3. Deglaze with a little stock
4. Add onion, celery, & bell peppers and cook until translucent
5. Add stock, V8, seasonings, and rice to the pot
6. Return meat to the pot and stir
7. Bring to a boil and transfer to the oven
8. Bake for 45 minutes until liquid is absorbed and rice is tender
9. Stir in shrimp and cook for another 5 minutes
10. Remove bay leaves and let rest, covered, for 10-15 minutes
11. Serve with additional hot sauce
