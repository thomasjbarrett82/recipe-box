# Kimchi

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Side, Spicy, Favorite
- Source: <https://mykoreankitchen.com/kimchi-recipe/>
- Image: /images/kimchi.jpg
- Serves: 12

## Description

Homemade napa cabbage kimchi with gochugaru, garlic, ginger, daikon and carrot.

## Cook Times

Prep: 45 min
Salt: 1–3 h
Ferment: 24–48 h
Total: 27 h

## Ingredients

- 1 kg napa cabbage
- 50 g kosher salt
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) cornstarch
- 80 ml (1/3 cup) water
- 80 ml (1/3 cup) gochugaru flakes
- 5 ml (1 tsp) kosher salt
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) fish sauce or salted shrimp
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) minced garlic
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) sugar
- 5 ml (1 tsp) minced ginger
- 240 ml (1 cup) julienned daikon radish
- 240 ml (1 cup) julienned carrots
- 60 ml (1/4 cup) minced chives or green onions

## Method

### Prep

1. Chop cabbage into quarters and rinse with running water
2. Chop into smaller pieces and place in large bowl
3. Use hands to cover every piece with kosher salt
4. Set aside for 1-3 hours

### Directions

1. Move cabbage to a colander and rinse with water
2. Make a cornstarch and water slurry
3. Mix with gochugaru, salt, fish sauce, garlic, sugar, ginger
4. Stir in radish, carrots, and onions
5. Move cabbage back to large bowl
6. Add kimchi sauce and mix with gloved hands to coat everything
7. Transfer to an airtight container
8. Put lid on but don't tighten to allow for gas to escape
9. Leave out at room temperature for 24-48 hours
10. Transfer to fridge and let sit for 3+ days before eating
