# Korean Beef Bowl (IP)

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Spicy, Instant Pot
- Source: Two Sleevers
- Image: /images/korean-beef-bowl-ip.jpg
- Emoji: 🍚
- Serves: 4

## Description

Ground beef cooked in a gochujang, soy and sesame sauce in the Instant Pot, with vegetables over rice.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Cook: 25 min
Total: 35 min

## Ingredients

### Sauce

- 45 ml (3 tbsp) gochujang
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) soy sauce
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) sesame oil
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) sugar
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) vinegar
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) water

### Beef bowls

- 455 g (1 lb) ground beef
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) soy sauce
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) sesame oil
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 60 ml (1/4 cup) water
- 720–960 ml (3–4 cups) chopped or shredded vegetables (cabbage, spinach, bell pepper, onion, broccoli, bean sprouts, etc)
- 480 ml (2 cups) rice, cooked
- Optional: pickled onions and/or cucumbers, kimchi, fried egg

## Method

1. Mix sauce in a bowl and set aside
2. Set IP to saute and cook beef
3. Add in soy, sesame, garlic, water
4. Wrap vegetables in foil packet and place on meat, on rack if you have one
5. Close lid and cook on high for 4 minutes
6. Natural release for 10 minutes and then quick release
7. Stir vegetables and rice into beef mixture
8. Serve with gochujang and optional ingredients
