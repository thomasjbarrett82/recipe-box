# Korean Coleslaw

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Side, Spicy, Vegetarian, Vegan
- Source: MyHealthyDish (TikTok)
- Image: /images/korean-coleslaw.jpg
- Serves: 4

## Description

Crunchy cabbage and carrot slaw dressed with soy, rice vinegar, gochugaru and sesame.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Chill: 1 h
Total: 1 h 15 min

## Ingredients

- 1/2 head green cabbage, thinly sliced
- 1 large carrot, thinly julienned
- 3-4 green onions, sliced
- 1-2 garlic cloves, minced
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) soy sauce
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) rice vinegar
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) gochugaru
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) brown sugar
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) sesame seeds

## Method

1. Mix everything in a large bowl
2. Refrigerate for at least 1 hour to let flavors mix
