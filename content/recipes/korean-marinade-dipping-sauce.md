# Korean Marinade or Dipping Sauce

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Quick, Spicy
- Source: DJU
- Serves: 26

## Description

A spicy-sweet soy, gochugaru and maple sauce for marinating meat or dipping.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Total: 10 min

## Ingredients

- 240 ml (1 cup) soy sauce
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) gochugaru flakes
- 3-4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 scallions, chopped
- 60 ml (1/4 cup) red onion, minced
- 1-2 jalapenos, minced
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) maple syrup
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) rice vinegar
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) mirin
- 5 ml (1 tsp) black pepper
- 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) turmeric

## Method

1. Whisk all ingredients in bowl
2. Use as meat marinade or a dipping sauce
