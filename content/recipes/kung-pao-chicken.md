# Kung Pao Chicken

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Spicy
- Image: /images/kung-pao-chicken.jpg
- Serves: 6

## Description

Chicken thighs stir-fried with peppers, celery and peanuts in a glossy, tangy kung pao sauce.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Cook: 15 min
Total: 35 min

## Ingredients

- 905 g (2 lb) boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into small chunks
- 5 ml (1 tsp) kosher salt
- 5 ml (1 tsp) + 15 ml (1 tbsp) soy sauce
- 5 ml (1 tsp) + 15 ml (1 tbsp) rice vinegar
- 5 ml (1 tsp) + 15 ml (1 tbsp) sugar
- 5 ml (1 tsp) + 15 ml (1 tbsp) sesame oil
- 5 ml (1 tsp) + 15 ml (1 tbsp) cornstarch
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) chicken stock
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) + 30 ml (2 tbsp) vegetable oil
- 3 bell peppers, diced
- 3 stalks celery, diced
- 120 ml (½ cup) roasted peanuts or sunflower seeds
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) fresh garlic, minced
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) fresh ginger, minced
- 1 scallion, minced
- 8-10 dried red chilis OR 5 ml (1 tsp) red pepper flakes

## Method

### Prep

1. Combine chicken, salt, 5 ml (1 tsp) soy/vinegar/sugar/sesame oil/cornstarch
2. Marinate for 20+ minutes
3. Combine 15 ml (1 tbsp) soy/vinegar/sugar/sesame oil/cornstarch, chicken stock for sauce

### Directions

1. Heat 15 ml (1 tbsp) oil over high heat in wok until smoking
2. Add chicken and cook until lightly browned
3. Transfer to a clean bowl and set aside
4. Heat 30 ml (2 tbsp) oil in wok until smoking
5. Add bell peppers and celery and cook until lightly browned
6. Add peanuts/sunflower seeds and toss to combine
7. Clear center of wok and add remaining ingredients
8. Cook for 30 seconds and then toss to combine
9. Add chicken and sauce and cook for another minute to thicken
10. Serve immediately with rice or noodles
