# Lasagna

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Pasta, Favorite, Main
- Image: /images/lasagna.jpg
- Emoji: 🍝
- Serves: 12

## Description

Classic beef and tomato lasagna layered with Swiss, Parmesan and mozzarella.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Cook: 1 h 10 min
Rest: 20 min
Total: 1 h 50 min

## Ingredients

- 455 g (1 lb) ground beef
- 1 onion, chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 can tomato paste
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) Worcestershire
- 5 ml (1 tsp) oregano
- 5 ml (1 tsp) parsley
- 2 cans tomato sauce
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) salt & pepper
- 225 g (8 oz) uncooked lasagna
- 455 g (16 oz) shredded Swiss
- 455 g (16 oz) shredded Parmesan
- 455 g (16 oz) shredded Mozzarella
- Parsley for garnish

## Method

### Prep

1. Lasagna should be assembled and left covered in fridge overnight
2. If not, use cooked lasagna noodles
3. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F) before cooking
4. Place large pan over med-high heat

### Directions

1. Brown beef, onion, & garlic in pan, drain off fat
2. Stir in tomato sauce, paste, and seasonings
3. Bring to boil then cover and simmer for 30 minutes, stirring occasionally
4. In 9x13 dish, layer noodles, sauce, & cheese
5. Top with a sprinkle of parsley
6. Cover with foil and place in fridge overnight
7. When ready, bake for 40 minutes
8. Remove foil and bake 20 more minutes
9. Remove from oven and let rest for 20 minutes before serving
