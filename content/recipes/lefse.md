# Lefse

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Bread, Holiday, Minnesota
- Image: /images/lefse.jpg
- Emoji: 🥔
- Serves: 40

## Description

Soft Norwegian potato flatbread, rolled thin and cooked on a griddle. A holiday tradition.

## Cook Times

Prep: 1 h
Chill: several hours
Griddle: 1 h
Total: 2 h

## Ingredients

- 3.8 L (16 cups) potatoes
- 120 ml (½ cup) shortening
- 60 ml (¼ cup) butter
- 180 ml (¾ cup) evaporated milk
- 60 ml (¼ cup) sugar
- Salt to taste
- 720 ml (3 cups) flour

## Method

### Prep

1. Boil and rice the potatoes
2. Mix potatoes, shortening, butter, milk, sugar, salt in large bowl
3. Beat well
4. Set aside for several hours in the fridge

### Directions

1. Mix in flour thoroughly
2. Roll out in small portions, like a tortilla
3. Cook on flat griddle or pan
4. Wrap in cloth towel and keep warm until serving
