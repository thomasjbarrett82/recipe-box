# Lentil Stew

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Stew, Vegetarian, Vegan, High fiber
- Source: Derek Sarno
- Serves: 6

## Description

A loose, adaptable lentil stew with onion, garlic and whatever spices and quick vegetables you have, finished with lime.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Cook: 30 min
Total: 40 min

## Ingredients

- 480 ml (2 cups) lentils, washed and drained
- 1.4 L (6 cups) water
- 45–60 ml (3–4 tbsp) cooking oil
- 1 onion, peeled and diced
- 5-6 cloves garlic, peeled and minced
- Spices and seasoning, ~60 ml (4 tbsp) total (Use whatever sounds good: cumin, curry, Italian mix, smoked paprika, etc.)
- OPTIONAL: quick cooking veggies, ~480 ml (2 cups) total
- 1 lime, zested and juiced
- Salt & pepper

## Method

1. Combine lentils and water in large bowl, set aside
2. Heat stew pot and cooking oil over medium
3. Add onion and cook, stirring, for 5 minutes
4. Add garlic and cook for another 2-3 minutes
5. Add spices and bloom for ~1 minute
6. Add lentils & water to the pot, deglazing if needed
7. Add any veggies, if using them
8. Bring to a boil and then cover
9. Reduce heat to low and simmer for ~15 minutes until lentils are tender
10. Add the lime juice and stir
11. Let sit for 10 minutes before serving

## Notes

This recipe is pretty loose, adapt with whatever you want or have on hand.
