# Lime Cracker Pie

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert
- Serves: 12

## Description

No-bake icebox pie of Ritz crackers layered with a tangy lime, condensed milk and cream filling.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Chill: 2–12 h
Total: 2 h 15 min

## Ingredients

- 2 (395 g / 14 oz) cans sweetened, condensed milk
- 470 ml (1 pint) heavy cream
- 6 limes
- 2 sleeves Ritz crackers

## Method

### Prep

1. Halve limes and squeeze juice into bowl
2. Zest 15 ml (1 tbsp) from the limes

### Directions

1. Combine milk & cream in large bowl
2. Add lime juice and whisk until thickened
3. Spread 240 ml (1 cup) filling in deep-dish pie plate or casserole dish
4. Top with single layer of crackers
5. Repeat layering until full, finishing with filling
6. Sprinkle with zest
7. Refrigerate 2-12 hours before serving
