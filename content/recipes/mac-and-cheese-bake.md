# Mac & Cheese Bake

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Pasta, Holiday
- Source: Tom
- Image: /images/mac-and-cheese-bake.jpg
- Serves: 12

## Description

Extra-cheesy baked mac and cheese with five cheeses and a custardy egg binder.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Bake: 30–35 min
Cool: 10 min
Total: 1 h 5 min

## Ingredients

- 455 g (1 lb) elbow macaroni, uncooked
- 1.4 L (6 cups) chicken broth
- 240 ml (1 cup) half and half
- 240 ml (1 cup) heavy cream
- 225 g (8 oz) sharp cheddar, shredded
- 225 g (8 oz) colby jack, shredded
- 225 g (8 oz) mozzarella, shredded
- 225 g (8 oz) American, cubed
- 225 g (8 oz) cream cheese, softened and cubed
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) smoked paprika
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) garlic powder
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) onion powder
- 5 ml (1 tsp) mustard powder (or 5 ml (1 tsp) mustard)
- 2 large eggs
- Optional: breadcrumbs or crushed cornflakes
- Optional: parsley

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)
2. Grease casserole dish
3. Heat stock pot over medium
4. In large bowl mix creams, half of cheddar and colby, and all of other cheeses
5. In small bowl whisk spices and eggs together

### Directions

1. Fill stock pot with broth and bring to a boil
2. Add pasta and cook until under al dente
3. Remove from heat and drain water
4. Stir chese mixture and egg misture into the pasta
5. Pour entire mixture into casserole dish
6. Sprinkle remaining cheddar and colby over the top
7. Sprinkle crumbs over the top, if using
8. Sprinkle the top with a little smoked paprika and parsley, if desired
9. Bake for 30-35 minutes until bubbly and browned on top
10. Remove and cool for 10 minutes before serving
