# Mac & Cheese (Dairy Free)

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Pasta, Quick, Vegetarian, Vegan, Dairy free
- Serves: 6

## Description

Creamy stovetop mac and cheese made dairy-free with rice or soy milk and Daiya cheddar.

## Cook Times

Prep: 5 min
Cook: 20 min
Total: 25 min

## Ingredients

- 455 g (16 oz) shells or elbow macaroni
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) dairy-free margarine
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) flour
- Rice milk or soy milk
- 1.25 ml (¼ tsp) nutmeg
- 1 package Cheddar Daiya

## Method

### Prep

1. Cook pasta in boiling water, drain & set aside
2. Heat medium pot over medium-low

### Directions

1. Melt margarine in pot
2. Whisk in flour until smooth
3. Slowly add rice/soy milk, whisking each addition until smooth
4. Stop adding milk when it’s a little more liquid texture than desired
5. Stir in the nutmeg
6. Add the Daiya by small handfuls, stirring constantly
7. Make sure each addition is melted in before adding more
8. Once everything is smooth & creamy, stir in the pasta
