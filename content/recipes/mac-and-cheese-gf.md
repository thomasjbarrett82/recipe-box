# Mac & Cheese (Gluten Free)

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Pasta, Quick, Vegetarian, Gluten free
- Image: /images/mac-and-cheese-gf.jpg
- Emoji: 🧀
- Serves: 6

## Description

Creamy stovetop mac and cheese made with gluten-free pasta and flour.

## Cook Times

Prep: 5 min
Cook: 20 min
Total: 25 min

## Ingredients

- 455 g (16 oz) gluten-free shells or elbow macaroni
- 60 ml (4 tbsp) butter
- 45 ml (3 tbsp) gluten-free flour
- Milk (about 960 ml (4 cups))
- 5 ml (1 tsp) salt
- 1.25 ml (¼ tsp) nutmeg
- 225 g (8 oz) shredded Cheddar cheese

## Method

### Prep

1. Cook pasta in boiling water, drain & set aside
2. Heat medium pot over medium-low

### Directions

1. Melt butter in pot
2. Whisk in flour until smooth
3. Slowly add milk, whisking each addition until smooth
4. Stop adding milk when the texture is a little more watery than desired
5. Stir in the nutmeg and salt
6. Add the Cheddar by small handfuls, stirring constantly
7. Make sure each addition is melted in before adding more
8. Once everything is smooth & creamy, stir in the pasta
9. For extra-cheesy sauce, add another 225 g (8 oz) of Cheddar
10. For a dairy-free option, substitute dairy-free margarine, plain almond milk, and Daiya Cheddar in the same amounts
