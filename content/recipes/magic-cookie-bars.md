# Magic Cookie Bars

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert, Minnesota
- Source: @bdylanhollis
- Serves: 24

## Description

Seven-layer bars: graham crust, sweetened condensed milk, chocolate chips, coconut and nuts.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Bake: 30 min
Total: 40 min

## Ingredients

- 120 ml (1/2 cup) butter, melted
- 360 ml (1 1/2 cups) graham cracker crumbs
- 395 g (14 oz) can sweetened condensed milk
- 480 ml (2 cups) chocolate chips
- 240 ml (1 cup) coconut flakes
- 240 ml (1 cup) chopped nuts

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)

### Directions

1. Pour butter in 13x9 baking dish
2. Layer remaining ingredients
3. Bake for 30 mins
4. Let cool completely before cutting and serving
