# Mashed Potatoes

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Side, Vegetarian, Holiday
- Image: /images/mashed-potatoes.jpg
- Emoji: 🥔
- Serves: 8

## Description

Creamy mashed potatoes with butter and milk or cream, with optional Cheddar, sour cream and chives.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Cook: 25 min
Total: 40 min

## Ingredients

- 1.4 kg (3 lb) russet potatoes, peeled & chunked
- 720+ ml (3+ cups) water
- 60 ml (4 tbsp) butter
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) milk or cream
- Optional: 240 ml (1 cup) Cheddar
- Optional: 120 ml (1/2 cup) sour cream
- Optional: 15 ml (1 tbsp) chives, chopped
- Salt & pepper

## Method

1. Place potatoes in pot
2. Add water to cover by 1”
3. Cover with lid and set on high
4. Boil until potatoes are soft
5. Drain and return to pot
6. Mash with remaining ingredients and serve
