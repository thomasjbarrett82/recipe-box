# Mashed Pro-tatoes

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Side
- Image: /images/mashed-pro-tatoes.jpg
- Serves: 12

## Description

Higher-protein mashed potatoes whipped with cottage cheese and an egg.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Cook: 25 min
Total: 40 min

## Ingredients

- 2.3 kg (5 lb) russet potatoes, peeled & chunked
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) bouillon
- water
- 360 ml (1.5 cups) cottage cheese
- 5 ml (1 tsp) garlic salt
- 5 ml (1 tsp) kosher salt
- 60 ml (4 tbsp) butter
- 1 egg

## Method

1. Place potatoes in pot
2. Add water to cover by 1"
3. Stir in the bouillon
4. Cover with lid and set on high
5. Add cottage cheese and salts to blender
6. Boil until potatoes are fork tender
7. Before potatoes are finished, add 240 ml (1 cup) of water to blender
8. Blend cheese, water, salts
9. Drain potatoes and return to pot
10. Add cheese mix, butter, and egg to pot
11. Mash or blend everything together before serving
