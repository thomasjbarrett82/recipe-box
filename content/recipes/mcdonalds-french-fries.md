# McDonalds French Fries

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Side
- Image: /images/mcdonalds-french-fries.jpg
- Serves: 4

## Description

A copycat of McDonald's fries: thin-cut, blanched, frozen, then fried twice in beef tallow.

## Cook Times

Prep: 30 min
Soak: 2 h
Fry: 15 min
Freeze: 2 h
Total: 4 h 45 min

## Ingredients

- 4-5 russet potatoes, peeled & fry cut

### Brine

- 1.9 L (8 cups) cold water
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) salt
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) sugar
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) white vinegar

### For frying

- Beef tallow or lard
- Table salt

## Method

### Prep

1. Mix the brine in 2.8 L (3 quarts) pot with lid
2. Place the fries into the pot and refrigerate for at least 2 hours

### Directions

1. Bring pot to a full boil for 2 minutes
2. Drain & rinse potatoes under cold water
3. Blot dry
4. Place flat into freezer bags without crowding
5. Freeze flat for at least 2 hours
6. Heat tallow/lard in cast iron pan or fryer to 190 °C (375 °F)
7. Fry until light golden brown, approximately 2-3 minutes
8. Remove and let drain, season lightly with salt
