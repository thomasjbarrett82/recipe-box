# Meatloaf

## Description

Simple, classic meatloaf with ketchup, bread crumbs and Italian seasoning.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Bake: 1 h
Rest: 5 min
Total: 1 h 20 min

## Ingredients

- 905 g (2 lb) ground beef
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) ketchup
- 2 eggs
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) bread crumbs
- 5 ml (1 tsp) Italian seasoning
- 5 ml (1 tsp) garlic salt
- 5 ml (1 tsp) onion powder
- Black pepper to taste

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)

### Directions

1. Mix everything in a large bowl
2. Form into a loaf in a small baking dish
3. Bake for 60 minutes until internal temp is 66 °C (150 °F)
4. Remove and let stand for 5 minutes
5. Spread more ketchup over the top for decoration, if desired
