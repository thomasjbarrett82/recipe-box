# Minestrone

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Soup, Slow cooker, High fiber
- Image: /images/minestrone.jpg
- Emoji: 🥣
- Serves: 5

## Description

Italian sausage minestrone with brown rice, spinach and white beans, made in the slow cooker.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Slow cook: 5–6 h
Total: 5 h 20 min

## Ingredients

- 455 g (1 lb) ground Italian sausage
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 carrots, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 3 stalks celery, chopped
- 1 can diced Italian-style tomatoes
- 1 can chicken broth (480 ml / 2 cups)
- 240 ml (1 cup) water
- 60 ml (¼ cup) brown rice, uncooked
- 5 ml (1 tsp) Italian seasoning
- 1 package chopped spinach
- 1 can northern beans, drained and rinsed
- 1.25 ml (¼ tsp) black pepper
- 1.25 ml (¼ tsp) salt

## Method

### Prep

1. Place pan over med-high heat

### Directions

1. Cook sausage and crumble
2. Drain and move to crockpot
3. Stir in remaining ingredients
4. Cook on low for 5-6 hours
