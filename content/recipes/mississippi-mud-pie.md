# Mississippi Mud Pie

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert, Favorite
- Source: Tom
- Image: /images/mississippi-mud-pie.jpg
- Serves: 12

## Description

An Oreo crust filled with baked chocolate-pecan custard, topped with chocolate pudding.

## Cook Times

Prep: 40 min
Bake: 25 min
Chill: 4–12 h
Total: 5 h 5 min

## Ingredients

### Crust

- 425 g (15 oz) package of Oreo cookies
- 90 ml (6 tbsp) butter, melted

### Brownie Layer

- 120 ml (1/2 cup) butter
- 100 g (3.5 oz) dark chocolate, chopped
- 3 large eggs
- 160 ml (2/3 cup) sugar
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) pecans, chopped
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) espresso/coffee grounds
- 5 ml (1 tsp) vanilla extract
- 60 ml (1/4 cup) flour
- 60 ml (1/4 cup) cocoa powder

### Pudding Layer

- 180 ml (3/4 cup) sugar
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) cornstarch
- 1.25 ml (1/4 tsp) salt
- 360 ml (1.5 cups) whole milk
- 2 egg yolks
- 100 g (3.5 oz) dark chocolate, chopped
- 5 ml (1 tsp) vanilla extract
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) butter

### Topping

- 480 ml (2 cups) heavy cream
- 10 ml (2 tsp) vanilla extract
- 90 ml (6 tbsp) powdered sugar
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) pecans, chopped
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) chocolate shavings

## Method

### Crust

1. Grind up cookies in food processor
2. Add butter and pulse to combine
3. Press in bottom and sides of deep dish 9 inch pie plate
4. Place in freezer

### Brownie Layer

1. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)
2. Melt butter and chocolate in bowl
3. Whisk eggs and sugar until thickened
4. Whisk in remaining brownie ingredients
5. Whisk in chocolate mixture until combined
6. Pour filling into cold crust
7. Bake for 25 minutes until set (barely jiggling)
8. Cool completely

### Pudding Layer

1. Combine sugar, cornstarch, salt, milk, yolks in saucepan
2. Cook over medium heat, whisking, until it starts to bubble
3. Remove from heat and whisk in remaining ingredients
4. Pour pudding on top of brownie layer
5. Place in fridge for 4-12 hours

### Topping

1. Whisk cream, vanilla, sugar until soft peaks form
2. Spoon on top of the pie
3. Garnish with pecans and chocolate
