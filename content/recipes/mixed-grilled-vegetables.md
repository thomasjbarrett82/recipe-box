# Mixed Grilled Vegetables

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Side, Quick, Grill, Vegetarian, Vegan
- Image: /images/mixed-grilled-vegetables.jpg
- Emoji: 🍆
- Serves: 4

## Description

Peppers, red onion, zucchini and carrot tossed in lemon and olive oil, then grilled.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Grill: 6–8 min
Total: 25 min

## Ingredients

- 2 bell peppers, quartered
- 1 red onion, quartered
- 1 zucchini squash, roughly chopped
- 1 large carrot, cut into ½” pieces
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) EVOO
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) lemon juice
- 5 ml (1 tsp) black pepper
- 5 ml (1 tsp) kosher salt

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat an outdoor or indoor grill

### Directions

1. Combine all ingredients in a bowl
2. Toss to combine
3. Grill for 6-8 minutes until tender-crisp
