# Monkey Bread

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Breakfast, Vegetarian, Holiday
- Serves: 12

## Description

Pull-apart breakfast bread of biscuit pieces baked in cinnamon brown-sugar butter.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Bake: 30–40 min
Total: 55 min

## Ingredients

- 4 cans refrigerated biscuits
- (optional) 240 ml (1 cup) chopped pecans
- 360 ml (1 ½ cups) brown sugar
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) cinnamon
- 180 ml (¾ cup) butter

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)
2. Grease a Bundt pan

### Directions

1. Cut biscuits into quarters
   - (optional: mix in chopped pecans)
2. Melt remaining ingredients in saucepan
3. Place half of biscuits in pan
4. Pour half of sauce over biscuits
5. Repeat with remaining biscuits and sauce
6. Bake for 30-40 minutes
