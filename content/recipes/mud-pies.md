# Mud Pies

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert
- Source: @bdylanhollis
- Image: /images/mud-pies.jpg
- Serves: 18

## Description

No-bake chocolate oatmeal cookies made with nut butter.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Chill: 1 h
Total: 1 h 15 min

## Ingredients

- 120 ml (1/2 cup) butter
- 480 ml (2 cups) sugar
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) milk
- 45 ml (3 tbsp) cocoa
- 240 ml (1 cup) nut butter
- 5 ml (1 tsp) vanilla extract
- 720 ml (3 cups) quick oats

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat large skillet on med-low heat

### Directions

1. Mix butter, sugar, milk, cocoa in skillet
2. Heat until boiling for 1 minute
3. Remove from heat
4. Add remaining ingredients
5. Roll/scoop into small balls and transfer to tray with wax paper
6. Cool in fridge for at least 1 hour
