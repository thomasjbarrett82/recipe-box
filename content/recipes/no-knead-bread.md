# No Knead Bread

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Bread, Dutch oven, Vegetarian, Vegan
- Serves: 16

## Description

Crusty artisan bread with almost no work: an overnight rise, then baked in a Dutch oven.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Rise: 18 h
Bake: 1 h
Total: 19 h 30 min

## Ingredients

- 650 g flour (a/p or bread)
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) instant yeast
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) kosher salt
- 480 ml (2 cups) water, room temperature
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) EVOO
- Cornmeal for dusting

## Method

### Prep 1

1. Combine flour, yeast, salt in a large bowl
2. Add water, EVOO and mix until sticky and shaggy
3. Cover with plastic wrap and rest for 18 hours at room temperature

### Prep 2

1. Lightly flour a work surface and transfer dough to it
2. Fold it once or twice to coat with flour
3. Cover and rest for 15 minutes
4. Shape into a ball, using enough flour to keep from sticking
5. Coat a cotton towel with cornmeal and place ball on it
6. Let rise for 2 more hours

### Directions

1. Heat oven to 230 °C (450 °F) with Dutch oven inside
2. Place dough inside pot and cover
3. Cover and bake for 30 minutes
4. Remove lid and bake for another 30 minutes
5. Remove and let cool for at least 20 minutes before slicing

Faster bread: Shorten the 18 hour rise to 8 hours in Prep 1, skip the 15 minute rest in Prep 2, and skip the 2 hour rise
