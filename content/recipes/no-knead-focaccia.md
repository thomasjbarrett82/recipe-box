# No Knead Focaccia

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Bread, Vegetarian, Vegan
- Serves: 12

## Description

Airy, olive-oil-rich focaccia with olives and rosemary from a slow overnight rise.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Rise: 12–24 h
Final rise: 2 h
Bake: 16–24 min
Total: 14 h 35 min

## Ingredients

- 500 g bread flour
- 15 g kosher salt
- 4 g instant yeast
- 325 g water
- EVOO
- 115 g (4 oz) sliced olives
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) fresh rosemary, chopped

## Method

### Prep

1. Prepare dough 12-24 hours ahead of cook time
2. Prepare dough for final rise 2 hours ahead of cook time
3. Preheat oven to 230 °C (450 °F)

### Dough Prep

1. Combine flour, salt, yeast, and water in large bowl
2. Mix until no dry flour remains
3. Cover and let sit for the dough to rise 12-24 hours

### Final Rise

1. Transfer to floured work surface and form into a tight ball
2. Pour 15 ml (1 tbsp) EVOO in large cast iron skillet or cake pan
3. Transfer dough to pan and rotate to cover with oil
4. Press down flatly to spread around the pan
5. Cover and let sit for 1-2 hours

### Cooking

1. Use fingertips to press dough down again, popping any bubbles
2. Spread olives & rosemary over surface and drizzle 15 ml (1 tbsp) EVOO
3. Sprinkle with a little kosher or coarse sea salt
4. Put the skillet in oven and bake until golden brown, 16-24 minutes
5. Skillet can be placed over burner on medium heat if the bottom is not crispy enough
6. Allow to cool slightly before transferring to a cutting board and serving
