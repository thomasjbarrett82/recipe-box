# Oatmeal Raisin Cookies

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert, Quick
- Serves: 18

## Description

Chewy oatmeal raisin cookies or bars, with a gluten-free option.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Bake: 8–10 min
Total: 30 min

## Ingredients

- 120 ml (1/2 cup) + 90 ml (6 tbsp) butter, softened (1 1/2 sticks)
- 180 ml (3/4 cup) brown sugar
- 180 ml (3/4 cup) white sugar
- 2 eggs
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) vanilla
- 300 ml (1 1/4 cups) a/p flour (GF: 360 ml (1 1/2 cups) GF flour + 5 ml (1 tsp) xanthan gum)
- 5 ml (1 tsp) baking soda
- 5 ml (1 tsp) cinnamon
- 5 ml (1 tsp) salt
- 480 ml (2 cups) uncooked rolled oats
- 240 ml (1 cup) raisins OR dried cranberries or cherries

## Method

### Prep

1. Preheat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)
2. Cover 2 baking sheets with parchment paper

### Directions

1. Beat butter and sugars in large bowl with a mixer until creamy
2. Add eggs and vanilla
3. Add flour, baking soda, cinnamon, and salt
4. Stir in the oats and raisins, and mix well
5. Drop the dough by rounded spoonful onto an ungreased cookie sheet
6. Bake 8-10 minutes until light golden brown

Bar cookies: bake 30-35 minutes in an ungreased 9x13 baking pan
