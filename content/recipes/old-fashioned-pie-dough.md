# Old Fashioned Pie Dough

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert
- Image: /images/old-fashioned-pie-dough.jpg
- Emoji: 🥧
- Serves: 8

## Description

An all-butter pie dough measured by weight, with instructions for blind baking.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Chill: 2 h
Total: 2 h 20 min

## Ingredients

- 225 g a/p flour, plus more for dusting
- 15 g sugar
- 5 g kosher salt
- 225 g (2 sticks) cold butter, cut into ½ inch cubes
- 115 g cold water

## Method

### Directions

1. Whisk the flour, sugar, and salt in a medium bowl
2. Toss the butter into the mixture and mash into the flour
3. Stir in water and knead with fingers until barely into a ball
4. Transfer to the fridge for 10 minutes to chill
5. On a floured work surface, roll into a 10x15 rectangle
6. Fold in half twice, and then divide into half
7. Refrigerate or freeze until ready to use

### For a blind-baked pie

1. Preheat oven to 175 °C (350 °F) and move rack to low-middle
2. Fill with sugar
3. Cook with sugar inside to weight the crust for 1 hour, until golden brown
