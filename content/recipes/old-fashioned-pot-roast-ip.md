# Old Fashioned Pot Roast (IP)

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Instant Pot
- Serves: 7

## Description

Chuck roast pressure-cooked with onion, tomato paste and broth, then potatoes and carrots, with a quick gravy.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Cook: 1 h 50 min
Total: 2 h 10 min

## Ingredients

- 1.4–1.8 kg (3–4 lb) chuck roast
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) oil
- 1 small onion, diced
- 480 ml (2 cups) beef or chicken broth
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) tomato paste
- 2 bay leaves
- 3 medium carrots, cut into 2 inch pieces
- 6 red potatoes, quartered
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) fresh parsley, chopped
- 60 ml (¼ cup) flour
- 60 ml (¼ cup) water
- Salt and pepper

## Method

1. Heat IP to Sauté and add oil
2. Sear roast on each side for 5 minutes, remove and set aside
3. Add onions and cook until soft
4. Deglaze with broth
5. Add tomato paste and bay leaves to the pot
6. Add the roast and cook on High for 75 minutes
7. Let pressure release naturally for 15 minutes
8. Remove the roast and set aside to rest
9. Remove bay leaves and add carrots & potatoes
10. Cook on High for 3 minutes
11. Quick release pressure and transfer vegetables to a bowl
12. Sprinkle with parsley
13. In a small bowl, whisk the flour & water
14. Pour 240 ml (1 cup) of cooking liquid into slurry and whisk together
15. Discard remaining cooking liquid
16. Add the slurry back to the pot and sauté until thickened
17. Transfer gravy to a serving vessel
18. Season everything with salt & pepper to taste
