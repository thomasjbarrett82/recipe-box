# One Pot Chicken and Rice

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main
- Serves: 5

## Description

Chicken thighs, carrots and rice baked together in one pan with cumin and smoked paprika.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Cook: 50 min
Total: 1 h 5 min

## Ingredients

- 15 ml (1 tbsp) EVOO
- 905 g (2 lb) chicken thighs
- 1 large onion, diced
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 large carrots, chopped
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) cumin
- 5 ml (1 tsp) smoked paprika
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) kosher salt
- 5 ml (1 tsp) black pepper
- 240 ml (1 cup) rice, uncooked
- 480 ml (2 cups) chicken stock

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)
2. Heat EVOO in large heavy pan over med-high heat

### Directions

1. Add chicken to pan
2. Cook 10-12 minutes until browned
3. Remove and set aside
4. Add onion, garlic, and carrots
5. Cook for 5 minutes
6. Add remaining ingredients
7. Stir and bring to a boil
8. Set chicken on top of rice
9. Cover and move to the oven
10. Bake for 25-30 minutes
11. Remove and rest covered for 5-10 minutes
