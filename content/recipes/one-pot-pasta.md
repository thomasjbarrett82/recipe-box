# One Pot Pasta

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Pasta, Spicy, Dutch oven
- Author: Tom Barrett
- Source: [Brian Lagerstrom](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yKWWRlh8qeE)
- Image: /images/one-pot-pasta.jpg
- Emoji: 🍝
- Serves: 8

## Description

This one pot pasta is rich, creamy, a little spicy, and tastes like it took way more work than it did.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Cook: 40 min
Rest: 10 min
Total: 1 h

## Ingredients

- Olive oil, for searing
- 680g (1½ lbs) hot Italian sausage
- 250g (1 large) onion, grated or minced
- 20g (5–6 cloves) garlic, minced
- 1 jar spaghetti sauce (or homemade)
- 1000g (4 cups) water
- 454g (1 lb) dry rigatoni or penne pasta
- 100g (7 tbsp) heavy cream
- 50g (1 cup) aged Parmesan, finely grated, plus more for serving
- 10g (¼ cup) fresh basil, minced

### For Serving

- Red pepper flakes
- Cracked black pepper
- Extra Parm
- Olive oil

## Method

### Cook

1. Preheat oven to 425°F/220°C
2. Heat long drizzle of olive oil in dutch oven over medium-high
3. Add the sausage and brown it, crumbling as fine as possible until nearly cooked
4. Add grated onion, minced garlic and cook for 2-3 mins until softened
5. Add sauce & water and bring to a simmer
6. Stir in the pasta until fully submerged
7. Bake uncovered for 20 minutes, stirring every 5 minutes
8. CAREFULLY pull from the oven
9. Stir in basil, cream, and parm. Adjust seasoning with salt
10. Let rest at least 10 mins before serving
