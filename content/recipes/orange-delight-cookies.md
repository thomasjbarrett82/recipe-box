# Orange Delight Cookies

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert
- Serves: 24

## Description

Soft buttermilk cookies with orange zest and walnuts, topped with an orange glaze.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Bake: 10–11 min
Total: 45 min

## Ingredients

- 180 ml (¾ cup) shortening
- 360 ml (1 ½ cups) brown sugar
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 7.5 ml (1 ½ tsp) grated orange rind
- 120 ml (½ cup) buttermilk
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) baking soda
- 720 ml (3 cups) flour
- 1.25 ml (¼ tsp) salt
- 10 ml (2 tsp) baking powder
- 180 ml (¾ cup) chopped walnuts

### Frosting

- 480 ml (2 cups) powdered sugar
- 7.5 ml (1 ½ tsp) grated orange rind
- 80 ml (1/3 cup) orange juice

## Method

### Prep

1. Dissolve baking soda in buttermilk
2. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)

### Directions

1. Cream shortening and sugar in medium bowl
2. Mix in eggs, vanilla, orange rind until smooth
3. Stir in buttermilk mixture
4. In large bowl, stir together flour, salt, baking powder, and walnuts
5. Mix the wet into the dry
6. Drop onto cookie sheet by spoonful’s
7. Bake for 10-11 minutes until done
8. Remove and cool
9. Mix the frosting ingredients in a separate bowl
10. Frost the cookies once they’ve finished cooling
