# Oreo Bombs

- Serves: 30

## Description

Oreo truffles: crushed Oreos and cream cheese rolled into balls and dipped in chocolate.

## Cook Times

Prep: 40 min
Freeze: 15 min
Total: 1 h

## Ingredients

- 2 packages Oreo cookies (gluten free)
- 225 g (8 oz) cream cheese, room temp
- 240 ml (1 cup) chocolate melting wafers
- 240 ml (1 cup) white chocolate melting wafers
- Decoration ideas: chocolate drizzle, Oreo crumbs, sprinkles, crushed peppermint, crushed nuts

## Method

### Prep

1. Line baking tray with parchment or wax paper

### Directions

1. Pulse cookies in food processor until fine crumbs
2. Use stand mixer to whip cream cheese until smooth
3. Add cookies and mix until well combined
4. Roll into small balls and place on tray
5. Freeze for at least 15 minutes
6. Place the melting wafers in 2 separate bowls
7. Microwave 30 seconds at a time, stirring, until melted
8. Remove balls from freezer and dip into chocolate with a fork
9. Decorate, if desired
10. Place back on tray and refrigerate for 1+ hour
11. Store in airtight container for up to 3 weeks
