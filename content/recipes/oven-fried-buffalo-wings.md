# Oven Fried Buffalo Wings

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Appetizer, Spicy
- Image: /images/oven-fried-buffalo-wings.jpg
- Serves: About 1½ per pound of wings

## Description

Crispy oven-baked wings, dry-brined with baking powder and tossed in Frank's RedHot butter.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Dry brine: 8–24 h
Bake: 35–50 min
Total: 8 h 50 min

## Ingredients

- 455 g–1.8 kg (1–4 lb) chicken wings
- 5 ml (1 tsp) baking powder (per pound)
- 5 ml (1 tsp) kosher salt (per pound)
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) butter (per pound)
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) Frank’s Red Hot (per pound)
- Blue cheese and ranch dressing
- Carrots and celery

## Method

### Prep

1. Line a baking sheet with foil and wire rack
2. Dry wings with paper towels
3. In a large bowl, toss wings with baking powder and salt
4. Spread on rack and place in fridge
5. Rest, uncovered, for 8 – 24 hours

### Directions

1. Adjust oven rack to upper-middle position
2. Heat oven to 230 °C (450 °F)
3. Add wings and cook for 20 minutes
4. Flip wings and continue to cook until crisp and golden brown (15 – 30 minutes longer)
5. Combine butter and hot sauce in small saucepan
6. Whisk until combined
7. Transfer wings to large bowl and toss thoroughly to coat
8. Serve immediately with dressing and veggies

## Notes

Works well in air fryer.
