# Pan Fried Beans and Wilted Greens

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Side, Quick, Vegetarian, Vegan, High fiber
- Image: /images/pan-fried-beans-wilted-greens.jpg
- Emoji: 🥬
- Serves: 4

## Description

White beans pan-fried with Swiss chard, garlic and lemon.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Cook: 15 min
Total: 25 min

## Ingredients

- 225 g (8 oz) Swiss chard (1/2 bunch)
- 1 onion, thinly sliced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 395 g (14 oz) cannelloni or northern beans, drained and rinsed
- Lemon zest
- 5 ml (1 tsp) lemon juice
- 5 ml (1 tsp) Italian seasoning
- 10 ml (2 tsp) kosher salt
- EVOO

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat 5 ml (1 tsp) EVOO in large skillet over med-high heat
2. Trim the stems from the chard and slice leaves into ribbons

### Directions

1. Cook onions with 2.5 ml (½ tsp) salt until soft and golden brown
2. Stir in garlic and chard stems for 1 minute
3. Transfer to a bowl
4. Heat another 10 ml (2 tsp) EVOO and add the beans
5. Cook for several minutes until beans are blistered all over without burning
6. Stir in the chard leaves, seasoning, and 2.5 ml (½ tsp) salt
7. Cook until leaves are wilted
8. Return the onion mixture to the pan
9. Add in the lemon zest, lemon juice, and 5 ml (1 tsp) salt
10. Drizzle another 5–10 ml (1–2 tsp) EVOO and serve

Can also serve as a main course with some pasta or poached eggs.
