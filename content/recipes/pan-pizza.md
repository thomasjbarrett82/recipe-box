# Pan Pizza

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Vegetarian, Favorite
- Image: /images/pan-pizza.jpg
- Emoji: 🍕
- Serves: 8

## Description

Thick, crispy-bottomed pan pizza from a 24-hour dough.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Rise: 24 h
Proof in pan: 2 h
Bake: 15 min
Total: 26 h 35 min

## Ingredients

- 400 grams bread flour
- 10 grams kosher salt
- 4 grams instant yeast
- 275 grams water
- 8 grams EVOO
- 360 ml (1.5 cups) pizza sauce
- 455 g (16 oz) full-fat dry shredded mozzarella
- Toppings (as desired)
- Grated Parmesan

## Method

### Prep

1. Prep the dough 24 hours ahead of time

### Directions

1. Combine the flour, salt, yeast, water, and oil in large bowl
2. Mix until no dry flour remains
3. Cover tightly with plastic wrap and let sit
4. 2 hours before cooking: move to a floured work surface and form into a ball
5. Split it into 2 halves if using 10 inch skillets for the pizzas
6. Pour 15–30 ml (1–2 tbsp) of oil into large plates
7. Place dough in plates and turn to coat evenly with the oil
8. Use a flat palm and press the dough around the pan
9. Let sit for 2 hours to finish rising
10. Preheat oven to 260 °C (500 °F) with pans inside
11. Transfer dough to pans, careful not to burn yourself
12. Use fingertips to finish pressing dough, popping any large bubbles
13. Top dough with sauce
14. Spread evenly with mozzarella
15. Add other toppings, if desired
16. Sprinkle with Parmesan
17. Transfer to oven and cook until crispy and golden brown
