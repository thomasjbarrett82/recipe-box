# Pan Seared Pork Chops

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main
- Serves: 2

## Description

Dry-brined bone-in pork chops, cooked gently, then seared and basted with butter, shallot and thyme.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Dry brine: 8–24 h
Cook: 40 min
Total: 8 h 55 min

## Ingredients

- 2 bone-in pork rib chops, 1.5 inches thick
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) kosher salt
- 7.5 ml (1.5 tsp) sugar
- Black pepper
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) oil
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) butter
- 1 medium shallot, thinly sliced
- 8 sprigs fresh thyme

## Method

### Prep

1. Marinate chops 8-24 hours ahead of cooking
2. Preheat oven to 120 °C (250 °F)

### Directions

1. Combine salt and sugar in a small bowl
2. Season chops on all sides with the mixture
3. Transfer to a baking sheet & wire rack in the fridge and let sit for 8-24 hours
4. Move the baking sheet to the oven and cook until chops are 43 °C (110 °F), approximately 30 minutes
5. Meanwhile, heat the oil in a large heavy skillet over high heat
6. Place pork chops in skillet and turn occasionally until just starting to brown
7. Add butter, shallots, and thyme to skillet
8. Continue cooking the chops
9. Spoon the oil mixture over the chops until golden brown and well-crusted
10. Pick up the chops with tongs to sear the fat caps on the sides
11. Transfer the chops to a plate and let rest for 5 minutes before serving
12. Just before serving, reheat the drippings until smoking, then pour over the chops
