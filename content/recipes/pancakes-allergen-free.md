# Pancakes (Allergen-Free)

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Breakfast, Quick, Vegetarian, Favorite
- Image: /images/pancakes-allergen-free.jpg
- Emoji: 🥞
- Serves: 4

## Description

Fluffy pancakes that can be made with egg substitute, rice milk and gluten-free flour.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Cook: 15 min
Total: 25 min

## Ingredients

- 2 eggs OR egg-substitute equivalent
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) sugar
- 240 ml (1 cup) buttermilk OR 240 ml (1 cup) rice milk & 15 ml (1 tbsp) white vinegar
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) oil
- 360 ml (1 ½ cups) flour OR 360 ml (1 ½ cups) gluten-free all-purpose flour
- 5 ml (1 tsp) baking powder
- 5 ml (1 tsp) baking soda
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) salt

## Method

### Prep

1. Mix rice milk & vinegar and let sit for 5 minutes to make "buttermilk"
2. Heat skillet on medium-low

### Directions

1. Beat eggs (or substitute) with sugar until creamy
2. Add buttermilk and oil and mix well
3. Mix in remaining ingredients
4. Let sit for a few minutes to aerate
5. Drop onto hot skillet by ¼ cupful and cook until golden brown on each side
