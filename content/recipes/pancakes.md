# Pancakes

- Serves: 4

## Description

Fluffy buttermilk pancakes with whipped egg whites.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Cook: 15 min
Total: 25 min

## Ingredients

- 2 eggs
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) sugar
- 160 ml (2/3 cup) buttermilk
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) oil
- 360 ml (1 ½ cups) flour
- 5 ml (1 tsp) baking powder
- 5 ml (1 tsp) baking soda
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) salt

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat skillet on medium

### Directions

1. Separate eggs
2. Beat whites into peaks and set aside
3. Beat yolks with sugar until creamy
4. Add buttermilk and oil and mix well
5. Mix in remaining ingredients
6. Fold in the egg whites and let sit for 5 minutes
7. Drop onto hot skillet by ¼ cupful and cook until golden brown on each side
