# Pasta Fagioli (IP)

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Soup, Instant Pot, High fiber
- Image: /images/pasta-fagioli-ip.jpg
- Emoji: 🫘
- Serves: 6

## Description

Bean and sausage soup with tomatoes, vegetables and pasta, starting from dried beans in the Instant Pot.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Soak: 6–8 h
Cook: 1 h
Total: 7 h 15 min

## Ingredients

- 160 ml (2/3 cup) dried northern or navy beans
- 160 ml (2/3 cup) dried kidney beans
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) EVOO
- 455 g (1 lb) ground Italian sausage
- ½ yellow onion, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 960 ml (4 cups) chicken broth
- 1 (425 g / 15 oz) can tomato sauce
- 1 (425 g / 15 oz) can tomatoes, diced
- 2 celery ribs, chopped
- 120 ml (½ cup) carrots, sliced
- 240 ml (1 cup) pasta (optional)
- 5 ml (1 tsp) salt
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) black pepper
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) Italian seasoning
- 1.25 ml (¼ tsp) crushed red pepper flakes

## Method

### Prep

1. Soak beans in cold water for 6-8 hours

### Directions

1. Cook beans in IP for 30 minutes, remove and set aside
2. Sauté sausage & onion in oil until browned
3. Add garlic and cook for 2 minutes
4. Deglaze with broth
5. Add remaining ingredients
6. Lock lid and cook for 10 minutes
7. Allow pressure to naturally release
8. Serve with fresh basil and grated Parmesan cheese
