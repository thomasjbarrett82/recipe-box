# Pasta Fagioli

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Soup, Slow cooker, High fiber
- Serves: 6

## Description

Slow-cooker pasta e fagioli with ground beef, northern beans, tomatoes and a little heat.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Slow cook: 8–10 h
Total: 8 h 15 min

## Ingredients

- 1 can Northern beans
- 455 g (1 lb) ground beef, browned & drained
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 stalks celery, chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 cans beef broth
- 2 cans diced tomato
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) black pepper
- 1.25 ml (¼ tsp) cayenne
- 1.25 ml (¼ tsp) hot sauce
- 5 ml (1 tsp) basil
- 720 ml (3 cups) cooked pasta

## Method

1. Combine all ingredients except pasta in crockpot
2. Cook on LOW for 8-10 hours or HIGH for 4-5 hours
3. Add pasta and serve
