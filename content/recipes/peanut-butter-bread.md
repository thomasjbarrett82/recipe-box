# Peanut Butter Bread

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Bread
- Source: @bdylanhollis
- Serves: 10

## Description

A simple, slightly sweet quick bread made with peanut butter.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Bake: 70 min
Total: 1 h 20 min

## Ingredients

- 480 ml (2 cups) flour
- 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) salt
- 60 ml (1/4 cup) sugar
- 20 ml (4 tsp) baking powder
- 320 ml (1 1/3 cups) milk
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) peanut butter OR other nut butter

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 165 °C (325 °F)
2. Grease bread pan

### Directions

1. Whisk flour, salt, sugar, baking powder in large bowl
2. Stir in milk and nut butter
3. Bake for 70 minutes
4. Let cool before serving
