# Peanut Butter Cookies (Gluten Free)

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert, Quick, Gluten free, Dairy free
- Image: /images/peanut-butter-cookies-gf.jpg
- Emoji: 🥜
- Serves: 12

## Description

Four-ingredient flourless peanut butter cookies.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Bake: 10–12 min
Total: 25 min

## Ingredients

- 240 ml (1 cup) peanut butter
- 180 ml (3/4 cup) sugar
- 5 ml (1 tsp) vanilla extract
- 1 egg

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)

### Directions

1. In large bowl, thoroughly mix ingredients
2. Scoop into small balls and arrange on parchment lined pan
3. Flatten mounds with a fork
4. Bake 10-12 minutes
5. Let cool completely before serving
