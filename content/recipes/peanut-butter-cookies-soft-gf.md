# Peanut Butter Cookies (Soft) (Gluten Free)

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert, Gluten free
- Image: /images/peanut-butter-cookies-soft-gf.jpg
- Serves: 38

## Description

Soft, thick gluten-free peanut butter cookies made with cream cheese.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Chill: 1 h
Bake: 12–13 min
Total: 1 h 30 min

## Ingredients

- 600 ml (2 1/2 cups) GF A/P flour
- 7.5 ml (1 1/2 tsp) baking soda
- 5 ml (1 tsp) baking powder
- 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) salt
- 240 ml (1 cup) unsalted butter, softened
- 480 ml (2 cups) peanut butter (or other nut butter)
- 360 ml (1 1/2 cups) + 60 ml (4 tbsp) sugar
- 240 ml (1 cup) brown sugar
- 2 eggs
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) cream cheese, softened
- 10 ml (2 tsp) vanilla extract
- 480 ml (2 cups) peanut butter baking chips (optional)

## Method

### Prep

1. In large bowl whisk flour, baking soda & powder, salt
2. In mixer bowl beat butter and peanut butter until creamy
3. Beat sugars into mixer bowl
4. Beat in egg, cream cheese, vanilla extract
5. Slowly add flour mixture on low speed until integrated
6. Stir in the baking chips (optional)
7. Place bowl in fridge for at least 1 hour, and up to 3 days

### Directions

1. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)
2. Place 60 ml (4 tbsp) sugar in a bowl
3. Line cookie sheets with parchment or silicone
4. Use small scoop or spoon to make 30 gram dough balls
5. Roll balls in sugar and arrange on cookie sheets (will make 70-80 small cookies)
6. Bake for 12-13 minutes until they no longer look "wet" on outside but still underdone
7. Let cool completely before serving
