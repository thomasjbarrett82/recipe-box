# Peanut Butter Cups

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert, Holiday
- Image: /images/peanut-butter-cups.jpg
- Emoji: 🥜
- Serves: 60

## Description

Homemade peanut butter cups: sweetened peanut butter filling in chocolate almond bark.

## Cook Times

Prep: 45 min
Chill: 30 min
Total: 1 h 15 min

## Ingredients

- 240 ml (1 cup) peanut butter
- 480 ml (2 cups) powdered sugar
- 120 ml (½ cup) butter, melted
- 1.1 kg (2 ½ lb) chocolate almond bark

## Method

### Prep

1. Line cupcake pan or mini candy cups

### Directions

1. Mix peanut butter, sugar, and butter
2. Roll into small balls and slightly flatten on wax paper
3. Place in fridge to cool
4. Melt chocolate in double boiler
5. Place small amount of chocolate in each candy cup
6. Add peanut butter ball
7. Cover with more chocolate
8. Place in fridge to cool before serving
