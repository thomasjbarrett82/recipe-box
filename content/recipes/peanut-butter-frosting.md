# Peanut Butter Frosting

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert, Quick
- Image: /images/peanut-butter-frosting.jpg
- Serves: 24

## Description

Old-fashioned cooked peanut butter frosting.

## Cook Times

Prep: 5 min
Cook: 5 min
Total: 15 min

## Ingredients

- 480 ml (2 cups) sugar
- 120 ml (½ cup) milk
- 120 ml (½ cup) butter
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) peanut butter

## Method

1. Combine sugar, milk, butter in pot over medium heat
2. Bring to a boil for 2 minutes
3. Remove from heat and let cool
4. Add peanut butter and beat thoroughly
