# Peanut Butter Krispie Bars

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert, Minnesota
- Serves: 24

## Description

Rice Krispies bars with peanut butter and brown sugar, topped with chocolate.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Set: 1 h
Total: 1 h 15 min

## Ingredients

- 240 ml (1 cup) smooth peanut butter
- 240 ml (1 cup) brown sugar
- 240 ml (1 cup) butter
- 1.4 L (6 cups) Rice Krispies
- 240 ml (1 cup) chocolate chips

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat saucepan over medium

### Directions

1. Melt together peanut butter, sugar, and butter
2. Stir in Rice Krispies and press into casserole dish
3. Melt chips and pour over as frosting
