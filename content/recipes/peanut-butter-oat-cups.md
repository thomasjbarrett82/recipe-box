# Peanut Butter Oat Cups

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert
- Source: TikTok
- Image: /images/peanut-butter-oat-cups.jpg
- Serves: 8

## Description

No-bake peanut butter and oat cups with chocolate chips, set in the freezer.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Freeze: 2 h
Total: 2 h 15 min

## Ingredients

- 240 ml (1 cup) rolled oats
- 60 ml (1/4 cup) GF flour
- 240 ml (1 cup) + 30 ml (2 tbsp) peanut butter
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) honey
- 5 ml (1 tsp) vanilla extract
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) chocolate chips

## Method

### Prep

1. Mix oats, flour, 480 ml (2 cups) peanut butter, honey, vanilla in bowl
2. Divide into 8 cupcake papers and freeze for 2 hours

### Directions

1. Mix chocolate and 30 ml (2 tbsp) peanut butter in bowl
2. Microwave in 20 second intervals, stirring until melted together
3. Spread over oat mixtures evenly
4. Refrigerate for 10+ minutes before serving
