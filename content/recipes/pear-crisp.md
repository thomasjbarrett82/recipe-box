# Pear Crisp

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert
- Image: /images/pear-crisp.jpg
- Serves: 8

## Description

Spiced pears baked under a buttery oat and pecan topping.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Bake: 45 min
Cool: 5–10 min
Total: 1 h 15 min

## Ingredients

### Filling

- 1.4 L (6 cups) pears, peeled and sliced
- 5 ml (1 tsp) vanilla extract
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) nutmeg
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) salt

### Topping

- 115 g (1 stick) butter, softened
- 120 ml (½ cup) A/P flour
- 120 ml (½ cup) rolled oats
- 120 ml (½ cup) sugar
- 120 ml (½ cup) brown sugar
- 120 ml (½ cup) pecans, finely chopped
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) salt

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)
2. Grease 8-inch square baking dish

### Directions

1. Toss filling ingredients in large bowl and transfer to pan
2. Mix topping ingredients with fingers/fork until crumbly
3. Spread evenly over filling
4. Sprinkle some more cinnamon over top
5. Bake for 45 minutes
6. Let cool 5-10 minutes before serving

## Notes

Serve with vanilla ice cream and caramel sauce, if you like.
