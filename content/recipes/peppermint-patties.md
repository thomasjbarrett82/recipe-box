# Peppermint Patties

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert, Holiday
- Source: @bdylanhollis
- Image: /images/peppermint-patties.jpg
- Serves: 30

## Description

Homemade peppermint patties: a creamy mint center dipped in dark chocolate.

## Cook Times

Prep: 45 min
Freeze: 20 min
Chill: 30 min
Total: 1 h 35 min

## Ingredients

- 240 ml (1 cup) sweetened condensed milk
- 7.5 ml (1.5 tsp) peppermint extract
- About 1.2 L (5 cups) powdered sugar
- 960 ml (4 cups) melting chocolate
- 10 ml (2 tsp) shortening (or coconut oil)

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat pot of water for double boiler

### Directions

1. Add extract to milk
2. Slowly add the powdered sugar until a firm dough forms
3. Roll out on parch paper
4. Cut into discs
5. Freeze until firm (~20 minutes)
6. Use double boiler to melt chocolate and shortening
7. Carefully dip into chocolate with fork
8. Slide onto parchment to cool to room temp before refrigerating at least 30 mins
