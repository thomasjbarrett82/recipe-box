# Perfect Green Smoothie

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Drink, Quick, Vegetarian, Vegan, High fiber
- Image: /images/perfect-green-smoothie.jpg
- Emoji: 🥤
- Serves: 2

## Description

A naturally sweet green smoothie of banana, tropical fruit, dates, almonds and spinach.

## Cook Times

Prep: 5 min
Total: 5 min

## Ingredients

- 1 banana, sliced
- 240 ml (1 cup) tropical fruit
- 3 pitted dates
- 30 ml (1/8 cup) raw almonds
- 480 ml (2 cups) baby spinach
- 360 ml (1 ½ cups) water

## Method

### Prep

1. If desired, combine all ingredients in a wide-mouth mason jar
2. Freeze for later use

### Directions

1. Add all ingredients to a strong blender
2. Blend until smooth
