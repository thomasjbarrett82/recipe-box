# Pie Dough

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert
- Image: /images/pie-dough.jpg
- Serves: 8

## Description

Flaky all-butter pie dough, with an option to use vodka for extra tenderness.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Chill: 2 h
Total: 2 h 20 min

## Ingredients

- 350 g flour, plus more for dusting
- 25 g sugar
- 5 g kosher salt
- 285 g (2 ½ sticks) cold butter, cut into ½” pieces
- 90 ml (6 tbsp) cold water or vodka

## Method

1. Whisk flour, sugar, and salt in a large bowl
2. Add butter pieces and squish with fingers to combine with flour
   - Can also use a food processor
3. Sprinkle with water/vodka and fold until flour is moistened
4. Chill in fridge for at least 30 minutes
5. Remove and place on dusted surface
6. Roll out into large disk
7. Fold into halves 2 or 3 times
8. Divide the block into 2 pieces
9. Wrap tightly in plastic and chill for at least 2 hours before using
