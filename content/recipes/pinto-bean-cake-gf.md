# Pinto Bean Cake (GF)

- Serves: 12

## Description

A moist, gluten-free cake made from pinto beans, peanuts and honey.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Bake: 45 min
Cool: 10 min
Total: 1 h 10 min

## Ingredients

- 2 (765 g / 27 oz) cans pinto beans, drained
- 360 ml (1 1/2 cups) peanuts
- 180 ml (3/4 cup) honey
- 5 eggs
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) melted butter
- 10 ml (2 tsp) baking powder

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 190 °C (375 °F)
2. Grease a 9 inch springform pan

### Directions

1. Add beans, peanuts, honey, eggs to food processor
2. Blend until smooth
3. Transfer to greased pan
4. Bake for 45 minutes
5. Let cool at least 10 minutes before frosting and serving
