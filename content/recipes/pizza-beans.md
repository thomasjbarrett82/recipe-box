# Pizza Beans

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, High fiber
- Source: Subversive Table
- Image: /images/pizza-beans.jpg
- Serves: 6

## Description

White beans baked in pizza sauce with mushrooms, mozzarella and your favorite toppings.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Cook: 40 min
Total: 50 min

## Ingredients

- 15 ml (1 tbsp) EVOO
- 1 small onion, minced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 can mushrooms, drained
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) Italian seasoning
- 5 ml (1 tsp) chili flakes
- 795 g (28 oz) pizza or pasta sauce
- 795 g (28 oz) canned white beans, drained and rinsed
- 480 ml (2 cups) mozzarella cheese
- Toppings (olives, picled veggies, pepperoni, etc.)
- Parmesan

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat large CI skillet over med heat
2. Preheat oven to 205 °C (400 °F)

### Directions

1. Saute onion in EVOO until softened
2. Add garlic and mushrooms for a few minutes
3. Add seasonings until fragrant
4. Add sauce and beans and simmer for 5-10 minutes
5. Cover with mozzarella and toppings
6. Move to oven for ~30 mins until bubbling and golden brown
7. Remove and add Parmesan and fresh herbs, if desired
8. Let cool 5 mins before serving
