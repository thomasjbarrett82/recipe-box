# Pizza Casserole

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Pasta, Favorite
- Image: /images/pizza-casserole.jpg
- Serves: 8

## Description

Penne baked with pizza sauce, tomatoes, pepperoni and lots of mozzarella.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Bake: 45 min
Total: 1 h 5 min

## Ingredients

- EVOO
- 455 g (1 lb) penne pasta
- 1 (395 g / 14 oz) jar pizza or spaghetti sauce
- 1 (395 g / 14 oz) can diced tomatoes
- 170 g (6 oz) sliced pepperoni
- 2 (225 g / 8 oz) packages shredded Mozzarella (960 ml / 4 cups)
- Parmesan cheese

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)
2. Lightly grease a 9x13 baking dish with EVOO

### Directions

1. Cook the pasta to al dente and drain
2. In a medium bowl combine the sauce and tomatoes (with liquid)
3. Pour half the pasta in the baking dish
4. Pour half the sauce over the pasta
5. Place a layer of pepperoni over the sauce
6. Sprinkle half the Mozzarella on the pepperoni
7. Repeat the layering with the remaining pasta, sauce, pepperoni, & cheese
   - Make sure all pasta is covered
8. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes
9. Remove foil and bake another 20 minutes until bubbly & brown
10. Allow to cool for 5 minutes before serving

Can be frozen before baking to cook later. Remove from freezer 1 day before to thaw. Bake for 1 hour covered and 15 minutes uncovered.
