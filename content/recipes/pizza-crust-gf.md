# Pizza Crust (GF)

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten free
- Source: Brian Lagerstrom
- Image: /images/pizza-crust-gf.jpg
- Serves: 8

## Description

A gluten-free pizza crust with a short rise, par-baked on a pizza stone.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Rise: 90 min
Bake: 10 min
Total: 1 h 55 min

## Ingredients

- 220 g or 240 ml (1 cup) warm water
- 7 g or 11.5 ml (2 1/4 tsp) instant yeast
- 3 g or 3.25 ml (2/3 tsp) baking soda
- 8 g or 10 ml (2 tsp) sugar
- 5 g or 5 ml (1 tsp)  salt
- 15 g or 19 ml (1 1/4 tbsp) olive oil
- 360 g or 720 ml (3 cups) gluten free ap flour (i recommend king arthur or bob’s red mill 1 to 1)
- Pizza sauce and toppings

## Method

### Prep

1. Before cooking heat oven to 260 °C (500 °F) with pizza stone or steel inside

### Directions

1. Into a medium/large bowl, add water, yeast, baking soda, sugar, salt, olive oil, and flour.
2. Stir to combine.
3. Once a ball begins to form, switch to using a soaking wet hand to finish mixing until a ball is formed.
4. Cover and allow to “rise” on the counter for 90 minutes.
5. Divide into 2 even pieces and round each into a ball.
6. Add a little water if ball is crumbly.
7. Oil 2 pieces parchment and place ball between them.
8. Use large flat object to press into wide round, then rolling pin to spread out to 12" rounds.
   - Gently push the dough out, don't roll it, rolling will crack the dough.
9. Discard the top parchment paper.
10. Mark dough with 12" pan or object and cut off extra dough.
11. Use butter knife to gently push in edges and create a lip.
12. Add pizza sauce and toppings.
13. Gently transfer pizza with paper under it to the pizza stone.
14. Cook for 7 minutes and remove to cool for a couple minutes.
15. Remove from parchment paper and return to pizza stone for 2-3 minutes to crisp the crust.
