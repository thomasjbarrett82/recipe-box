# Pizza Pizza

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Vegetarian
- Image: /images/pizza-pizza.jpg
- Serves: 4

## Description

New York-style pizza from a cold-fermented 24-hour dough.

## Cook Times

Prep: 30 min
Cold rise: 24 h
Bake: 7–10 min
Rest: 1 h 30 min
Total: 26 h 10 min

## Ingredients

- 30 ml (2 tbsp) sugar
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) kosher salt
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) + 10 ml (2 tsp) EVOO
- 180 ml (¾ cup) warm water
- 480 ml (2 cups) bread flour
- 5 ml (1 tsp) instant yeast
- 45 g (1.5 oz) pizza sauce
- Shredded cheese
- Additional toppings, as desired

## Method

### Prep

1. Prepare the dough 24 hours ahead
2. Preheat the oven to 260 °C (500 °F) and move rack to the bottom

### Directions

1. Put the sugar, salt, 15 ml (1 tbsp) oil, water, yeast, and flour into a bread machine or standing mixer bowl
2. Run through a dough cycle, or mix for 15-20 minutes on medium speed
3. Roll out the dough into a smooth ball and place into a glass bowl
4. Coat with 10 ml (2 tsp) oil and cover with plastic wrap
5. Refrigerate for 24 hours
6. Remove the dough 1 hour before cooking and split into 2 equal balls
7. Cover and rest for 30 minutes
8. Stretch or throw the dough to form a round disc
9. Place onto a pizza peel
10. Brush the dough with EVOO
11. Spread pizza sauce evenly around the pizza
12. Sprinkle toppings and cheese over the sauce
13. Bake for 7-10 minutes until golden brown and bubbly
14. Rest for 3-5 minutes before slicing and serving
