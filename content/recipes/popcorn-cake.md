# Popcorn Cake

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert, Holiday, Minnesota
- Image: /images/popcorn-cake.jpg
- Emoji: 🍿
- Serves: 16

## Description

A festive no-bake cake of popcorn, peanuts and M&M's held together with marshmallow.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Set: 1 h
Total: 1 h 20 min

## Ingredients

- 455 g (1 lb) marshmallows
- 120 ml (½ cup) oil
- 120 ml (½ cup) butter
- 4.8 L (20 cups) popped corn
- 240 ml (1 cup) peanuts
- 240 ml (1 cup) M&M’s
- Food coloring

## Method

1. Microwave marshmallows, butter, and oil
2. Go 30 seconds at a time until melted together
3. Mix remaining ingredients together
4. Pour into greased angel food pan
5. Let cool and tip out when done
