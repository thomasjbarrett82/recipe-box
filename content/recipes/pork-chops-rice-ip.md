# Pork Chops and Rice (IP)

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Instant Pot
- Image: /images/pork-chops-rice-ip.jpg
- Serves: 6

## Description

Pork chops, rice and green beans cooked together in cream of mushroom sauce in the Instant Pot.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Cook: 30 min
Total: 40 min

## Ingredients

- 455–905 g (1–2 lb) pork chops (not thick cut)
- Salt & pepper
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) EVOO
- 480 ml (2 cups) rice, rins
- 720 ml (3 cups) water (3.5 for brown rice)
- 1 can cream of mushroom soup
- 1 can French-cut green beans

## Method

### Prep

1. Set Instant Pot to sauté to pre-heat

### Directions

1. Season the chops with salt and pepper
2. Heat the oil in the pot
3. Brown the chops in the oil for 1-2 minutes on each side
4. Remove chops and set aside
5. Deglaze the pot with a little water
6. Add the rice and water
7. Place the chops on top of the rice
8. Pour the soup and beans on top of the chops, and do not stir anything
9. Season with some more salt & pepper
10. Close and seal the pot
11. Cook on high pressure for 10 (white rice) or 15 (brown rice) minutes
12. Let pressure naturally release for 5 minutes, and then quick release
