# Pork Rub with Coffee

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Quick
- Serves: 12

## Description

A sweet and smoky dry rub for pork with ground coffee, chili powder and brown sugar.

## Cook Times

Prep: 5 min
Total: 5 min

## Ingredients

- 120 ml (½ cup) brown sugar
- 60 ml (¼ cup) kosher salt
- 10 ml (2 tsp) garlic powder
- 5 ml (1 tsp) chili powder
- 5 ml (1 tsp) ground coffee
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) lemon pepper (optional)
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) celery salt
- 5 ml (1 tsp) black pepper
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) allspice
- 5 ml (1 tsp) smoked paprika

## Method

1. Mix all ingredients together in a bowl.
2. Store in an airtight container. Rub generously over pork before smoking, roasting or grilling.
