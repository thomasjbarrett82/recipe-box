# Pork Rub

- Image: /images/pork-rub.jpg

- Serves: 17

## Description

An all-purpose sweet and savory dry rub for pork.

## Cook Times

Prep: 5 min
Total: 5 min

## Ingredients

- 120 ml (½ cup) brown sugar
- 60 ml (¼ cup) kosher salt
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) paprika
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) chili powder
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) mustard powder
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) garlic powder
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) onion powder
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) black pepper
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) Italian seasoning
- 5 ml (1 tsp) ginger

## Method

1. Mix all ingredients together in a bowl.
2. Store in an airtight container. Rub generously over pork before smoking, roasting or grilling.
