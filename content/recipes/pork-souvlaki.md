# Pork Souvlaki

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Grill
- Source: Serious Eats
- Image: /images/pork-souvlaki.jpg
- Serves: 4

## Description

Lemon, garlic and oregano marinated pork skewers, grilled and served with tzatziki.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Marinate: 2 h
Grill: 15 min
Total: 2 h 35 min

## Ingredients

### Meat

- 120 ml (1/2 cup) EVOO
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) red onion, chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 60 ml (4 tbsp) lemon juice
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) red wine vinegar
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) oregano
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) kosher salt
- 680 g (1.5 lb) boneless pork loin, cubed
- Only use lean pork, no pork shoulder or roasts

### Tzatziki

- 240 ml (1 cup) cucumber, peeled and shredded
- 480 ml (2 cups) Greek yogurt
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) lemon juice
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) EVOO
- 5 ml (1 tsp) kosher salt

### To serve

- Pita bread, preferably fresh

## Method

### Prep

1. Mix marinade and meat together and refrigerate for 2 hours
2. Mix tzatziki together and refrigerate

### Directions

1. Heat charcoal grill to high heat
2. Add the meat to kabob skewers
3. Grill pork until charred and cooked through
4. Move to plate and rest for 5 mins
5. Optionally, lightly grill the pita bread to toast it
6. Serve by removing pork to pita bread and lay some tzatziki across the meat
