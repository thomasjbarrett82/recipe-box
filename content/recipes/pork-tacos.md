# Pork Tacos

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Dutch oven
- Source: Brian Lagerstrom
- Serves: 12

## Description

Pork shoulder braised with citrus, cinnamon and bay, then crisped in its own fat for tacos.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Season: 2–8 h
Cook: 4 h 30 min
Rest: 6–8 h
Total: 12 h 50 min

## Ingredients

- 2.3 kg (5 lb) pork shoulder, cut into 3 inch cubes, bone removed
- 45 ml (3 tbsp) kosher salt
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) dried oregano
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) onion powder
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) garlic powder
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) black pepper
- EVOO
- Chicken stock
- 1 cinnamon stick
- 3 bay leaves
- 1 orange, halved and deseeded (ALTERNATIVE: 2 limes, halved and deseeded)
- 60 ml (1/4 cup) rendered pork fat, reserved from cooking
- 240 ml (1 cup) pork liquid, reserved from cooking
- OPTIONAL: 45–60 ml (3–4 tbsp) lime juice
- Serve with: warm tortillas, white onion, cilantro, pickled onions, salsa

## Method

### Prep 1

1. Combine pork with salt, oregano, onion & garlic powder, black pepper in large bowl
2. Refrigerate 2-8 hours to cure

### Prep 2

1. Heat EVOO in Dutch oven over med-high heat
2. Sear pork to get some extra flavor
3. Pack pork in bottom of pot with cinnamon stick, bay leaves, orange
4. Add enough stock to almost cover the pork
5. Bring to a boil
6. Cover and simmer for 3 hours until almost fork tender
7. Remove from heat and let rest, covered, for 1 hour
8. Move to fridge and let sit 6-8 hours (or overnight)

### Directions

1. Move pot to the stove top
2. Remove pork pieces from the pot and set aside
3. Remove the rendered fat and set aside
4. Remove the pork liquid (gelatinized now) and set aside
5. Add lime juice, if using it
6. Return pork to pot and gently shred by hand
7. Mix about 60 ml (1/4 cup) fat and 240 ml (1 cup) pork liquid with pork by hand
   - Add more, if needed
8. Heat pot on med-low for about 30 minutes until warmed through
9. OPTIONAL: crisp up the pork in a non-stick pan over med-high heat for carnitas
10. Serve with tortillas and toppings
