# Portabella Burgers

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Spicy, Grill, Vegetarian, High fiber
- Image: /images/portabella-burgers.jpg
- Emoji: 🍄
- Serves: 4

## Description

Balsamic-marinated grilled portabella caps with roasted peppers, pepper jack and avocado.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Marinate: 2 h
Grill: 15 min
Total: 2 h 35 min

## Ingredients

- 4 large portabellas
- 240 ml (1 cup) olive oil
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) balsamic
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 jalapeño, minced
- 2 poblano peppers, halved
- 1 sweet onion, sliced large
- 4 slices pepper-jack cheese
- 2 avocados, sliced
- 4 hamburger buns

## Method

### Prep

1. Marinate mushrooms at least 2 hours before cooking
2. Heat grill to med-high

### Directions

1. Mix oil, balsamic, garlic, & jalapeño in bag
2. Marinate mushrooms at least 2 hours, gill side up
3. Skewer poblanos and onion slices, brush with olive oil
4. Grill mushrooms and skewers until browned
5. Assemble burgers
   - Bun – mushroom – cheese – pepper – onion – avocado - Bun
