# Pot Roast (fancy)

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Dutch oven, High fiber
- Image: /images/pot-roast-fancy.jpg
- Serves: 6

## Description

Chuck roast braised in red wine and crushed tomatoes with rosemary and balsamic.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Braise: 3 h 30 min
Total: 3 h 50 min

## Ingredients

- 1.4 kg (3 lb) chuck roast
- 1 onion, sliced
- 2 stalks celery, diced
- 30–45 ml (2–3 tbsp) tomato paste
- 45 ml (3 tbsp) flour
- 480 ml (2 cups) red wine or beef stock
- 795 g (28 oz) can crushed tomatoes
- 30–45 ml (2–3 tbsp) Worcestershire
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) garlic powder
- 455 g (1 lb) carrots, chopped rough
- 15–30 ml (1–2 tbsp) balsamic vinegar
- 15–30 ml (1–2 tbsp) fresh rosemary, chopped
- Salt & pepper to taste

## Method

### Prep

1. Trim excess fat from roast
2. Heat Dutch oven over med heat with a little oil

### Directions

1. Brown roast in pot
2. Remove roast and add onions & celery
3. Cook until translucent and add tomato paste & flour
4. Deglaze with red wine, crushed tomatoes, Worcestershire, garlic powder
5. Return roast to pot and cover
6. Bake at 175 °C (350 °F) for 2.5 hours
7. Add carrots on top and return to oven, uncovered, for 1 hour
8. Stir in vinegar, rosemary, S&P to finish
9. Serve with mashed or baked potatoes on the side
