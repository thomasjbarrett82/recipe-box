# Potato Chip Cookies

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert
- Source: @bdylanhollis
- Serves: 24

## Description

Buttery chocolate chip cookies with crushed potato chips for a salty crunch.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Chill: 30 min
Bake: 15 min
Total: 1 h

## Ingredients

- 240 ml (1 cup) butter, room temperature
- 300 ml (1 1/4 cups) sugar
- 2 eggs
- 5 ml (1 tsp) vanilla extract
- 225 g (8 oz) potato chips, smashed
- 480 ml (2 cups) flour
- 15 ml (3 tsp) baking powder
- 240 ml (1 cup) chocolate chips

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)

### Directions

1. Cream butter and sugar in large bowl
2. Mix in eggs and vanilla
3. Mix in flour, baking powder, chocolate chips, and half of the smashed chips
4. Put in fridge for 30 mins to firm up
5. Scoop and roll dough in remaining chips
6. Arrange balls on baking pan with parchment paper
7. Bake for 15 mins
8. Let cool before eating
