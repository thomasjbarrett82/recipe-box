# Potato Soup

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Soup
- Serves: 9

## Description

Hearty bacon and potato soup topped with shredded Cheddar.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Cook: 40 min
Total: 55 min

## Ingredients

- 225 g (1/2 lb) bacon, uncooked
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 stalks celery, chopped
- 2.3 kg (5 lb) potatoes, peeled & diced
- 1.9 L (8 cups) water
- 20 ml (4 tsp) chicken bouillon
- Shredded Cheddar

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat large pot over medium

### Directions

1. Fry bacon and drain, reserving 30 ml (2 tbsp) of fat
2. Crumble bacon and set aside
3. Heat fat in pot and cook onion, celery, and garlic for 5 minutes
4. Add potatoes, water, and bouillon
5. Bring to a boil, reduce heat to low and cover
6. Let simmer until potatoes are tender
7. Use potato masher or immersion blender to break up potatoes and thicken soup
8. Stir in crumbled bacon and serve with cheese sprinkled over top
