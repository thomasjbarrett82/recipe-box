# Potato Wedges

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Side, Vegetarian, High fiber
- Image: /images/potato-wedges.jpg
- Serves: 4

## Description

Ranch-seasoned potato wedges made in the air fryer.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Air fry: 30 min
Total: 40 min

## Ingredients

- 905 g (2 lb) Yukon or Gold potatoes, cut into wedges
- 15 + 15 ml (1 + 1 tbsp) EVOO
- 1 packet Ranch seasoning (or other flavors)

## Method

1. Pat wedges dry and toss with 15 ml (1 tbsp) EVOO
2. Add to 205 °C (400 °F) air fryer for 20 mins
3. Remove potatoes and toss with EVOO and ranch
4. Return to fryer for 8-10 minutes until golden brown
