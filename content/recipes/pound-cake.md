# Pound Cake

- Serves: 20

## Description

Old-fashioned pound cake from four ingredients: butter, sugar, eggs and flour.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Bake: 80–100 min
Total: 2 h

## Ingredients

- 455 g (1 lb) butter
- 455 g (1 lb) sugar
- 455 g (1 lb) eggs (~9)
- 455 g (1 lb) flour

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 165 °C (325 °F)
2. Grease and flour a bundt cake pan (or 8x8)

### Directions

1. Cream butter and sugar
2. Mix in eggs thoroughly
3. Stir in flour
4. Add to cake pan
5. Bake for 80-100 mins
6. Let cool before serving with fruit and ice cream/whipped cream
