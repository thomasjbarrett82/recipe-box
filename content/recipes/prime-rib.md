# Prime Rib

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Grill, Smoker, Holiday, Favorite
- Source: Serious Eats
- Image: /images/prime-rib.jpg
- Emoji: 🥩
- Serves: About 1 per 340 g (¾ lb) of bone-in roast

## Description

Dry-brined prime rib, smoked, then slow-roasted and seared, with horseradish cream sauce.

## Cook Times

Prep: 30 min
Dry brine: 24–72 h
Smoke and roast: 4–6 h
Total: 29 h

## Ingredients

- Charcoal + wood chips for smoke
- 1.4–5.4 kg (3–12 lb) prime rib roast
- Worcestershire
- Kosher salt & pepper
- Butcher twine

### Horseradish sauce

- 80 ml (1/3 cup) mayo
- 60 ml (1/4 cup) sour cream
- 60 ml (1/4 cup) creme fraiche
- 60 ml (1/4 cup) prepared horseradish
- 10 ml (2 tsp) lemon juice
- 10 ml (2 tsp) Worcestershire
- 5 ml (1 tsp) white wine vinegar
- 10 ml (2 tsp) chives, chopped
- Salt & pepper to taste

### Pan sauce

- 240 ml (1 cup) good red wine
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) balsamic vinegar

## Method

### Prep

1. Remove bones from roast and set aside
2. Place roast (fat cap up) on wire rack in baking sheet
3. Liberally coat with Worcestershire, salt and pepper
4. Tie off with twine to avoid falling apart
5. Let sit in fridge for 24-72 hours
6. Whisk horseradish sauce ingredients and set aside

### Directions

1. Heat convection oven to 220 °C (425 °F)
2. Remove roast from fridge and let come to room temp
3. Set up grill for short smoke cycle
4. Smoke roast for 90-120 minutes at ~107 °C (225 °F)
5. Meanwhile toss bones with some oil on pan and roast for 45-60 minutes
6. Heat oven to 107 °C (225 °F)
7. Move bones to small stock pot, deglazing with a little water
8. Cover bones with water to simmer uncovered on low-med heat
9. Move roast to oven and tent with foil
10. Slow roast until center is 43 °C (110 °F) (~4 hours)
11. Remove, keeping foil, and let rest for 30-60 minutes
12. Strain out solids from stock (can remove any excess fat at this time)
13. Heat stock on med heat to reduce by half
14. Heat wine, balsamic, 5 ml (1 tsp) Worcestershire in another pan to reduce
15. Add reduced wine to stock
16. Season with a bit of salt & pepper
17. Heat convection oven to 260 °C (500 °F)
18. Remove foil and roast for 5-15 minutes to crisp the exterior (don't go over 49 °C (120 °F) internal)
19. Remove and rest for at least 5 minutes
20. Carve and serve immediately with sauces
