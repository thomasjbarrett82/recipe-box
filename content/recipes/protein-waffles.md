# Protein Waffles

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Breakfast, Quick, Vegetarian
- Source: Instagram
- Image: /images/protein-waffles.jpg
- Serves: 2

## Description

High-protein oat waffles made with protein powder and egg.

## Cook Times

Prep: 5 min
Cook: 10 min
Total: 15 min

## Ingredients

- 40 g rolled oats
- 60 g gluten free flour
- 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) baking powder
- 1 scoop protein powder
- 1 egg
- 120+ ml skim milk

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat waffle iron

### Directions

1. Blend all ingredients until smooth
2. Add more milk, if needed, to get a pourable consistency
3. Cook batter in waffle iron
4. Makes ~4-5 mini waffles
