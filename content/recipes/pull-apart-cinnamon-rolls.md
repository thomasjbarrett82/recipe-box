# Pull-Apart Cinnamon Rolls

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Breakfast, Vegetarian
- Image: /images/pull-apart-cinnamon-rolls.jpg
- Emoji: 🥐
- Serves: 12

## Description

Easy pull-apart cinnamon rolls made from canned biscuits baked in melted ice cream, butter and brown sugar.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Bake: 30 min
Total: 40 min

## Ingredients

- 4 cans Pillsbury biscuits, quartered
- 120 ml (½ cup) butter
- 360 ml (1 ½ cups) brown sugar
- 240 ml (1 cup) vanilla ice cream
- Cinnamon

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)

### Directions

1. Place biscuits in greased 9x13 casserole dish
2. Melt butter in saucepan
3. Add sugar and stir
4. Heat until starting to boil around edges
5. Add ice cream and return to a boil
6. Pour sauce over biscuits
7. Sprinkle with cinnamon
8. Bake for 30 minutes until biscuits are done
