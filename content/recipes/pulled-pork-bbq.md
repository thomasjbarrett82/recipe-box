# Pulled Pork BBQ

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Smoker
- Serves: 12

## Description

Smoked pork butt with a sweet paprika rub, pulled for sandwiches.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Dry rub: 4–8 h
Smoke: 10–14 h
Total: 14 h 50 min

## Ingredients

- 1 whole pork butt, 2.3–3.2 kg (5–7 lb)
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) paprika
- 60 ml (¼ cup) brown sugar
- 45 ml (3 tbsp) kosher salt
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) whole mustard seed
- 5 ml (1 tsp) black pepper
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) garlic powder
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) onion powder
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) dried oregano
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) whole coriander seed
- 5 ml (1 tsp) red pepper flakes

## Method

### Prep

1. Mix spice rub together and cover pork with it
2. Let sit in fridge for 4-8 hours before cooking

### Directions

1. Place in BBQ smoker at 120 °C (250 °F)
2. Plan for approximately 1 hour per pound, plus 2 hours for any stall
3. Cook until internal temp is 91 °C (195 °F)
4. If necessary, cover with foil and move into oven to cook faster (350)
5. Remove and let rest for at least 30 minutes before shredding
