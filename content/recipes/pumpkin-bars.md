# Pumpkin Bars

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert, Holiday
- Image: /images/pumpkin-bars.jpg
- Emoji: 🎃
- Serves: 24

## Description

Spiced pumpkin sheet-cake bars with cream cheese frosting.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Bake: 30 min
Chill: 1 h
Total: 1 h 50 min

## Ingredients

### Wet

- 4 eggs
- 480 ml (2 cups) sugar
- 240 ml (1 cup) veg. oil
- 1 (425 g / 15 oz) can pumpkin puree (480 ml / 2 cups)

### Dry

- 480 ml (2 cups) A/P flour, sifted
- 10 ml (2 tsp) baking powder
- 10 ml (2 tsp) cinnamon
- 5 ml (1 tsp) salt
- 5 ml (1 tsp) baking soda
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) cloves
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) nutmeg
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) allspice

### Frosting

- 225 g (2 sticks) butter, softened
- 10 ml (2 tsp) vanilla extract
- 960 ml (4 cups) powdered sugar, sifted
- 2 (170 g / 6 oz) cream cheese packages, softened

## Method

### Bars

1. Mix the wet ingredients together until light and fluffy
2. In a large bowl, stir together the dry ingredients
3. Combine the wet into the dry
4. Pour into a greased 9x13 pan
5. Bake at 350 for 30 minutes
6. Remove and let cool completely

### Frosting

1. Mix the frosting ingredients together in another bowl
2. Spread on fully cooled bars

Refrigerate for at least 1 hour before serving
