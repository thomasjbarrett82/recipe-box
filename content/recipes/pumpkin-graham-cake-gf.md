# Pumpkin Graham Cake (GF)

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert, Gluten free, Holiday
- Source: B. Dylan Hollis
- Image: /images/pumpkin-graham-cake-gf.jpg
- Serves: 10

## Description

A gluten-free pumpkin cake made from cinnamon graham crackers, with cream cheese frosting.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Bake: 50 min
Total: 1 h 5 min

## Ingredients

- 425 g (15 oz) GF cinnamon graham crackers (2 packages)
- 15 ml (3 tsp) baking powder
- 2 eggs
- 425 g (15 oz) can pumpkin puree
- 5 ml (1 tsp) vegetable oil
- Cream cheese frosting

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 165 °C (325 °F)
2. Grease a bread loaf pan

### Directions

1. Use food processor to turn graham crackers into crumbs (~960 ml (4 cups))
2. Add crumbs to large bowl and stir in baking powder
3. In medium bowl mix eggs, puree, and oil
4. Stir into crumbs to combine
5. Pour into greased pan and smooth the top
6. Bake for ~50 minutes
7. Remove and let cool completely before frosting
