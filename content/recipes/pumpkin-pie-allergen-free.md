# Pumpkin Pie (Allergen Free)

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert, Gluten free, Dairy free, Holiday
- Image: /images/pumpkin-pie-allergen-free.jpg
- Serves: 8

## Description

Pumpkin pie made free of dairy, eggs and gluten, in a gluten-free graham cracker crust.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Bake: 45–50 min
Cool: 2 h
Total: 3 h

## Ingredients

### Crust

- 360 ml (1 ½ cups) crushed GF graham crackers
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) water
- 45 ml (3 tbsp) butter substitute, melted

### Filling

- 80 ml (1/3 cup) rice milk
- 15 ml (3 tsp) powdered egg substitute
- 480 ml (2 cups) unsweetened pumpkin puree (1 can)
- 180 ml (¾ cup) firmly packed brown sugar
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) GF flour
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) salt
- 7.5 ml (1 ½ tsp) ground cinnamon
- 1.25 ml (¼ tsp) ground nutmeg
- 1.25 ml (¼ tsp) ground ginger
- 1.25 ml (¼ tsp) ground allspice
- 240 ml (1 cup) coconut creamer
- 10 ml (2 tsp) pure vanilla extract

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 205 °C (400 °F)

### Directions

1. Place crackers in food processor and pulse until finely ground
2. Add water & butter, pulse until combined
3. Press into pie plate and up sides
4. Bake 4-6 minutes, until set and lightly golden
5. Remove to cool
6. In large bowl, whisk together rice milk and egg substitute
7. Whisk in remaining ingredients until well combined
8. Pour mixture into pie plate
9. Back until filling is set but slightly wobbly in center
10. 40-45 minutes
11. Toothpick inserted into center will come out mostly clean
