# Punch

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Drink, Quick, Holiday
- Serves: 19

## Description

Party punch of lemon-lime soda, ginger ale and sherbet.

## Cook Times

Prep: 5 min
Total: 5 min

## Ingredients

- 2L bottle lemon-lime soda
- 2L bottle ginger ale
- 470 ml (1 pint) sherbet

## Method

1. Mix sodas in a large punch bowl
2. Add sherbet to bowl by the scoop
