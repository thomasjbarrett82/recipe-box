# Puppy Chow

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Appetizer, Quick, Holiday, Minnesota
- Serves: 16

## Description

Crispix or Chex coated in melted chocolate and peanut butter, then shaken in powdered sugar. Also known as muddy buddies.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Cool: 15 min
Total: 30 min

## Ingredients

- 1.4 L (6 cups) Crispix or Rice Chex
- 115 g (1 stick) butter
- 170 g (6 oz) milk chocolate chips
- 120 ml (½ cup) peanut butter
- 360 ml (1 ½ cups) powdered sugar

## Method

1. Pour cereal into a large bowl
2. Melt butter, chocolate chips, and peanut butter in double boiler
3. Pour over cereal and mix carefully
4. Sprinkle with powdered sugar while mixing
   - Alternately, toss in a large paper bag with sugar
5. Spread onto cookie sheet to cool
