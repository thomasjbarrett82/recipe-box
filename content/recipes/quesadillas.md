# Quesadillas

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Quick
- Source: Tom
- Serves: 4

## Description

Taco-seasoned chicken and melted cheese in crisp tortillas, with your favorite toppings.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Cook: 20 min
Total: 30 min

## Ingredients

- EVOO or vegetable oil
- 455 g (1 lb) chicken breast or thigh, chopped
- Taco seasoning
- Flour or corn tortillas
- Shredded cheese
- Salt
- Toppings (sour cream, jalapenos, salsa, etc.)

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat pan over medium heat

### Directions

1. Add oil to pan when it's hot enough to shimmer
2. Cook chicken in pan until no longer pink
3. Add taco seasoning and cook for a minute before removing and setting aside
4. Change stove temperature to medium-low
5. Wipe out the pan and put back on stove
6. Add a small amount of oil to the pan and a tortilla
7. Add some seasoned chicken and shredded cheese
8. If the tortilla is large enough, fold it in half
9. If the tortilla is smaller, add another tortilla on the top
10. Let cook for a bit until golden brown, then flip
11. Sprinkle a bit of salt on the cooked tortilla
12. Let cook until golden brown, then remove and serve with toppings
13. Repeat with the rest of the cooked chicken
