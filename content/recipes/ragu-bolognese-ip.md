# Ragu Bolognese (IP)

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Pasta, Instant Pot
- Serves: 12

## Description

A rich, long-cooked-tasting Bolognese of beef, pork, pancetta and chicken livers, made in the pressure cooker.

## Cook Times

Prep: 30 min
Cook: 1 h 15 min
Total: 1 h 45 min

## Ingredients

- 240 ml (1 cup) chicken stock
- 4 packets unflavored gelatin
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) EVOO
- 225 g (½ lb) pancetta, diced
- 1 large onion, minced
- 2 large carrots, diced
- 2 celery stalks, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 60 ml (¼ cup) fresh sage, minced
- 120 ml (½ cup) fresh parsley, minced
- 225 g (½ lb) chicken livers, minced
- 905 g (2 lb) ground beef
- 455 g (1 lb) ground pork
- 480 ml (2 cups) dry red wine
- 395 g (14 oz) can crushed tomatoes
- 1 + 120 ml (½ cup) heavy cream
- 2 bay leaves
- 85 g (3 oz) Parmesan
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) soy sauce
- 60 ml (¼ cup) fresh basil, minced

## Method

1. Bloom the gelatin in the stock
2. Heat EVOO and brown the pancetta
3. Add onions, carrots, celery, garlic, sage, parsley and cook for 5 minutes
4. Add meat and cook until browned and excess liquid is evaporated
5. Add stock, wine, tomatoes, 240 ml (1 cup) cream, bay leaves
6. Cook under pressure for 30 minutes, fast release
7. Simmer until thickened
8. Stir in remaining ingredients
9. Season to taste with salt and pepper
10. Serve with tossed pasta
