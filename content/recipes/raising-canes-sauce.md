# Raising Cane's Sauce

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Side
- Source: copykat
- Image: /images/raising-canes-sauce.jpg
- Serves: 13

## Description

A copycat of Raising Cane's dipping sauce: mayo, ketchup, Worcestershire, garlic and plenty of pepper.

## Cook Times

Prep: 5 min
Chill: 1 h
Total: 1 h 5 min

## Ingredients

- 240 ml (1 cup) mayo (Hellman's or Dukes)
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) Heinz ketchup
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) + 5 ml (1 tsp) Worcestershire
- 5 ml (1 tsp) garlic powder
- 1.25 ml (1/4 tsp) salt
- 5 ml (1 tsp) black pepper

## Method

1. Mix all ingredients in small bowl
2. Refrigerate in air-tight container for 1+ hours before serving
