# Red Beans & Rice

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Spicy, Dutch oven, High fiber
- Serves: 8

## Description

New Orleans red beans simmered with andouille, the holy trinity and Cajun seasoning, served over rice.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Soak: overnight
Cook: 2–3 h
Total: 2 h 20 min

## Ingredients

- 455 g (1 lb) dry red beans
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) lard or vegetable oil
- 455 g (1 lb) Andouille sausage, chopped
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 green bell pepper, diced
- 4 celery ribs, chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) kosher salt
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) black pepper
- 1 bay leaf
- 5 ml (1 tsp) ground sage
- 5 ml (1 tsp) Cajun seasoning
- 960 ml–1.2 L (4–5 cups) chicken stock
- 1 ham hock (optional)
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) Worcestershire
- Hot sauce to taste
- 480 ml (2 cups) uncooked rice, prepared separately

## Method

### Prep

1. Cover beans with 2x water and let sit overnight, drain and rinse before cooking
2. Heat large Dutch oven over med-high heat

### Directions

1. Brown sausage in Dutch oven with the lard
2. Remove and set aside, leaving the grease
3. Add onion, pepper, celery, salt, & black pepper and cook for 5 minutes
4. Add garlic and cook for 1 minute
5. Add seasonings, beans, and the ham hock (if using)
6. Add enough chicken stock to cover the ingredients
7. Bring to a boil and then reduce to a simmer
8. After 1 hour when the beans are soft, smash about ½ of the beans against the pot
9. Continue cooking until beans are tender, 1-2 hours longer
10. Add chicken stock, if necessary, to keep from drying out
11. Discard bay leaf (and ham hock bone, if using)
12. Stir in the Worcestershire and add hot sauce to taste
13. Serve over cooked rice
