# Reuben Sandwiches

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Slow cooker, High fiber, Favorite
- Image: /images/reuben-sandwiches.jpg
- Serves: 4

## Description

Corned beef slow-cooked with sauerkraut, onion and caraway, piled on rye with Swiss and Thousand Island.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Slow cook: 5–6 h
Total: 5 h 30 min

## Ingredients

- 680 g (1 ½ lb) corned beef
- 480 ml (2 cups) drained sauerkraut
- 120 ml (½ cup) beef broth
- 1 sweet onion, sliced
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1.25 ml (¼ tsp) caraway seeds
- 4-6 peppercorns
- Rye bread
- Sliced Swiss cheese
- 1000 Island dressing

## Method

1. Trim excess fat from corned beef and place into crockpot
2. Add sauerkraut, broth, onion, garlic, seeds, & peppercorns on top
3. Cover and cook on LOW for 5-6 hours
4. Remove beef and cut across grain into slices
5. Remove sauerkraut mixture and drain
6. Layer beef and sauerkraut on rye bread, and top with Swiss cheese and dressing
