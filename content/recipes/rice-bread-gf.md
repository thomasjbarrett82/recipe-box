# Rice Bread (GF)

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Bread, Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten free
- Image: /images/rice-bread-gf.jpg
- Serves: 12

## Description

A simple gluten-free yeast bread made from rice flour and psyllium husk.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Rise: 1 h
Bake: 25–35 min
Total: 1 h 50 min

## Ingredients

- 240 ml (1 cup) warm water
- 5 ml (1 tsp) yeast
- 480 ml (2 cups) hot water
- 455 g (1 lb) rice flour
- 5 ml (1 tsp) psyllium husks
- 5 ml (1 tsp) salt

## Method

### Prep

1. Stir yeast into 240 ml (1 cup) warm water and let sit to start fermenting

### Directions

1. Bring hot water to a slow boil
2. Slowly mix 115 g (¼ lb) flour and husks into hot water until it’s a gelatinous goo
3. Transfer to a mixing bowl
4. Mix in yeast water once it has cooled to ~38 °C (100 °F)
5. Mix in remaining rice flour and salt
6. Let rise in fridge overnight
7. Transfer to greased bread or cast-iron pan
8. Bake at 205 °C (400 °F) for 25-35 minutes
