# Rice Corn Bread

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Bread
- Source: @bdylanhollis
- Serves: 9

## Description

An old-fashioned spoon-style cornbread made with scalded milk and cooked rice.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Bake: 30 min
Total: 45 min

## Ingredients

- 360 ml (1 1/2 cups) scalded milk
- 360 ml (1 1/2 cups) cornmeal
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) butter
- 1 egg
- 240 ml (1 cup) cooked mushy rice
- 20 ml (4 tsp) baking powder
- pinch of salt

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 205 °C (400 °F)
2. Grease 8x8 baking dish

### Directions

1. Pour milk over cornmeal & butter
2. Cool
3. Add beaten egg, rice, baking powder, salt
4. Pour into dish
5. Bake for 30 mins
6. Let cool before serving with butter and honey
