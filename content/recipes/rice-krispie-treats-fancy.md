# Rice Krispie Treats (Fancy)

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert, Favorite
- Source: Alton Brown
- Image: /images/rice-krispie-treats-fancy.jpg
- Serves: 24

## Description

Grown-up Rice Krispie treats with browned Kerrygold butter, extra marshmallows, a splash of bourbon and vanilla.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Set: 1 h
Total: 1 h 15 min

## Ingredients

- 1 box Rice Krispies
- 115 g (1 stick) butter (Kerry Gold is preferred)
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) kosher salt
- 2 bags Jet-Puffed mini marshmallows
- 60 ml (1/4 cup) bourbon
- 5 ml (1 tsp) vanilla extract

## Method

1. Lightly toast the cereal in a large wok over medium-high heat
2. Remove to a large bowl
3. Brown the butter in the wok
4. Add the salt
5. Turn down the heat to low
6. Add the marshmallows, bourbon, vanilla
7. Stir until melted
8. Add the cereal back in and stir until thoroughly combined
9. Transfer to lightly greased casserole dish
10. Let cool before serving
