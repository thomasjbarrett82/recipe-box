# Rice Krispie Treats

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert
- Serves: 24

## Description

Classic Rice Krispie treats, extra gooey with a pinch of salt.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Set: 1 h
Total: 1 h 10 min

## Ingredients

- 115 g (1 stick) butter, melted
- 2 bags Jet-Puffed mini marshmallows
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) kosher salt
- 1 box Rice Krispies

## Method

1. Mix butter, marshmallows, & salt in large bowl
2. Microwave in 30 second increments, stirring, until fully melted
3. Add the cereal in and stir until thoroughly combined
4. Transfer to lightly greased casserole dish
5. Let cool before serving
