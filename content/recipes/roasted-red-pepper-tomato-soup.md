# Roasted Red Pepper & Tomato Soup

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Soup, Vegetarian, High fiber
- Image: /images/roasted-red-pepper-tomato-soup.jpg
- Emoji: 🫑
- Serves: 4

## Description

Roma tomatoes, peppers, onion and garlic roasted and blended into a smooth soup, served hot or chilled.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Cook: 50 min
Total: 1 h 5 min

## Ingredients

- 1 kg (2 ¼ lb) Roma tomatoes, halved & deseeded
- 3 bell peppers, quartered & deseeded
- 1 onion, wedged
- 4 cloves garlic
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) olive oil
- Kosher salt & black pepper
- 5 ml (1 tsp) thyme
- 240–480 ml (1–2 cups) water
- Optional: heavy cream

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 230 °C (450 °F)

### Directions

1. Arrange tomatoes (cut-side up), peppers, onions, garlic on baking sheet
2. Drizzle oil over everything
3. Sprinkle generously with salt & pepper
4. Roast in oven until browned, ~40 minutes
5. Remove and let cool
6. Transfer to blender, food processor, or use stick blender
7. Add thyme leaves
8. Puree, adding water as needed to get desired consistency
9. Chill for 3-24 hours to let flavors mix together
10. Garnish with heavy cream, if desired
11. Serve hot or cold
