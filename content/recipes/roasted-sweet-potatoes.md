# Roasted Sweet Potatoes

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Side, Vegetarian, High fiber, Holiday
- Serves: 8

## Description

Sweet potatoes roasted cut-side down until deeply browned, with parsley and an optional drizzle of honey.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Roast: 50 min
Rest: 1 h
Total: 2 h

## Ingredients

- 1.4 kg (3 lb) sweet potatoes, chopped into ½ cubes
- 75 ml (5 tbsp) + 15 ml (1 tbsp) EVOO
- Kosher salt & black pepper
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) fresh parsley, chopped
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) honey (optional)

## Method

### Prep

1. Preheat oven to 205 °C (400 °F)

### Directions

1. Place sweet potatoes in large pot and cover with water
2. Heat water to 71 °C (160 °F), then cover and set aside for 1 hour
3. Drain sweet potatoes and transfer to a large bowl
4. Toss with 75 ml (5 tbsp) EVOO and season to taste with salt & pepper
5. Spread on 2 baking sheets and roast until bottom is browned (30 minutes)
6. Flip and roast for another 20 minutes until the other side is browned
7. Transfer back to the large bowl
8. Toss with the remaining 15 ml (1 tbsp) EVOO, parsley, and honey
