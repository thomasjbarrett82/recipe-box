# Roasted Tomato Basil Soup

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Soup, Slow cooker, Vegetarian, Favorite
- Image: /images/roasted-tomato-basil-soup.jpg
- Emoji: 🍅
- Serves: 6

## Description

Canned whole tomatoes roasted with brown sugar and onion, then simmered with basil and finished with evaporated milk.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Cook: 4–5 h
Total: 4 h 40 min

## Ingredients

- 55–795 g (2–28 oz) cans peeled whole tomatoes
- 45 ml (3 tbsp) brown sugar
- 1 onion, diced
- 720 ml (3 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
- 115 g (4 oz) can tomato paste
- 1.25 ml (¼ tsp) allspice
- 60 ml (¼ cup) fresh basil
- 140 g (5 oz) can evaporated milk
- Salt & pepper

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 230 °C (450 °F)

### Directions

1. Line baking sheet with foil and spray
2. Drain tomatoes and reserve liquid
3. Arrange tomatoes in a single layer
4. Sprinkle with brown sugar and onion
5. Roast for 30 minutes
6. Chop or blend ingredients
7. Combine with broth, paste, & allspice in a crockpot
8. Cook on HIGH for 3-4 hours
9. Add basil, milk, and salt & pepper
10. Cook for another 30 minutes
