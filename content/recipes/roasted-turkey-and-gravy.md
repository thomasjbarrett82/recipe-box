# Roasted Turkey and Gravy

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Holiday
- Source: ThatDudeCanCook (YouTube)
- Image: /images/roasted-turkey-and-gravy.jpg
- Serves: 18

## Description

Herb-butter roasted turkey, parts cooked to their ideal temperatures, with pan gravy.

## Cook Times

Prep: 45 min
Roast: 2–3 h
Total: 3 h 15 min

## Ingredients

- 5.4–7.3 kg (12–16 lb) turkey, thawed
- EVOO
- Kosher salt & black pepper
- 2 carrots, peeled and halved
- 1 onion, peeled and halved
- Fresh thyme, whole
- 240 ml (1 cup) kosher salt
- Fresh rosemary leaves
- Fresh sage leaves
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 lemon
- Spray cooking oil

### Gravy (double for large crowd)

- 115 g (4 oz) unsalted butter
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) flour
- 5 ml (1 tsp) Worcestershire

## Method

### Prep

1. Disassemble the turkey into pieces
   - Break joints before cutting
   - Cut out wishbone before cutting out breasts
   - Set aside neck, giblets, backbone, breast bone, tail, wing tips (chunk big pieces)
2. Heat convection oven to 190 °C (375 °F)
3. Toss turkey bones & onion (cut side down) on roasting pan with oil and salt & pepper
4. Add to oven and roast for 30 minutes, flip, roast 30 minutes
5. Add pieces to stock pot, deglazing the roasting pan with a little hot water
6. Top pieces with water
7. Add carrots, thyme
8. Simmer over low-med heat for at least an hour
9. Add kosher salt, rosemary, sage, garlic, lemon zest to food processor
10. Blend for 1 minute until fully integrated (can store in fridge)
11. Arrange turkey pieces on roasting pan and season both sides with rosemary salt
12. Store in fridge for an hour while stock is simmering

### Directions

1. Dab any excess moisture on turkey and light spray of oil for browning
2. Remove breasts & wings when they reach 74 °C (165 °F) (~35 minutes)
3. Remove legs when they reach 82 °C (180 °F) (~1 hour)
4. Deglaze roasting pan back into stock
5. Cut and arrange turkey in large covered pan with 120 ml (1/2 cup) stock and cover with foil
6. Strain solid object out of the stock, setting aside neck
7. Disassemble any meat from the neck
8. Chop neck meat finely and set aside
9. Add butter & flour to large sauce pot and make a roux (same color as stock)
10. Slowly whisk in stock until more liquid than actually desired (will thicken as cools)
11. Season with rosemary salt, pepper, and Worcestershire
12. Stir neck meat into gravy
13. Reheat turkey for 7-8 minutes before serving
