# Russian Tea

## Description

A spiced instant tea mix with Tang, lemonade, cinnamon, allspice and cloves. Stir a spoonful into hot water.

## Cook Times

Prep: 5 min
Total: 5 min

## Ingredients

- 240 ml (1 cup) white sugar
- 480 ml (2 cups) Tang
- 120 ml (½ cup) unsweetened ice tea mix
- 1 pkg. unsweetened lemonade mix
- 5 ml (1 tsp) ground cinnamon
- 5 ml (1 tsp) ground allspice
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) ground cloves

## Method

### Prep

1. Mix all ingredients in airtight container
2. Can be stored for several months

### Directions

1. Mix 15 ml (3 tsp) of mix in 240 ml (1 cup) of boiling water
