# Salsa

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Appetizer, Spicy, Vegetarian, Vegan, Favorite
- Source: Serious Eats
- Image: /images/salsa.jpg
- Serves: 12

## Description

Fresh tomato salsa with peppers, jalapeños, cilantro and a little chipotle in adobo.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Chill: 30 min
Total: 50 min

## Ingredients

- 455 g (1 lb) tomatoes, halved
- 4 cloves garlic
- 1 red onion, halved
- 1 red & 1 green bell pepper, halved
- 3 jalapeños, halved
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) kosher salt
- Olive oil
- 60 ml (¼ cup) fresh cilantro, chopped
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) ground cumin
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) smoked paprika
- 1 chipotle pepper in adobo sauce
- Lime juice to taste (at least 15 ml (1 tbsp))
- Salt & pepper to taste

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat convection oven to 230 °C (450 °F)

### Directions

1. Place tomatoes, garlic, onion, peppers, & jalapeños on a pan
2. Drizzle with olive oil and sprinkle with kosher salt
3. Place in oven and roast until skins start to blacken
4. Place all ingredients in food processor or blender
5. Blend until still slightly chunky
6. Let stand in fridge for at least 30 minutes
