# Santa Fe Chicken

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Spicy, Slow cooker, High fiber
- Image: /images/santa-fe-chicken.jpg
- Serves: 4

## Description

Chicken slow-cooked with tomatoes and chiles, black beans, corn and cumin.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Slow cook: 4–5 h
Total: 4 h 10 min

## Ingredients

- 455 g (1 lb) chicken breast
- 5 ml (1 tsp) olive oil
- 395 g (14 oz) canned diced tomatoes with chilies
- 395 g (14 oz) canned black beans, drained & rinsed
- 395 g (14 oz) canned corn, drained
- 60 ml (¼ cup) fresh cilantro, chopped
- 395 g (14 oz) can fat-free chicken broth
- 60 ml (¼ cup) green onions, chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 5 ml (1 tsp) onion powder
- 5 ml (1 tsp) cumin
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) cayenne pepper
- Salt to taste

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat pan to medium

### Directions

1. Heat oil in pan
2. Brown chicken on both sides, remove and shred
3. Place all ingredients in crockpot
4. Cook on LOW for 4-5 hours
