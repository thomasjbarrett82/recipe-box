# Sausage Gravy

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Breakfast, Quick, Favorite
- Image: /images/sausage-gravy.jpg
- Serves: 8

## Description

Creamy country sausage gravy for biscuits.

## Cook Times

Prep: 5 min
Cook: 20 min
Total: 25 min

## Ingredients

- 455 g (1 lb) ground sausage
- 600 ml (2 ½ cups) milk
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) butter
- 45 ml (3 tbsp) flour
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) cornmeal
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) black pepper
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) salt

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat saucepot over med-high

### Directions

1. Brown and crumble sausage
2. Remove with slotted spoon and set aside
3. Combine milk, flour, cornmeal, salt & pepper in bowl
4. Melt butter in pot
5. Mix gravy into pot and stir frequently while bringing to a boil
6. Simmer and return sausage to pot
7. Serve over biscuits
