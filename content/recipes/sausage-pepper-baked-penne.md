# Sausage & Pepper Baked Penne

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Pasta, Spicy
- Image: /images/sausage-pepper-baked-penne.jpg
- Emoji: 🫑
- Serves: 6

## Description

Penne baked with spicy Italian sausage, peppers, marinara, ricotta and mozzarella.

## Cook Times

Prep: 25 min
Bake: 20 min
Total: 45 min

## Ingredients

- 225 g (8 oz) cooked penne pasta (al dente)
- 10 ml (2 tsp) olive oil
- 1 red pepper, chopped
- 2 cubanelle peppers, chopped
- 1 onion, chopped
- 455 g (1 lb) spicy Italian sausage
- 240 ml (1 cup) ricotta cheese
- 360 ml (1 ½ cups) mozzarella
- 60 ml (¼ cup) Parmesan
- 60 ml (¼ cup) fresh parsley, chopped
- 480 ml (2 cups) marinara sauce

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 205 °C (400 °F)
2. Heat oil in large skillet over medium heat

### Directions

1. Cook peppers, onion, and sausage in skillet until browned
2. Remove from heat
3. Mix ricotta and half of the other cheeses and parsley in large bowl
4. Toss pasta into bowl with sausage mix and 240 ml (1 cup) sauce
5. Pour into baking dish
6. Spoon remaining sauce over top
7. Sprinkle remaining cheese and parsley over top
8. Bake uncovered for 20 minutes until bubbly
