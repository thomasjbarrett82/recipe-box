# Sausage & Pepper Stew

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Stew
- Image: /images/sausage-pepper-stew.jpg
- Emoji: 🫑
- Serves: 6

## Description

Sausage, peppers and onion stewed with tomatoes, rice and basil.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Cook: 40 min
Total: 55 min

## Ingredients

- 455 g (1 lb) ground sausage
- 3 bell peppers, diced
- 1 sweet onion, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) balsamic vinegar
- 960 ml (4 cups) chicken stock
- 1 (795 g / 28 oz) can diced tomato
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) fresh basil, chopped
- 240 ml (1 cup) uncooked rice
- Salt & pepper to taste

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat large pot over medium

### Directions

1. Brown sausage in pot
2. Add peppers, onion, and garlic
3. Cook 5 minutes until soft
4. Deglaze with chicken stock
5. Add remaining ingredients
6. Bring to a boil, then cover and simmer until rice is soft, 20 minutes
