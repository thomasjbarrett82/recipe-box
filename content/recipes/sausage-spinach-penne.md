# Sausage & Spinach Penne

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Pasta, High fiber
- Serves: 6

## Description

Penne with Italian sausage, spinach and white beans in a light broth with Parmesan.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Cook: 25 min
Total: 35 min

## Ingredients

- 455 g (1 lb) penne pasta
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) olive oil
- 1 small onion, chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 455 g (1 lb) Italian sausage
- 1 package spinach
- 1 can northern beans, drained & rinsed
- 240 ml (1 cup) chicken broth
- Parmesan, salt & pepper to taste

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat large pot with water for pasta

### Directions

1. Cook pasta according to box, drain and set aside
   - Stir in a little olive oil to keep from sticking
2. Heat olive oil in pot
3. Add onion and garlic, and cook for 5 minutes
4. Add sausage and brown
5. Add spinach, beans, and broth
6. Cook for 5 minutes
7. Return pasta to pot and toss to combine
