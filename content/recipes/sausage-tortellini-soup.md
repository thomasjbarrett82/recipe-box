# Sausage Tortellini Soup

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Soup, Dutch oven
- Serves: 8

## Description

Italian sausage and cheese tortellini in a creamy tomato broth with spinach.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Cook: 35 min
Total: 45 min

## Ingredients

- 455 g (1 lb) Italian sausage
- 2 cans diced tomatoes
- 960 ml (4 cups) chicken broth
- 225 g (8 oz) cream cheese
- 565 g (20 oz) cheese tortellini (2 pkgs of Rana)
- 720 ml (3 cups) fresh spinach

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat large pot or Dutch oven over med-high heat

### Directions

1. Crumble and brown sausage in pot
2. Add tomatoes and broth
3. Heat until starting to boil
4. Stir in cream cheese until incorporated and smooth
5. Add tortellini and spinach
6. Turn to low and cover
7. Cook for 20-30 minutes before serving
