# Sausage Zucchini Egg Bake

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Breakfast
- Source: My Crazy Good Life
- Image: /images/sausage-zucchini-egg-bake.jpg
- Emoji: 🥒
- Serves: 8

## Description

Breakfast bake of sausage, zucchini, peppers, onions, mushrooms, eggs and Cheddar.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Bake: 30–40 min
Total: 1 h

## Ingredients

- 455 g (1 lb) ground sausage
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) butter
- 60 ml (1/4 cup) water
- 225 g (8 oz) mushroom, cleaned and sliced
- 1 bag frozen onions & bell peppers, thawed and drained
- 720 ml (3 cups) zucchini, shredded and drained
- 8 eggs, beaten
- 240 ml (1 cup) Cheddar, shredded

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)
2. Heat large pan over medium

### Directions

1. Brown and crumble sausage in pan, remove and set aside
2. Melt butter in pan and add water
3. Add mushrooms and saute until browned
4. Add onions, peppers, zucchini and cook for 5 minutes
5. Stir in sausage and eggs
6. Sprinkle Cheddar over the top
7. Move to oven and bake 30-40 minutes until set and browned on top
8. Season and serve
