# Sauteed Cabbage

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Side, Spicy, Vegetarian, Vegan, High fiber
- Source: @whatinthechef on Instagram
- Image: /images/sauteed-cabbage.jpg
- Serves: 4

## Description

Cabbage sautéed with onion, garlic, green chiles and garam masala, with optional spinach. Serve with naan.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Cook: 25 min
Total: 35 min

## Ingredients

- EVOO
- 1 onion, sliced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 head cabbage, thinly sliced
- OPTIONAL: 455 g (1 lb) fresh spinach
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) kosher salt
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) garam masala
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) green chilis
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) water
- Naan, pita, or rice for a base

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat EVOO in large pan over med heat

### Directions

1. Add onion, garlic and cook 3-5 mins until soft
2. Add cabbage and seasonings to pan
3. Stir until starting to soften and wilt
4. Add water, turn to low, and cover pan
5. Cook for 15-20 minutes
6. Top with any fresh herbs and serve over the base
