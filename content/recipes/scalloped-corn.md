# Scalloped Corn

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Side, Vegetarian, Holiday, Minnesota
- Serves: 8

## Description

Creamed corn baked into a custardy casserole with saltines and a corn flake topping.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Bake: 40–50 min
Total: 1 h 5 min

## Ingredients

- 2 cans creamed corn
- ¾ can milk
- 60 ml (4 tbsp) butter, melted
- 2 eggs, beaten
- Salt & pepper
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) sugar
- 1 bag saltine crackers, crushed
- 240 ml (1 cup) corn flakes, crushed

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)
2. Lightly grease a casserole dish

### Directions

1. Mix corn, milk, butter, eggs, s&p, sugar, & crackers in casserole dish
2. Top with corn flakes
3. Bake for 40-50 minutes
