# Scalloped Potatoes

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Side, Holiday
- Image: /images/scalloped-potatoes.jpg
- Serves: 8

## Description

Thinly sliced potatoes baked in a creamy mushroom sauce, with optional ham.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Bake: 45–60 min
Total: 1 h 20 min

## Ingredients

- 60 ml (4 tbsp) butter, melted
- 1/2 onion, minced
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) flour
- 480 ml (2 cups) milk
- 5 ml (1 tsp) salt
- 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) pepper
- 1 can cream of mushroom soup
- 6 large russet potatoes, sliced thin (1.4 L / 6 cups)
- Optional: 360 ml (1.5 cups) cooked ham, cubed

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)
2. Heat small saucepan over med-low

### Directions

1. Melt butter in saucepan
2. Cook onion in butter for 2-3 minutes until soft
3. Stir in flour, milk, salt, pepper
4. Cook until smooth and bubbly, stirring often
5. Remove from heat and stir in cream soup
6. Layer potatoes in greased casserole dish
7. Pour sauce over potatoes and shake gently to mix it evenly
8. Place in oven and cook for 45-60 minutes, until potatoes are done
