# Scotch Eggs

- Serves: 6

## Description

Soft-boiled eggs wrapped in seasoned sausage, breaded and air-fried.

## Cook Times

Prep: 30 min
Air fry: 12–15 min
Total: 45 min

## Ingredients

- 6 + 2 whole eggs
- 455 g (1 lb) ground sausage
- 1 + 5 ml (1 tsp) Italian seasoning
- 1 + 5 ml (1 tsp) garlic powder
- 1 + 5 ml (1 tsp) kosher salt
- 1 + 5 ml (1 tsp) black pepper
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) flour
- 480 ml (2 cups) breadcrumbs or Panko

## Method

### Prep

1. Soft boil 6 eggs, cool and peel
2. Heat air fryer to 220 °C (425 °F)
3. Divide sausage into 6 equal servings and flatten

### Directions

1. Mix flour with 1 serving of spices in a bowl
2. Mix breadcrumbs with remaining spices in another bowl
3. Beat 2 eggs in another bowl
4. Mold 1 serving sausage around 1 egg, repeat for the rest
5. Dip covered eggs in flour, then eggs, then breadcrumbs
6. Cook in air fryer for 12-15 minutes until golden brown on outside
