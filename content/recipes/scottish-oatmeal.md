# Scottish Oatmeal

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Breakfast, Vegetarian
- Image: /images/scottish-oatmeal.jpg
- Serves: 4

## Description

Creamy steel-cut oats toasted in butter, then simmered in water and whole milk.

## Cook Times

Prep: 5 min
Cook: 40–55 min
Total: 1 h

## Ingredients

- 15 ml (1 tbsp) butter
- 240 ml (1 cup) steel cut oats
- 720 ml (3 cups) boiling water
- 240 ml (1 cup) whole milk
- 5 ml (1 tsp) kosher salt

## Method

1. Melt the butter in a heavy pot
2. Toast the oats in the butter
3. Add the boiling water to the oats
4. Turn to a simmer and cover
5. Let cook for 30-45 minutes
6. Add milk and salt to the pot
7. Stir gently and let cook for 10 minutes before serving
