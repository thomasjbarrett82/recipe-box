# Seven Layer Dip

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Appetizer, Vegetarian
- Image: /images/seven-layer-dip.jpg
- Emoji: 🥑
- Serves: 10

## Description

Layers of seasoned refried beans, sour cream, salsa, lettuce, cheese, green onions and olives.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Chill: 2–8 h
Total: 2 h 20 min

## Ingredients

- 455 g (16 oz) can refried beans
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) taco seasoning mix
- 240 ml (1 cup) sour cream
- 240 ml (1 cup) chunky salsa
- 240 ml (1 cup) shredded lettuce
- 240 ml (1 cup) shredded Cheddar or Mexican blend cheese
- 4 green onions, chopped
- Black olives, sliced
- Tortilla chips

## Method

1. Mix the beans & taco seasoning in a small bowl
2. Layer the ingredients in a shallow pan
3. Refrigerate for 2-8 hours ahead of time, if necessary
4. Serve with chips
