# Shepherd's Pie

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main
- Image: /images/shepherds-pie.jpg
- Emoji: 🐑
- Serves: 6

## Description

Lamb and beef in a rich gravy with carrots, topped with mashed potatoes and baked.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Cook: 40 min
Cool: 15 min
Total: 1 h 15 min

## Ingredients

- Instant mashed potatoes
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) + 2.5 ml (½ tsp) black pepper
- 1 egg
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) oil
- 1 small onion, chopped
- 2 carrots, peeled and chopped
- 2 cloves garlic
- 455 g (1 lb) ground lamb
- 455 g (1 lb) ground beef
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) kosher salt
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) flour
- 1 (115 g / 4 oz) can tomato paste
- 240 ml (1 cup) chicken stock
- 5 ml (1 tsp) Worcestershire
- 10 ml (2 tsp) fresh rosemary, chopped
- 5 ml (1 tsp) fresh thyme, chopped
- 1 bag frozen corn and peas

## Method

### Prep

1. Preheat the oven to 205 °C (400 °F)

### Directions

1. Prepare the mashed potatoes for 4 servings (box instructions)
2. Add 2.5 ml (½ tsp) pepper and egg yolk and mix together
3. Heat the oil in a large heavy pan over med-high heat
4. Sauté the onion and carrots until soft
5. Add garlic, lamb, beef, salt, and pepper
6. Cooked until browned
7. Sprinkle meat with flour and continue to cook for another minute
8. Add the tomato paste, stock, Worcestershire, and herbs
9. Bring to a boil, then simmer for 10-12 minutes until thickened
10. Add meat mix and frozen veggies to a large baking dish
11. Cover completely with mashed potatoes
12. Bake for 25 minutes until the potatoes start to brown
13. Let cool for 15 minutes before serving
