# Shrimp Boil

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Spicy, High fiber
- Image: /images/shrimp-boil.jpg
- Emoji: 🦐
- Serves: 8

## Description

Shrimp, sausage, corn, potatoes, mushrooms and green beans boiled in an Old Bay, citrus and garlic broth.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Cook: 45 min
Total: 1 h 5 min

## Ingredients

- 1 head garlic, unpeeled
- 4 bay leaves
- 60 ml (4 tbsp) Old Bay
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) kosher salt
- 5 ml (1 tsp) black pepper
- 2 oranges, quartered
- 2 lemons, quartered
- 1.4 kg (3 lb) red potatoes, washed
- 6 ears of corn, broken in half
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 455 g (1 lb) whole mushrooms, washed
- 225 g (½ lb) fresh green beans, trimmed
- 905 g (2 lb) smoked or Andouille sausage, sliced
- 905 g (2 lb) uncooked tail-on shrimp (Can also use whole crabs or crawfish)

## Method

### Prep

1. Fill a very large pot 1/3 full of water over high heat

### Directions

1. Add garlic, bay leaves, seasonings, oranges, lemons, and potatoes to pot
2. Bring to boil and simmer covered for 20 minutes
3. Add corn, onions, mushrooms, green beans
4. Cook for another 15 minutes
5. Add sausage and shrimp
6. Cook for 5-10 minutes until shrimp is cooked (don’t go too long)
7. Drain well
8. Spread parchment paper in large sheet pan and pour everything into pan for serving
