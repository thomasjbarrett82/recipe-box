# Shrimp Fra Diavolo

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Quick, Spicy
- Image: /images/shrimp-fra-diavolo.jpg
- Emoji: 🍤
- Serves: 4

## Description

Shrimp in a spicy tomato and white wine sauce with garlic and red pepper flakes.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Cook: 20 min
Total: 30 min

## Ingredients

- 455 g (1 lb) large shrimp, peeled & deveined
- 5 ml (1 tsp) kosher salt
- 5 ml (1 tsp) red pepper flakes
- 45 + 30 ml (3 + 2 tbsp) olive oil
- 1 onion, sliced
- 1 can diced tomato
- 240 ml (1 cup) dry white wine
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1.25 ml (¼ tsp) oregano
- 45 ml (3 tbsp) fresh parsley, chopped
- 45 ml (3 tbsp) fresh basil, chopped

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat 45 ml (3 tbsp) olive oil in large heavy skillet over med-high

### Directions

1. Toss shrimp in bowl with salt and pepper flakes
2. Add shrimp to skillet and sauté for 1 minute, toss and finish cooking for 2 minutes longer
3. Transfer shrimp to plate and set aside
4. Add additional oil to skillet and heat
5. Add onion to skillet and cook until translucent
6. Add tomatoes, wine, garlic, oregano
7. Simmer 10 minutes until slightly thickened
8. Return shrimp to skillet and cook for another minute or two
9. Stir in parsley and basil
10. Add more salt if needed, and serve
