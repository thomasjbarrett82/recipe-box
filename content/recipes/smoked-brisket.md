# Smoked Brisket

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Grill, Smoker
- Source: Serious Eats
- Serves: 22

## Description

Whole packer brisket, dry-brined, smoked low and slow, wrapped and rested until tender.

## Cook Times

Prep: 30 min
Dry brine: 12–24 h
Smoke: 8–14 h
Total: 22 h 30 min

## Ingredients

- 4.5–5.4 kg (10–12 lb) whole brisket, trimmed
- 60 ml (1/4 cup) kosher salt
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) black pepper
- charcoal and wood chunks for smoking
- disposable aluminum pan
- 1.4 L (6 cups) water
- probe thermometer
- 240 ml (1 cup) beef stock
- parchment or butcher paper
- heavy duty aluminum foil

## Method

### Prep

1. Mix salt & pepper and coat brisket with rub
2. Refrigerate on wire rack for 12-24 hours
3. Add charcoal to grill using snake method
   - Make a larger snake for winter smoking
4. Place pan in middle and pour water into it
5. Clean & oil the grill grate

### Directions

1. Start some charcoals in chimney and start the snake
2. Position thermometer in middle of brisket
3. Add brisket to grate over pan, fat side up
4. Cover and adjust until temp is 107–135 °C (225–275 °F)
5. Smoke until brisket reaches 71–77 °C (160–170 °F)
   - 6-10 hours
   - Spritz with beef stock every hour
6. Remove brisket and wrap with single parchment and double foil
7. Add more water to pan, if needed
8. Return to grill, fat side up, and continue until 91–93 °C (195–200 °F)
   - 2-4 hours
   - Alternately, move to 107 °C (225 °F) oven to finish cooking
9. Remove and let rest, covered, for 2-3 hours
   - Can rest inside cooler to keep brisket warm
10. Transfer to carving board and slice against the grain
