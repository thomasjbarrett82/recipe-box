# Smoked Rack of Pork

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Grill, Smoker, Holiday, Favorite
- Image: /images/smoked-rack-of-pork.jpg
- Emoji: 🐖
- Serves: 1 per rib (usually 8)

## Description

A rubbed rack of pork smoked for several hours, with optional BBQ sauce under the broiler.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Rest with rub: 8 h
Smoke: 4–5 h
Total: 12 h 20 min

## Ingredients

- 1 rack of pork
- BBQ spice rub
- BBQ sauce (optional)

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat smoker to 107 °C (225 °F)
2. Optional: cover with rub and let stand for 8 hours in fridge

### Directions

1. Smother spice rub all over rack
2. Place on grill and smoke for 4-5 hours until internal temp is 60 °C (140 °F)
3. Occasionally baste with BBQ sauce in the last hour of cooking
4. Remove and let rest for at least 10 minutes
5. Optional: broil for 5 minutes to give a nice crust
6. Slice between ribs to serve
