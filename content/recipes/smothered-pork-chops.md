# Smothered Pork Chops

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main
- Serves: 7

## Description

Seasoned, pan-fried pork chops slow-cooked in an onion and mushroom gravy.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Slow cook: 3–4 h
Total: 3 h 30 min

## Ingredients

- 45 ml (3 tbsp) EVOO
- 180 ml (¾ cup) flour
- 5 ml (1 tsp) kosher salt
- 5 ml (1 tsp) black pepper
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) smoked paprika
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) garlic salt
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) onion powder
- 5 ml (1 tsp) Italian seasoning
- 1.4–1.8 kg (3–4 lb) thick cut pork chops
- 60 ml (4 tbsp) butter
- 1 onion, sliced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- Cream of mushroom soup
- 240–480 ml (1–2 cups) chicken broth

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat EVOO in large skillet over med-high heat
2. Mix flour and spices in large plastic baggie

### Directions

1. Dredge chops in flour mixture by tossing in the baggie
2. Fry in pan for ~3-4 minutes on each side
3. Remove and set aside
4. Melt butter in pan
5. Sauté onions for 5 minutes, and scraping up the bottom of the pan
6. Add garlic and cook for another minute
7. Stir in soup and broth
8. Bring to a boil to thicken a bit
9. Add chops back to pan, covering with the gravy
10. Turn heat to low
11. Cover and slow cook for 3-4 hours before serving
