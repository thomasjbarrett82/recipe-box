# Snickers Salad

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert, High fiber, Minnesota, Favorite
- Source: Instagram
- Image: /images/snickers-salad.jpg
- Serves: 4

## Description

A lighter take on the Midwestern classic: sliced apples in Greek yogurt and nut butter with caramel, chocolate chips and nuts.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Chill: 1 h
Total: 1 h 15 min

## Ingredients

- 3 thinly sliced apples
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) plain Greek yogurt
- 60 ml (1/4 cup) nut butter
- 60 ml (1/4 cup) caramel sauce
- 60 ml (1/4 cup) chocolate chips
- 60 ml (1/4 cup) chopped nuts

## Method

1. Mix everything in a large bowl
2. Chill for at least 1 hour before serving
