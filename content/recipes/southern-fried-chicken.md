# Southern Fried Chicken

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main
- Serves: 5

## Description

Buttermilk-marinated chicken in a seasoned flour and cornstarch crust, fried crisp.

## Cook Times

Prep: 30 min
Marinate: 4–12 h
Fry: 30 min
Total: 5 h

## Ingredients

- 480 ml (2 cups) buttermilk
- 1 large egg
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) baking powder
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) + 15 ml (1 tbsp) kosher salt
- 1.8 kg (4 lb) chicken, cut into pieces
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) smoked paprika
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) black pepper
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) garlic powder
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) onion powder
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) dried oregano
- 5 ml (1 tsp) cayenne pepper
- 1 packet ranch mix
- 480 ml (2 cups) flour
- 240 ml (1 cup) cornstarch
- 960 ml (4 cups) neutral frying oil

## Method

### Prep

1. Mix buttermilk, egg, baking powder, and salt in large plastic baggie
2. Marinate chicken for 4-12 hours ahead of time in fridge
3. Preheat the frying oil to 175 °C (350 °F) in a fryer pan with lid
4. Preheat the oven to 175 °C (350 °F)
5. Place a wire rack on a rimmed baking sheet to the side

### Directions

1. Whisk spices, flour, and cornstarch in large bowl
2. Add chicken pieces to the large bowl and toss until fully coated
3. Press the flour into the chicken to build a thick layer
4. Let set on a wire rack for 10 minutes before frying
5. Fry the chicken 6 minutes on one side with lid covered
6. Flip and fry the chicken for 6 more minutes
7. Transfer the chicken to the wire rack in oven
8. Bake in the oven until the chicken is fully cooked
9. For extra-crispy chicken, place in the fridge for 1-12 hours after baking
10. Re-fry with 400-degree oil until crispy
