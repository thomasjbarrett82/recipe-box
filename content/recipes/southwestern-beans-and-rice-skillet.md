# Southwestern Beans and Rice Skillet

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Dutch oven, High fiber
- Serves: 8

## Description

One-pot rice with chili beans, kidney beans, refried beans and tomatoes, with optional beef or sausage and Cheddar.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Cook: 40 min
Total: 50 min

## Ingredients

- 480 ml (2 cups) uncooked rice
- 455 g (1 lb) ground beef or sausage (optional)
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 can chili beans
- 1 can kidney beans, rinsed
- 1 can diced or stewed tomatoes
- 1 can refried beans
- 1 can chicken or beef broth
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) chili powder
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) cumin
- 5 ml (1 tsp) kosher salt
- 240 ml (1 cup) shredded Cheddar, Velveeta, or Pepperjack cheese

## Method

### Prep

1. Cook rice according to directions
2. Heat large skillet or Dutch oven over medium heat

### Directions

1. Brown the meat with the onions
2. Add the garlic
3. Stir in everything else except the cheese
4. Bring to a boil and let simmer at least 15 minutes until rice is cooked
5. Stir in the cheese and let it melt before serving
