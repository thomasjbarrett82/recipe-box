# Spaghetti Sauce (IP)

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Pasta, Instant Pot, High fiber
- Image: /images/spaghetti-sauce-ip.jpg
- Serves: 6

## Description

A quick meaty spaghetti sauce with carrots and celery, pressure-cooked for long-simmered flavor.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Cook: 45 min
Total: 1 h

## Ingredients

- 455 g (1 lb) lean ground beef
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 carrots, minced
- 2 celery, minced
- 1 onion, minced
- Splash of wine (optional)
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) EVOO
- 2 bay leaves
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) Italian seasoning
- 5 ml (1 tsp) kosher salt
- 240 ml (1 cup) chicken stock
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) soy sauce
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) A1
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) Worcestershire
- 1 (795 g / 28 oz) can crushed tomatoes
- 1 (115 g / 4 oz) can tomato paste

## Method

1. Sauté the ground beef
2. Add the garlic, carrots, celery, & onion and cook for 5 minutes
3. Deglaze the bottom with wine, if necessary
4. Add the remaining ingredients except for tomatoes & paste
5. Mix the tomatoes & paste in a bowl
6. Layer on top of the ingredients (avoids scorching issues)
7. Change the cook type to high pressure for 20 minutes
8. Quick release the pressure after finished cooking
9. Stir the ingredients together
10. If still runny, cook over Sauté heat until reduced
11. Taste and season with salt & pepper
12. Serve over cooked pasta
