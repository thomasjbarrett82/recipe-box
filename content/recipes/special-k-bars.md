# Special K Bars

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert, Minnesota
- Image: /images/special-k-bars.jpg
- Serves: 24

## Description

Peanut butter Special K bars topped with melted chocolate and butterscotch.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Set: 1 h
Total: 1 h 15 min

## Ingredients

- 240 ml (1 cup) light corn syrup
- 240 ml (1 cup) white sugar
- 1 + 120 ml (1/2 cup) peanut butter (or other nut butter)
- 1.4 L (6 cups) Special K cereal (or Rice Krispies, etc.)
- 340 g (12 oz) semi-sweet chocolate chips
- 170 g (6 oz) butterscotch chips
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) butter

## Method

1. Add corn syrup and sugar to large bowl
2. Microwave in 30 second intervals, stirring, until just boiling
3. Mix in 240 ml (1 cup) peanut butter
4. Stir in cereal until combined
5. Pour into a greased casserole dish and press flat
6. Add 120 ml (1/2 cup) peanut butter, chips, and butter to medium bowl
7. Microwave in 30 second intervals, stirring, until melted
8. Pour evenly over cereal to frost
9. Refrigerate for 30+ minutes before cutting into bars
