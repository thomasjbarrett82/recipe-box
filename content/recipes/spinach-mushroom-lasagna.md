# Spinach Mushroom Lasagna

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Pasta, Vegetarian, Favorite
- Source: From Rage To Real
- Image: /images/spinach-mushroom-lasagna.jpg
- Emoji: 🍄
- Serves: 8

## Description

Vegetarian lasagna with mushrooms, spinach, fresh herbs, ricotta and mozzarella.

## Cook Times

Prep: 45 min
Bake: 1 h
Cool: 15 min
Total: 2 h

## Ingredients

- 225 g (8 oz) mushrooms, cleaned and sliced
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) water
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) + 15 ml (1 tbsp) EVOO
- 1 large bag fresh spinach
- salt & pepper to taste
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) fresh basil, chopped
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) fresh oregano, chopped
- 4-5 cloves garlic, minced
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) balsamic vinegar
- 395 g (14 oz) can crushed tomatoes
- 395 g (14 oz) can diced tomatoes
- 455 g (16 oz) whole fat mozzarella
- 225 g (8 oz) ricotta
- 225–285 g (8–10 oz) no-bake lasagna noodles
- Fresh or dried parsley as garnish

## Method

### Prep

1. Let ricotta come to room temp for at least 30 minutes
2. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F) right before cooking
3. Heat large pot over medium-high heat

### Directions

1. Add water & mushrooms to pot
2. Let cook down until all water evaporates
3. Add 30 ml (2 tbsp) EVOO and brown for a couple of minutes
4. Add spinach and cook down until all wilted
5. Mix in seasonings, garlic, vinegar, tomatoes
6. Turn to low and cover
7. Spread 15 ml (1 tbsp) EVOO in large casserole dish
8. Layer noodles, ricotta, sauce, mozzarella in dish
9. Garnish with parsley, if desired
10. OPTIONAL: Can cover and move to fridge or freezer if making for later
11. Cover with foil and move to oven for 40 minutes
12. Remove foil and cook another 20 minutes
13. Remove from oven and let cool at least 15 minutes before serving
