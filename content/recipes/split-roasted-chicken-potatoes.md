# Split-Roasted Chicken & Potatoes

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, High fiber
- Image: /images/split-roasted-chicken-potatoes.jpg
- Serves: 4

## Description

Spatchcocked chicken roasted over potatoes, carrots and pearl onions that soak up the drippings.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Dry brine: 4–12 h
Roast: 45 min
Total: 5 h 15 min

## Ingredients

- 1.8–2.3 kg (4–5 lb) fryer chicken
- Kosher salt
- Black pepper
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) olive oil
- 3 large russet or Yukon Gold potatoes, diced 1”
- 455 g (1 lb) baby carrots or diced parsnips
- 240 ml (1 cup) pearl onions, peeled

## Method

### Prep

1. Butterfly chicken and season w/ salt & pepper
2. Let sit in fridge 4-12 hours to dry out
3. Before cooking, heat oven to 230 °C (450 °F)

### Directions

1. Pour olive oil in bottom of roasting pan and spread around
2. Loosely layer vegetables, seasoning each layer with salt & pepper
3. Place chicken on top of potatoes, cavity side down
4. Roast for 45 minutes, rotating pan once, until breast reads 77 °C (170 °F)
5. Remove and let rest for 10 minutes before serving
