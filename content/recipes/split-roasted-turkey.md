# Split Roasted Turkey

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Holiday
- Source: Epicurious
- Image: /images/split-roasted-turkey.jpg
- Serves: 17

## Description

Dry-brined turkey roasted with the legs split from the breast so everything cooks evenly, with herb butter.

## Cook Times

Prep: 30 min
Dry brine: 24–72 h
Roast: 80–90 min
Total: 26 h 15 min

## Ingredients

- 45 ml (3 tbsp) kosher salt
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) black pepper
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) brown sugar
- 5.4–6.4 kg (12–14 lb) turkey, thawed
- 115 g (1 stick) unsalted butter, room temperature
- 60 ml (1/4 cup) sage & thyme leaves, chopped
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) EVOO
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) water

## Method

1. Mix salt, pepper, sugar in small bowl
2. Spatchcock the turkey and lay on wire rack in baking sheet
3. Rub dry brine all over the turkey
4. Rest in the fridge for 24-72 hours
5. Remove from fridge and let come to room temp
6. Heat oven to 220 °C (425 °F)
7. Brush any excess salt from bird
8. Mix butter, herbs, and garlic
9. Rub the butter mixture under the skin
10. Brush EVOO over all the skin
11. Pour water into baking sheet and place in oven
12. Roast until breast reads 66 °C (150 °F) (80-90 minutes)
13. Remove and rest for 15 minutes before carving
