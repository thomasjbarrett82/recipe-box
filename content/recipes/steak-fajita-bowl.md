# Steak Fajita Bowl

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Favorite
- Source: Tom
- Image: /images/steak-fajita-bowl.jpg
- Serves: 6

## Description

Seasoned flank steak with fajita peppers and onions over rice, topped with avocado.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Cook: 25 min
Total: 40 min

## Ingredients

- 480 ml (2 cups) uncooked rice
- EVOO
- 680–905 g (1.5–2 lb) flank or sirloin steak

### Marinade

- 45 ml (3 tbsp) EVOO
- 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) garlic powder
- 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) onion powder
- 5 ml (1 tsp) salt
- 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) black pepper
- 5 ml (1 tsp) lime juice

### Fajitas

- Taco/fajita seasoning
- 1 onion, sliced
- 2-3 bell peppers, sliced
- 1 avocado, sliced
- Taco/fajita sauce
- Sour cream/queso
- Salsa

## Method

### Prep

1. Cook rice
2. Mix marinade together and cover steak with it
3. Heat 2 cast iron pans on medium and med-high

### Directions

1. Add 15 ml (1 tbsp) EVOO to medium pan and cook onions
2. Toss with some taco seasoning, remove and set aside
3. Add 15 ml (1 tbsp) EVOO to med-high pan and cook steaks (in 2 batches, if needed)
4. Flip when seared, remove and set aside when medium-rare
5. Add 15 ml (1 tbsp) EVOO to medium pan and cook bell peppers
6. Toss with some taco seasoning, remove and set aside
7. After steaks have rested, thinly slice across the grain
8. Serve steak in bowls with rice, onion, peppers, avocado, and sauces
