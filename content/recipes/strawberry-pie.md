# Strawberry Pie

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert
- Serves: 8

## Description

Fresh strawberries set in a glossy strawberry glaze in a graham cracker crust.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Macerate: 1 h
Cook: 10 min
Chill: 4–6 h
Total: 5 h 30 min

## Ingredients

- 1 graham cracker crust
- 455 g (1 lb) + 905 g (2 lb) fresh strawberries, sliced
- 240 ml (1 cup) sugar
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) water
- 10 ml (2 tsp) gelatin
- Pinch of kosher salt
- 5 ml (1 tsp) lemon juice
- Whipped cream for serving

## Method

### Prep

1. Slice the 905 g (2 lb) of strawberries
2. Stir in large bowl with sugar to macerate for 1 hour

### Directions

1. Place 455 g (1 lb) strawberries in food processor and puree
2. Transfer to 480 ml (2 cups) measuring cup
3. Add macerated liquid to make 360 ml (1 ½ cups) total
4. Drain any remaining liquid and reserve or discard
5. Place water in bowl with gelatin and let stand 5 minutes
6. Microwave in 15 second intervals until melted and hot
7. Scrape into large bowl
8. Whisk in puree mixture and salt until combined
9. Stir in macerated strawberries and lemon juice
10. Pour into crust
11. Refrigerate until set, 4-6 hours
12. Serve with whipped cream
