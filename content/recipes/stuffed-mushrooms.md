# Stuffed Mushrooms

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Appetizer, Vegetarian, Holiday
- Serves: 6

## Description

Mushroom caps stuffed with garlicky spinach, cream cheese and Parmesan.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Bake: 15 min
Total: 35 min

## Ingredients

- 15-20 medium mushrooms
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) butter
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 480 ml (2 cups) fresh spinach, chopped
- 225 g (8 oz) cream cheese, softened
- 60 ml (¼ cup) breadcrumbs
- 120 ml (½ cup) shredded Parmesan
- 5 ml (1 tsp) kosher salt
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) black pepper

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)
2. Remove stems from mushrooms and mince
3. Arrange the tops on a baking sheet

### Directions

1. Melt the butter in a large pan
2. Sauté the garlic and mushroom stems for 2 minutes
3. Add the spinach and cook until wilted
4. Add cream cheese, salt, pepper and cook until melted
5. Stir in the breadcrumbs and Parmesan and remove from heat
6. Fill each mushroom with a spoonful of mixture
7. Bake for 15 minutes until golden brown
8. Cool and serve
