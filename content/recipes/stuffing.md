# Stuffing

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Side, Holiday
- Image: /images/stuffing.jpg
- Emoji: 🍞
- Serves: 12

## Description

Thanksgiving stuffing with Italian sausage, mushrooms, apple, raisins and fresh sage.

## Cook Times

Prep: 30 min
Bake: 45–60 min
Total: 1 h 30 min

## Ingredients

- 455 g (1 lb) ground Italian sausage
- Turkey gizzard & heart, minced
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) EVOO
- 455 g (1 lb) sliced mushrooms (or fresh)
- 2 celery stalks, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 onion, chopped
- 120 ml (½ cup) raisins
- 1 small apple, diced
- ¼ fresh parsley, chopped
- 60 ml (¼ cup) fresh sage, minced (or 10 ml (2 tsp) dried sage)
- 2.9 L (12 cups) stale Italian bread, cubed
- 1 can chicken stock

### Vegetarian

- Substitute 225 g (8 oz) chopped pecans for meat
- Substitute 1 can vegetable stock for chicken stock
- Add 1 large leek, minced

## Method

### Prep

1. Place large pan over med-high heat
2. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)

### Directions

1. Brown sausage & turkey in pan
2. Transfer to casserole dish, leaving the drippings
3. Heat oil in pan with drippings
4. Add mushrooms, celery, garlic, & onion, deglazing with the veggies
5. Cook for 5 minutes until soft and starting to brown
6. Transfer to casserole dish
7. Mix remaining ingredients into casserole dish
8. Bake for 45-60 minutes, covered with foil
