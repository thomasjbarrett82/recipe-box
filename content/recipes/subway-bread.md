# Subway Bread

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Bread, Vegetarian, Vegan
- Serves: 8

## Description

Soft, quick sandwich rolls inspired by Subway's Italian bread.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Rise: 30 min
Bake: 25 min
Total: 1 h 45 min

## Ingredients

- 240 ml (1 cup) warm water
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) yeast
- 45 ml (3 tbsp) sugar
- 60 ml (4 tbsp) EVOO
- 240 ml (1 cup) + 420 ml (1 ¾ cups) A/P flour
- 10 ml (2 tsp) kosher salt
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) baking powder

## Method

### Prep

1. Add water, yeast, sugar, EVOO to mixer bowl, let sit for 5-10 minutes

### Directions

1. Add 240 ml (1 cup) flour and mix for 3 minutes
2. Add remaining flour, salt, and baking powder
3. Mix for another 3 minutes
4. Turn out onto floured surface and knead until soft and smooth
5. Roll into a ball and place in greased bowl to rise for 30 minutes
6. Turn out onto a clean surface and divide into 4 equal pieces
7. Knead each piece into a rough rectangle ~9-10 inches in length
8. Roll each piece lengthwise and pinch to close (bottom)
9. Place each loaf onto a large baking sheet with parchment paper
10. Lightly score the top of each loaf once lengthwise
11. Let sit for at least an hour to rise
12. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F) and bake for 25 minutes
13. Remove sheet and rub tops with a stick of butter
14. Let cool for at least 30 minutes before serving
