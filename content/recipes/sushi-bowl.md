# Sushi Bowl

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Quick, High fiber
- Source: Instagram
- Serves: 2

## Description

A no-roll sushi bowl of rice, smoked salmon, avocado and sesame.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Total: 10 min

## Ingredients

- 480 ml (2 cups) cooked white rice (brown for more fiber)
- 1 avocado, sliced
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) lemon juice
- 340 g (12 oz) smoked salmon
- Salt & pepper to taste
- Sesame seeds
- Optional: sushi ingredients like diced cucumber, kimchi, etc.

## Method

### Prep

1. Toss avocado with lemon juice to keep fresh

### Directions

1. Split rice into 2 bowls
2. Arrange salmon, avocado, other ingredients over rice
3. Sprinkle with S&P, sesame seeds
4. Serve with soy sauce
