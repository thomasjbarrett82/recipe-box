# Sweet Potato Chickpea Bowl

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Quick, Vegetarian, High fiber
- Source: PlantYou
- Image: /images/sweet-potato-chickpea-bowl.jpg
- Emoji: 🍠
- Serves: 2

## Description

Roasted sweet potatoes and BBQ chickpeas over coleslaw mix with a honey-lime yogurt dressing.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Roast: 20 min
Total: 30 min

## Ingredients

- 2 sweet potatoes, peeled and cubed
- 425 g (15 oz) can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) EVOO
- 5 ml (1 tsp) smoked paprika
- 5 ml (1 tsp) garlic powder
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) BBQ sauce
- 1 bag coleslaw mix
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) plain Greek yogurt
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) lime juice
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) honey (hot honey also good)
- Salt & pepper to taste

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat convection oven to 205 °C (400 °F)

### Directions

1. Toss potatoes, chickpeas, EVOO, paprika, garlic, BBQ in medium bowl
2. Spread on pan and roast for 20 mins until potatoes are softened
3. Meanwhile mix remaining ingredients in medium bowl
4. Stir in potatoes & chickpeas once finished cooking
5. Divide between 2 large bowls to serve
