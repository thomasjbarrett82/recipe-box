# Sweet Potato & Lentil Stew

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Stew, Slow cooker, Vegetarian, Vegan, High fiber
- Source: Taste of Home
- Image: /images/sweet-potato-lentil-stew.jpg
- Emoji: 🍠
- Serves: 8

## Description

Sweet potatoes, lentils and carrots slow-cooked with cumin, ginger and a little cayenne.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Slow cook: 5–6 h
Total: 5 h 15 min

## Ingredients

- 3 sweet potatoes, peeled and chunked
- 1 (455 g / 16 oz) bag lentils, rinsed
- 3 carrots, peeled and chunked
- 1 onion, chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 5 ml (1 tsp) cumin
- 5 ml (1 tsp) ginger
- 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) cayenne pepper
- 905 g (32 oz) broth
- 60 ml (1/4 cup) fresh cilantro, minced

## Method

1. Combine everything except cilantro in slow cooker
2. Cook on low for 5-6 hours until potatoes and lentils are tender
3. Stir in cilantro before serving
