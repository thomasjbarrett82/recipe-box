# Sweet Potato Pie

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert, Holiday
- Serves: 10

## Description

Silky sweet potato pie infused with vanilla bean and nutmeg, in a blind-baked butter crust.

## Cook Times

Prep: 30 min
Cook: 1 h 40 min
Cool: 20 min
Total: 2 h 30 min

## Ingredients

- 455 g (16 oz) sweet potatoes, peeled and diced
- 1 vanilla bean, split & scraped (seeds reserved for whipped cream topping
- 2 or 3 cinnamon sticks
- 1 whole nutmeg
- 720 ml (3 cups) whole milk
- 170 g (6 oz) heavy cream
- 200 g (7 oz) white sugar
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) ground cinnamon
- 5 ml (1 tsp) fresh grated nutmeg
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) kosher salt
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) vanilla extract
- 3 large eggs
- Old Fashioned Pie Dough (prepared for blind-baked pie)
- Brown Sugar Whipped Cream

## Method

### Prep

1. Preheat oven to 175 °C (350 °F) and put the rack on the low-middle position

### Directions

1. In a 2.8 L (3 quarts) saucier, combine potatoes, vanilla pod, cinnamon sticks, whole nutmeg, milk, cream, and sugar (measure the weight of everything)
2. Bring to a boil, then adjust to med-low and maintain a simmer
3. Scrape frequently to prevent milk solids from building up
4. Simmer until potatoes are fall-apart and reduced, approximately 1 hour
5. Saucier should weigh 540 g (19 oz) less than at the beginning
6. Remove vanilla bean & spices from saucier and puree
7. Whisk in ground cinnamon, grated nutmeg, salt, vanilla, & eggs
8. Bake 40 minutes until gently set and internal temp is 91 °C (195 °F)
9. Cool at least 20 minutes before cutting
10. Serve with whipped cream
