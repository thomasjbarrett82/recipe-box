# Sweet & Sour Brussels Sprouts

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Side, Quick, Holiday, Favorite
- Image: /images/sweet-sour-brussels-sprouts.jpg
- Serves: 8

## Description

Brussels sprouts simmered with bacon, onion and a sweet-and-sour cider vinegar sauce.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Cook: 20 min
Total: 30 min

## Ingredients

- 225 g (½ lb) bacon, sliced
- 905 g (32 oz) frozen Brussels sprouts, thawed
- ½ onion, minced
- 80 ml (1/3 cup) cider vinegar
- 45 ml (3 tbsp) brown sugar
- 7.5 ml (1 ½ tsp) salt
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) dry mustard
- 1.25 ml (¼ tsp) pepper

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat large, heavy pan over medium

### Directions

1. Cook bacon in pan, then remove with slotted spoon and set aside
2. In the drippings, cook the sprouts and onion until crisp-tender
3. Add remaining ingredients and simmer for 10 minutes until tender
4. Return bacon to pan and stir
