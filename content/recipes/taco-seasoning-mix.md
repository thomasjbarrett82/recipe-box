# Taco Seasoning Mix

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Quick, Vegetarian, Vegan
- Serves: 5

## Description

Homemade taco seasoning with chili powder, cumin and a little cayenne.

## Cook Times

Prep: 5 min
Total: 5 min

## Ingredients

- 15 ml (1 tbsp) chili powder
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) cumin
- 5 ml (1 tsp) garlic salt
- 5 ml (1 tsp) onion powder
- 5 ml (1 tsp) oregano
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) cayenne pepper
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) paprika

## Method

1. Mix all ingredients in a bowl
2. Add to taco meat after browned
