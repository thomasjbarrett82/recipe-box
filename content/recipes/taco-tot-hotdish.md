# Taco-tot Hotdish

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Minnesota
- Image: /images/taco-tot-hotdish.jpg
- Emoji: 🌮
- Serves: 8

## Description

Tater tot hotdish goes Tex-Mex: taco-seasoned beef, corn and nacho cheese soup under crispy tots.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Bake: 45 min
Total: 1 h

## Ingredients

- 455 g (1 lb) ground beef
- 1 package taco seasoning
- 1 can sweet corn, drained
- 1 can nacho cheese soup
- Tater tots
- OPTIONAL: salsa & sour cream

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 190 °C (375 °F)
2. Heat pan over med-high

### Directions

1. Brown beef in pan and drain the grease
2. Stir in seasoning and cook for 3 minutes
3. Spoon beef into casserole dish
4. Layer corn and then soup over the top
5. Cover with tater-tots
6. Bake for 45 minutes until lightly browned on top
