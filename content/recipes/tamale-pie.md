# Tamale Pie

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main
- Serves: 8

## Description

Taco-seasoned beef, beans and corn baked under a cheesy cornbread topping with green chiles.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Bake: 30 min
Total: 50 min

## Ingredients

- 1 package corn bread mix + eggs & milk
- 240 ml (1 cup) Cheddar, shredded
- 1 small can green chili’s
- 905 g (2 lb) ground or diced beef
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 packages taco seasoning
- 1 can black beans, rinsed
- 1 can corn, drained

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat large cast iron skillet over med-high heat
2. Heat oven to 205 °C (400 °F)
3. Prepare corn bread according to instructions
4. Add Cheddar and chili’s to the corn bread mixture

### Directions

1. Cook meat in skillet
2. Add onion and cook for 2-3 minutes
3. Mix in seasoning, beans, and corn
4. Spread corn bread mix over the top
5. Place in oven and bake until corn bread is done
6. Serve with salsa and sour cream
