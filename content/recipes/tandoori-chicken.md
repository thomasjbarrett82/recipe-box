# Tandoori Chicken

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main
- Serves: 4

## Description

Brined chicken quarters in a tandoori-style spice rub, roasted or air-fried.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Brine: 5–6 h
Marinate: 2–3 h
Cook: 25–30 min
Total: 7 h 45 min

## Ingredients

- 4 chicken quarters, scored to increase surface area

### Brine (optional)

- 180 ml (¾ cup) vinegar
- 480 ml (2 cups) water
- 60 ml (¼ cup) kosher salt

### Marinade

- 30 ml (2 tbsp) curry
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) smoked paprika
- 5 ml (1 tsp) onion powder
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) garlic
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) ginger
- 5 ml (1 tsp) cumin
- 5 ml (1 tsp) cinnamon
- 5 ml (1 tsp) black pepper
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) EVOO

## Method

### Prep

1. Brine chicken for 5-6 hours (optional)
2. Marinate chicken for 2-3 hours

### Directions

1. Cook in air fryer/roaster @ 205 °C (400 °F) for 15 minutes
2. Flip and cook another 10-15 minutes
