# Tater-Tot Hotdish

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Minnesota, Favorite
- Image: /images/tater-tot-hotdish.jpg
- Serves: 8

## Description

Minnesota's favorite: beef, peas and carrots in a creamy mushroom sauce under a layer of crisp tater tots.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Bake: 45 min
Rest: 5 min
Total: 1 h 5 min

## Ingredients

- 455 g (1 lb) ground beef
- 1 package dry onion soup mix
- 1 can cream of mushroom soup
- 1 bag frozen peas & carrots
- 225 g (8 oz) sour cream
- 240 ml (1 cup) milk
- 240 ml (1 cup) shredded cheddar (OPTIONAL)
- Tater-tots

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat large pan over med heat

### Directions

1. Brown beef in pan, drain and return
2. Turn to low
3. Stir in soups and frozen veggies
4. Cook for 5 minutes
5. Stir in sour cream
6. Fold entire mixture into 9x13 casserole dish
7. Cover with a layer of tater-tots
8. Bake for 45 minutes
9. Sprinkle cheddar over top after 30 minutes
10. Remove from oven and let rest for 5 minutes before serving
