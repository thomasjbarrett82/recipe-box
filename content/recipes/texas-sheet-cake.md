# Texas Sheet Cake

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert
- Source: B. Dylan Hollis
- Serves: 24

## Description

Thin, fudgy chocolate sheet cake with a pecan chocolate icing poured on warm.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Bake: 20–25 min
Total: 45 min

## Ingredients

- 480 ml (2 cups) flour
- 480 ml (2 cups) sugar
- 240 ml (1 cup) water
- 240 ml (1 cup) + 120 ml (1/2 cup) butter
- 60 ml (1/4 cup) + 60 ml (1/4 cup) cocoa powder
- 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) kosher salt
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) buttermilk
- 5 ml (1 tsp) + 5 ml (1 tsp) vanilla extract
- 5 ml (1 tsp) baking soda
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 80 ml (1/3 cup) whole milk
- 600 ml (2 1/2 cups) powdered sugar
- 240 ml (1 cup) pecans, chopped

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 165 °C (325 °F)

### Directions

1. Cake
   - Add flour, sugar to bowl
   - Add water, 240 ml (1 cup) butter, 60 ml (1/4 cup) cocoa, salt to saucepan
   - Bring to a boil and mix into flour mixture until smooth
   - Stir in buttermilk, 5 ml (1 tsp) vanilla, baking soda, eggs
   - Pour into greased half sheet pan
   - Bake for 20-25 minutes
   - Remove and let cool
2. Frosting
   - Add 120 ml (1/2 cup) butter, 60 ml (1/4 cup) cocoa, milk to saucepan
   - Bring to a brief boil
   - Remove and whisk in powdered sugar
   - Stir in 5 ml (1 tsp) vanilla and pecans'
   - Spread over the cooled cake and let cool before serving
