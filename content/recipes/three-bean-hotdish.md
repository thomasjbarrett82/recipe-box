# Three Bean Hotdish

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Side, Slow cooker, High fiber, Minnesota
- Serves: 10

## Description

Baked beans, kidney and butter beans with ground beef and bacon in a sweet-tangy sauce. Can be made vegetarian.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Slow cook: 4–6 h
Total: 4 h 20 min

## Ingredients

- 455 g (1 lb) ground beef (substitute mushrooms for vegetarian)
- ½ yellow onion, chopped
- 225 g (½ lb) bacon, chopped (omit for vegetarian)
- 1 large can baked beans (optionally vegetarian)
- 1 can kidney beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 can butter beans, drained and rinsed
- 120 ml (½ cup) ketchup
- 120 ml (½ cup) firmly packed brown sugar
- 45 ml (3 tbsp) vinegar
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) Liquid Smoke
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) mustard
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) black pepper

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat pan or skillet over med-high

### Directions

1. Place ground beef and onions in pan
2. Cook until browned, then drain and remove
3. Mix with remaining ingredients in crockpot
4. Cook on LOW for 4-6 hours
   - Or bake at 175 °C (350 °F) for 45 minutes
