# Tomato Sauce

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Pasta, Vegetarian
- Serves: 12

## Description

Fresh tomatoes, peppers, onion and garlic slow-roasted with balsamic and honey, then blended into sauce.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Roast: 3–4 h
Total: 3 h 20 min

## Ingredients

- 905 g–1.4 kg (2–3 lb) fresh tomatoes
- 2-4 red/yellow/orange bell peppers, deseeded
- 1 large sweet onion, peeled
- 4-6 cloves garlic, peeled
- 45–60 ml (3–4 tbsp) olive oil
- 15–30 ml (1–2 tbsp) balsamic vinegar
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) honey
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) kosher salt

## Method

### Prep

1. Cut the tomatoes & peppers in half
2. Quarter the onion and keep pieces together
3. Preheat oven to 120 °C (250 °F)

### Directions

1. Arrange the vegetables in a large casserole dish (2-3 inches thick)
2. Drizzle the liquids over everything
3. Sprinkle salt over everything
4. Place casserole in oven for 3-4 hours
5. Remove and add fresh basil & oregano
6. Use an immersion blender or food processor to create the sauce
