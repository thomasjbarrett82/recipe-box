# Tuna Casserole

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Pasta, Minnesota, Favorite
- Image: /images/tuna-casserole.jpg
- Emoji: 🐟
- Serves: 8

## Description

Classic tuna noodle casserole with peas and Cheddar under crushed potato chips.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Bake: 30 min
Cool: 5 min
Total: 55 min

## Ingredients

- 340 g (12 oz) egg noodles
- 1 small yellow onion, minced
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) butter
- 340 g (12 oz) can solid white tuna in water, drained
- 2 cans cream of mushroom soup
- 80 ml (1/3 cup) mayonnaise
- 80 ml (1/3 cup) sour cream
- 80 ml (1/3 cup) milk
- 5 ml (1 tsp) salt
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) black pepper
- 240 ml (1 cup) Cheddar cheese, shredded
- 1 (395 g / 14 oz) can peas, drained
- 240 ml (1 cup) crushed potato chips

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)
2. Grease 9x13 casserole dish
3. Prepare noodles to al dente

### Directions

1. Melt butter in skillet
2. Sauté onions until tender and translucent
3. Remove from heat
4. Mix onion, tuna, soup, mayo, sour cream, milk, salt & pepper, Cheddar, peas in large bowl
5. Add to casserole dish
6. Stir in noodles
7. Top with chips
8. Bake for 30 minutes uncovered until bubbly and starting to brown on top
9. Remove and let cool at least 5 minutes before serving
