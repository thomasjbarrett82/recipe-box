# Tuna Melt

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Quick
- Image: /images/tuna-melt.jpg
- Serves: 2

## Description

Griddled tuna salad sandwiches with melted American or Cheddar.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Cook: 10 min
Total: 20 min

## Ingredients

- 140 g (5 oz) canned tuna, drained well
- 120 ml (½ cup) mayo
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) Panko or bread crumbs
- Salt & pepper
- Butter
- 4 slices bread
- 4 slices American or Cheddar cheese

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat large heavy pan over medium-low heat

### Directions

1. Combine tuna, mayo, & panko in small mixing bowl
2. Mash vigorously until almost no flakes of tuna remain
3. Season with salt & pepper
4. Melt butter in the pan
5. Place half of bread and cheese
6. Spoon tuna mixture over the two sandwich halves
7. Cover with remaining cheese and bread
8. Cook for ~3 minutes until golden brown
9. Remove and melt a little more butter before flipping
10. Cook other side to golden brown
11. If not melted enough, bake or microwave until happy
