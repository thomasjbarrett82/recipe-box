# Tuna Steaks

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main
- Serves: 4

## Description

Tuna steaks marinated in garlic, ginger and soy, seared rare and finished with cilantro.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Marinate: 1–2 h
Sear: 5 min
Total: 1 h 15 min

## Ingredients

- 30 ml (2 tbsp) olive oil
- 905 g (32 oz) of tuna steaks

### MARINADE

- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) ginger
- 60 ml (¼ cup) soy sauce
- Dash of lemon juice
- 180 ml (¾ cup) olive oil
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) red pepper flakes
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) salt
- 1.25 ml (¼ tsp) pepper
- 45 ml (3 tbsp) chopped cilantro

## Method

### Prep

1. Mix marinade and let steaks sit for 1-2 hours before cooking
2. Before cooking, heat heavy skillet over med-high heat

### Directions

1. Place 30 ml (2 tbsp) of oil in hot skillet
2. Cook steaks for 2 ½ minutes on one side, 2 minutes on the other side
3. Center of steaks will still be pink
