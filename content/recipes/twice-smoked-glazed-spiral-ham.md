# Twice Smoked Glazed Spiral Ham

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Grill, Smoker, Holiday
- Image: /images/twice-smoked-glazed-spiral-ham.jpg
- Emoji: 🍖
- Serves: About 27

## Description

A spiral ham smoked again with a brown sugar, honey and Dijon glaze.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Smoke: 1 h 30 min–2 h
Total: 2 h

## Ingredients

- 3.6 kg (8 lb) spiral ham
- 240 ml (1 cup) chicken stock

### GLAZE

- 240 ml (1 cup) brown sugar
- 60 ml (¼ cup) honey
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) Dijon mustard
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) Worcestershire sauce
- 5 ml (1 tsp) cinnamon
- 5 ml (1 tsp) ginger
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) BBQ rub mix

## Method

### Prep

1. Prepare smoker for 107 °C (225 °F)

### Directions

1. Smoke ham, cut side down on grill grates, for 1.5-2 hours
2. Transfer ham to large cast iron skillet
3. Pour stock over ham and cover tightly with aluminum foil
4. Continue cooking at 150 °C (300 °F) for 2 more hours
   - Transfer to oven if not possible in the smoker
5. Mix glaze ingredients in small pot over low heat
6. Stir constantly until it almost comes to a boil
7. Uncover the ham and set it on its side to “fan” the spirals
8. Brush the glaze liberally over the ham
9. Place under a broiler for 2-3 minutes until glaze starts to caramelize
10. Serve with drippings on the side
