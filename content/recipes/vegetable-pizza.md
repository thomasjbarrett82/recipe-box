# Vegetable Pizza

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Appetizer, Vegetarian, Minnesota
- Image: /images/vegetable-pizza.jpg
- Serves: 12

## Description

Party favorite: a crescent roll crust spread with ranch-dill cream and topped with chopped fresh vegetables.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Bake: 10 min
Chill: 1 h
Total: 1 h 30 min

## Ingredients

- 2 packages crescent rolls
- 455 g (16 oz) sour cream
- 5 ml (1 tsp) dill weed
- 1 pkg. ranch dressing mix
- 720–960 ml (3–4 cups) mixed fresh veggies, chopped/sliced/grated

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)

### Directions

1. Place roll dough in cookie sheet and bake for 10 minutes
2. Remove and let cool
3. Mix sour cream, dill, and ranch dressing
4. Spread over crust
5. Top with mixed veggies
