# Wacky Cake (Vegan)

- Emoji: 🎂
- Serves: 9

## Description

Depression-era chocolate cake with no eggs, butter or milk, mixed right in the pan.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Bake: 30–35 min
Total: 45 min

## Ingredients

- 360 ml (1 1/2 cups) flour
- 5 ml (1 tsp) baking soda
- 240 ml (1 cup) sugar
- 80 ml (1/3 cup) cocoa powder
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) vinegar
- 80 ml (1/3 cup) vegetable oil
- 5 ml (1 tsp) vanilla extract
- 240 ml (1 cup) water

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)

### Directions

1. Mix flour, baking soda, sugar, & cocoa in ungreased 8x8 baking dish
2. Make a well in the middle
3. Add vinegar, oil, vanilla, & water to well and mix together
4. Bake 30-35 minutes
5. Let cool completely before frosting and serving
