# Waffles (Paleo)

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Breakfast, Quick, Vegetarian, Gluten free, Dairy free
- Image: /images/waffles-paleo.jpg
- Serves: 2

## Description

Grain-free waffles made with almond and coconut flour, banana and eggs.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Cook: 15 min
Total: 25 min

## Ingredients

- 2 eggs
- 1/2 banana, smashed
- 5 ml (1 tsp) maple syrup
- 1.25 ml (1/4 tsp) vanilla
- 2.5 ml (1/2 tsp) baking powder
- 60 ml (1/4 cup) almond flour
- 22.5 ml (1 1/2 tbsp) coconut flour
- Pinch of salt

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat waffle iron

### Directions

1. Mix dry ingredients together
2. Mix wet ingredients in another bowl
3. Stir wet into dry until just incorporated
4. Lightly grease waffle iron before adding batter
5. Drop into waffle iron by 60 ml (¼ cup) scoops
6. Cook until golden brown
7. Serve with any sweet or savory waffle toppings
