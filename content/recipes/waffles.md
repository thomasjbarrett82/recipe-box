# Waffles

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Breakfast, Quick, Vegetarian
- Image: /images/waffles.jpg
- Emoji: 🧇
- Serves: 4

## Description

Simple homemade waffles, with an optional scoop of vanilla protein powder.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Cook: 20 min
Total: 30 min

## Ingredients

- 240 ml (1 cup) flour
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) sugar
- 10 ml (2 tsp) baking powder
- 1.25 ml (1/4 tsp) salt
- 1 egg
- 240 ml (1 cup) milk
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) oil or melted butter
- 1.25 ml (1/4 tsp) vanilla
- Optional: 1 scoop vanilla protein powder

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat waffle iron

### Directions

1. Mix dry ingredients together
2. Mix wet ingredients in another bowl
3. Stir wet into dry until just incorporated
4. Lightly grease waffle iron before adding batter
5. Drop into waffle iron by 60 ml (¼ cup) scoops
6. Cook until golden brown
7. Serve with any sweet or savory waffle toppings
