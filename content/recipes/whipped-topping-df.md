# Whipped Topping (Dairy Free)

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert, Vegetarian, Vegan, Dairy free
- Serves: 10

## Description

Dairy-free whipped topping made from chilled coconut cream.

## Cook Times

Prep: 10 min
Chill can: overnight
Total: 10 min

## Ingredients

- 1 (425 g / 15 oz) can coconut milk
- 60 ml (¼ cup) powdered sugar
- 5 ml (1 tsp) vanilla extract

## Method

### Prep

1. Place coconut milk in freezer overnight.

### Directions

1. Open coconut milk and remove solids, discard the liquid.
2. Add to large bowl.
3. Add sugar and vanilla.
4. Mix on high for 5-6 minutes until light and creamy.
