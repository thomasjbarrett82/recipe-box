# White Chicken Chili

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Stew, Spicy, Dutch oven, High fiber
- Serves: 10

## Description

White bean chicken chili made from dried beans and roasted poblano, Anaheim and jalapeño chiles, finished with pepper jack.

## Cook Times

Prep: 30 min
Brine beans: 12–24 h
Cook: 2 h 30 min
Total: 15 h

## Ingredients

- 455 g (1 lb) dried white beans
- Kosher salt
- 2 fresh poblano chilis
- 4 fresh Anaheim or Hatch chilis
- 2 fresh jalapeno chilis
- 1 onion, halved
- 8 cloves garlic
- 1 whole pickled jalapeno
- 45 ml (3 tbsp) vegetable oil
- 950 ml (1 quart) chicken stock
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) ground cumin
- 5 ml (1 tsp) ground coriander seed
- 4 chicken breasts
- 455 g (1 lb) pepper Jack, shredded
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) lime juice
- ½ package cream cheese
- 240 ml (1 cup) fresh cilantro
- 4-6 green onions or scallions, sliced

## Method

### Prep

1. Soak beans with 60 ml (¼ cup) salt for 12-24 hours
2. Toss chilis, onion, & garlic with 15 ml (1 tbsp) oil

### Directions

1. Broil fresh chilis, onion, & garlic until blackened, and rest for 5 minutes
2. Remove skin and seeds from peppers and throw away
3. Puree chilis, onion, garlic, pickled jalapeno
4. Heat remaining oil in large Dutch oven and brown chicken breast
5. Add puree, cumin, coriander, beans, stock to pot
6. Bring to a boil and simmer until chicken is 66 °C (150 °F)
7. Remove chicken and set aside
8. Continue cooking for 1 ½ to 2 hours until beans are tender
9. Shred chicken and stir back into pot
10. Stir in half of pepper Jack, along with lime juice and cream cheese
11. Season to taste
12. Serve with remaining cheese, cilantro, green onions
