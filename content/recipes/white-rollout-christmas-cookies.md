# White Rollout Christmas Cookies

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Dessert, Holiday
- Source: Tara
- Image: /images/white-rollout-christmas-cookies.jpg
- Serves: 24

## Description

Soft sour cream sugar cookies for rolling, cutting and decorating at Christmas.

## Cook Times

Prep: 30 min
Chill: 30 min
Bake: 8–10 min
Total: 1 h 15 min

## Ingredients

- 240 ml (1 cup) sugar
- 240 ml (1 cup) butter
- 2 eggs
- 45 ml (3 tbsp) sour cream
- 5 ml (1 tsp) baking soda
- 720 ml (3 cups) flour
- 2.5 ml (½ tsp) salt
- 5 ml (1 tsp) vanilla

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat oven to 175 °C (350 °F)

### Directions

1. In medium bowl, cream sugar and butter
2. Add in eggs, sour cream, baking soda and mix well
3. Fold in flour, salt, and vanilla
4. Chill in fridge for 30 minutes
5. Roll out and cut into shapes
6. Bake until light brown
