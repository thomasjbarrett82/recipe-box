# Wild Rice & Cheese Soup

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Soup, Minnesota
- Image: /images/wild-rice-cheese-soup.jpg
- Emoji: 🌾
- Serves: 8

## Description

Rich wild rice soup with bacon, cream, cream of potato soup and Cheddar.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Cook: 1 h
Total: 1 h 15 min

## Ingredients

- 480 ml (2 cups) wild rice, cooked
- 455 g (1 lb) bacon
- 1 onion, chopped
- 950 ml (2 pints) heavy cream
- 2 cans cream of potato soup
- 480 ml (2 cups) shredded Cheddar

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat large, heavy pan over medium

### Directions

1. Cook bacon in pan, removing and crumbling after cool
2. Reserve 30 ml (2 tbsp) of fat and dispose of the rest
3. Cook onion in bacon fat for 5 minutes
4. Return bacon and stir in remaining ingredients
5. Cook for 5-10 minutes until heated through
