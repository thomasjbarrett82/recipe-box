# Wild Rice Hotdish

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Main, Minnesota
- Image: /images/wild-rice-hotdish.jpg
- Serves: 6

## Description

Wild rice baked with ground beef or chicken, celery, peppers and mushrooms in cream soups.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Soak: several hours
Bake: 1 h 30 min
Total: 1 h 50 min

## Ingredients

- 240 ml (1 cup) wild rice
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) EVOO
- 455 g (1 lb) ground beef or diced chicken
- 480 ml (2 cups) celery, diced
- 2 onions, diced
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 45 ml (3 tbsp) soy sauce
- 1 can cream of mushroom
- 1 can cream of chicken + 1 can cold water
- 240 ml (1 cup) mushrooms, diced

## Method

### Prep 1

1. Rinse rice with warm water
2. Cover rice with water and let sit for several hours

### Prep 2

1. Heat the oven to 165 °C (325 °F)
2. Grease a large casserole dish

### Directions

1. In large pan, heat EVOO until shimmering
2. Cook the meat until well browned
3. Remove and add to the casserole dish
4. Sauté the celery, onions, and pepper in the same pan
5. Move to the dish when done
6. Drain the rice and add it to the casserole dish
7. Add the remaining ingredients and mix together
8. Cover with foil
9. Bake for 1 ½ hours
