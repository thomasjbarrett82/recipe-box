# Wild Rice Soup

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Soup, Dinner, Minnesota
- Author: Claude
- Source: [claude.ai](https://claude.ai)
- Serves: 6

## Description

Creamy chicken soup with Minnesota wild rice and mushrooms. Freezes well for up to 3 months.

## Cook Times

Prep: 15 min
Cook: 45 min
Total: 60 min

## Ingredients

- 160 g wild rice, rinsed
- 1.4 L chicken stock, divided
- 55 g butter
- 150 g onion, diced
- 120 g carrot, diced
- 100 g celery, diced
- 225 g cremini mushrooms, sliced
- 35 g all-purpose flour
- 240 ml heavy cream
- 300 g cooked chicken, shredded

## Method

### Prep

1. Rinse the wild rice in a sieve under cold water.
2. Dice the onion, carrot and celery. Slice the mushrooms.
3. Shred the cooked chicken.

### Cook

1. Simmer the rice in 700 ml of the stock, covered, about 45 min, until the grains split open.
2. Meanwhile, melt the butter in a large pot over medium heat. Cook the onion, carrot, celery and mushrooms about 8 min, until soft.
3. Stir in the flour and cook 2 min. Whisk in the remaining stock and simmer about 10 min, until thickened.
4. Add the rice, chicken and cream. Heat through without boiling. Season to taste.

## Notes

Cooking time varies by brand of wild rice. Start checking at 30 min.

- The soup thickens as it sits. Thin it with a little stock when reheating.
- To freeze, leave out the cream and stir it in after thawing.
