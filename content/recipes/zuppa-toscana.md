# Zuppa Toscana

## Recipe Data

- Tags: Soup, Dutch oven, High fiber, Favorite
- Source: Tom
- Image: /images/zuppa-toscana.jpg
- Emoji: 🍲
- Serves: 7

## Description

A lighter Zuppa Toscana with sausage, potato, jicama or turnips, kale and a splash of half and half.

## Cook Times

Prep: 20 min
Cook: 45 min
Total: 1 h 5 min

## Ingredients

- Optional: 4 slices bacon OR salt pork
- 455 g (1 lb) ground sausage
- 1 onion, diced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 905 g (32 oz) chicken broth
- 1 large jicama OR 6-8 turnips, peeled and cubed
- 1 large potato, peeled and cubed
- 1 bunch kale, roughly chopped
- 240 ml (1 cup) carrots, shredded
- 240 ml (1 cup) celery, diced
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) Italian seasonings
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) apple cider vinegar
- 15 ml (1 tbsp) Worcestershire
- 240 ml (1 cup) half and half OR dairy free alternative
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) cornstarch

## Method

### Prep

1. Heat Dutch oven over medium heat

### Directions

1. If using bacon, fry in pot to render out fat
2. Add sausage, onion, garlic and brown the meat
3. Add broth, veggies, seasonings, vinegar, Worcestershire
4. Bring to a boil
5. Turn down heat to low and cover for 30-40 minutes
6. Uncover and turn heat to medium
7. Make a slurry with half and half and cornstarch
8. Add to pot and stir until thickened
9. Turn off heat and serve with Parmesan cheese
